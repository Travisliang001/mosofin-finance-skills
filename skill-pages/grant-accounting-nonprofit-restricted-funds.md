# Grant accounting: keeping the strings visible

> Each grant's restrictions, budget, allowable costs and reporting dates.

**Skill:** `grant-accounting-nonprofit-restricted-funds`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Tracks grants and restricted funds in detail: classifies each award as conditional or unconditional and by its restrictions, sets up tracking by grant, recognises revenue and releases restrictions as conditions are met, checks spending against the grant budget, period and allowable costs, and prepares the reporting calendar and proposed entries. Reads expenditures tagged to each grant through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded award letters and spending data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about grant accounting, restricted funds, allowable costs, a grant budget against actual, releasing restricted net assets, or funder reporting.

Not for:

- The nonprofit statement set as a whole (use [nonprofit-fund-accounting](nonprofit-fund-accounting.md)) or for a company's budget variances (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which organisation, which grants and which period?* | Sets the scope |
| *Can you supply the award letters: terms, restrictions, conditions, period, reporting?* | The core input |
| *For each grant: is it conditional, and what are its purpose and time restrictions?* | Decides when revenue is recognised and released |
| *What is each grant's budget by category, and what are the allowable cost rules and indirect rate?* | Needed for the compliance checks |
| *How do the dimensions in the books map to grants, and when are funder reports due?* | This is how spending is read by grant |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Award letters** | Required | PDF |
| **Spending and receipts by grant** | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Grant budgets** by category | Required for budget checks | XLSX |
| Allowable cost rules and indirect rate | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces grant schedules, proposed entries and a reporting calendar, and posts nothing.

- Grant Register
- Classification
- Spend vs. Budget
- Release from Restriction
- Restricted Net Assets Roll-Forward
- Compliance Exceptions
- JE Templates and Coding Rules
- Audit-Ready Evidence Checklist
- Reporting Calendar
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never decides that a grant is conditional or unconditional.** It asks whether there is a barrier and a right of return.
- **Never rules a cost allowable.** It compares spending with the rules supplied and reports exceptions.
- **Never releases more than the award.**
