# Debt and covenants: what you owe, and how much room is left

> Debt schedule and covenants on the agreement's own definitions.

**Skill:** `debt-and-covenant-tracking`  ·  **Area:** Forecasting, customers, credit, debt and fraud risk  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 47%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Tracks borrowings and loan covenants: the schedule of facilities and balances, amortisation checked against what was actually repaid, a sweep for debt-like items missing from the list, each covenant computed on the agreement's own definitions with its headroom, a forward projection, the maturity profile, and the compliance report. Computes no covenant until the agreement's definitions are supplied, and labels book figures as book figures.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to track debt or loan covenants, check covenant compliance or headroom, build a debt or amortisation schedule, prepare a compliance certificate, review the maturity profile, or assess a possible breach.

Not for:

- A weekly cash forecast (use [cash-flow-forecast-13-week](cash-flow-forecast-13-week.md)) or for interest accruals alone (use [accruals-and-deferrals](accruals-and-deferrals.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which facilities do you have, and what are the terms of each: principal, rate, repayment, maturity, fees?* | Balances are in the books; terms are not |
| *How does each agreement define its covenants: the exact numerator and denominator, addbacks and caps?* | Without these a covenant ratio is wrong |
| *What is the test date and the test period: trailing twelve months, quarterly or annual?* | It often differs from the accounting period |
| *Which companies are inside the borrowing group?* | The covenant is tested on that perimeter |
| *Do you have a forecast, the lender's certificate format, and any waivers or amendments in force?* | Needed for the forward view and the report |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Loan agreements**, or a term sheet summary for each facility | Required | PDF, or type the terms in the chat |
| **Covenant definitions** for each agreement | **Required for any covenant.** Without them only book components are given | PDF or pasted |
| **Balance sheet and income statement** for the test period | Required | XLSX, CSV or PDF |
| **Ledger detail for the debt and interest accounts** | Required to check amortisation against actual repayments | XLSX or CSV |
| Forecast, certificate format, waivers | Recommended | XLSX or PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system**, and never contacts a lender.
- **Never presents a book ratio as a compliance position.**
- **Never assumes the test period matches the accounting period**, and never invents a rate, a term or a definition.
- **Never certifies compliance.** It prepares the computation; an authorised person signs.
