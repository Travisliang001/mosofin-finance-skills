# Expense Report Processor

> Review expense claims against policy.

**Skill:** `expense-report-processor`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 65%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you process, review, or post employee expense reports against your books.

**In plain words:** staff spend their own money, or the company card, and want it back or accounted for. The job is to check each line against the rules, put it in the right account, catch what should not be claimed, and work out what the employee is actually owed.

## When to use it

Typical requests, or anything like them:

- "review these expense claims"
- "check this expense report against policy"
- "code these employee expenses"
- "post the expense report to the GL"

Not for:

- Vendor invoices: use [invoice-data-extractor](invoice-data-extractor.md).
- Petty cash: use [petty-cash-management](petty-cash-management.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for the ledger detail and listings the work needs as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a policy-validated, GL-coded expense report ready for reimbursement and posting, with a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Summary
- Line Detail
- Exceptions
- GL Posting Inputs
- Ledger Checks

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
