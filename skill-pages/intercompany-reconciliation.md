# Intercompany reconciliation: both sides of every pair

> Match both sides of every entity pair; settlements and eliminations.

**Skill:** `intercompany-reconciliation`  ·  **Area:** Analysis and reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 68%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Reconciles intercompany balances between companies in the same group: builds the entity matrix, matches transactions on both sides, reconciles each pair, names the cause of every mismatch, handles currency differences, and proposes settlements and elimination entries. Lists any pair it could not reconcile because only one side was available. Reads each entity's ledger through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded intercompany ledgers.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to reconcile intercompany accounts, find an intercompany mismatch, match intercompany receivables and payables, reconcile an intercompany loan, prepare elimination entries, or plan an intercompany settlement.

Not for:

- Reconciling a balance sheet account to its support (use [balance-sheet-reconciliations](balance-sheet-reconciliations.md)) or for a bank account (use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which companies are in the group, and is any member missing from what I can see?* | Decides which pairs can be reconciled at all |
| *Which period?* | Sets every read and every cut-off |
| *Which accounts are the intercompany accounts in each company, and how does each company name the others?* | The counterparty may be a customer, a vendor, a class or only a memo. This is the join |
| *What is the group reporting currency, and which FX rates apply? Give the table, its source and its dates.* | Separates a real difference from a rate difference |
| *Is there an intercompany agreement: pricing method and payment terms?* | Several mismatch causes can only be judged against it |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Intercompany ledger for each company**: date, reference, amount, currency and counterparty per transaction | Required, for **both** sides of every pair | XLSX or CSV |
| Intercompany account list for each company | Required | Type it in the chat, or a chart of accounts export |
| FX rate table with source and dates | Required for cross-currency pairs | XLSX, CSV or pasted |
| Intercompany agreement or policy | Recommended | PDF or pasted |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the reconciliation, proposed entries and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Entity Pair Summary
- Transaction-Level Matching
- Mismatches
- Settlement Proposal
- Elimination Entries
- Currency Reconciliation
- Pair Matrix
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It cannot force a match by adjusting one side, and every entry is a proposal.
- **Never presents a single-sided figure as a reconciliation.**
- **Never supplies an FX rate, a transfer price or the cause of a mismatch.** Rates come from you with their source and date; a cause is stated only where the evidence shows it.
