# Audit preparation: the PBC list, ready before fieldwork

> Build the PBC list, score readiness, track owners and dates.

**Skill:** `audit-prep-pbc-list`  ·  **Area:** Stock, costing, credit policy and audit preparation  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 47%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Prepares a business for its audit: scopes the audit areas, builds the PBC (prepared-by-client) list by financial statement area, states what makes each item audit-ready, scores readiness and gaps, and sets up a tracker with owners and dates working back from fieldwork. Produces the schedules the books can supply and lists the documents only people can, such as bank statements, contracts and minutes.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to prepare for an audit or review, build or manage a PBC list, check audit readiness, or track auditor requests.

Not for:

- Running the close (use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md)) or reconciling balance sheet accounts (use [balance-sheet-reconciliations](balance-sheet-reconciliations.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Is this an audit, a review or a compilation, and for which company and period?* | Sets the level of support needed |
| *When does fieldwork start, and are the figures final or still moving?* | The timeline is built backwards from that date |
| *Has the auditor sent a request list? Do you have last year's list, adjustments and management letter?* | Their list governs, and last year's issues come back |
| *Who owns each area, and what support is already prepared outside the books?* | Every document item needs a named person |
| *Has the auditor told you materiality?* | It sets the priority of each area |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** at the period end, and the prior year | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Auditor's request list**, if received | Recommended. It governs when it exists | PDF or XLSX |
| Prior-year list, audit adjustments and management letter | Recommended | PDF or XLSX |
| Engagement type and period, fieldwork date, owners | Required for B and C | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the list, the schedules and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- PBC Tracker
- Readiness Summary
- Judgmental Area Memos
- Prior-Year Follow-Ups
- Timeline

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system**, and sends nothing to the auditor.
- **Never assumes the engagement is an audit.** An audit, a review and a compilation need very different support.
- **Never invents materiality**, and never marks an item ready without its evidence.
- **Where the auditor has sent a list, that list governs.** The skill maps to it and does not build a parallel one.
