# Lockbox cash application: which invoices did each payment settle?

> Apply a lockbox or bank file to open invoices; companion to [cash-application](cash-application.md).

**Skill:** `ar-cash-application-lockbox`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 64%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Applies customer receipts from a lockbox, ACH or wire file to open invoices: normalises the receipts, matches them through a hierarchy from exact reference to amount and payer, handles short-pays, deductions and unidentified cash, proposes the entries, and ties the applied total to the bank. Reads open invoices through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded bank and remittance files.

## When to use it

Use it when you have a lockbox or bank deposit file and remittance advice to apply, asks about deductions and short-pays at volume, or needs unapplied cash cleared from a bank file.

Not for:

- Applying payments already recorded in the books without a bank file (use [cash-application](cash-application.md)) or for the bank account itself (use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and which dates?* | Sets the receipts and the open invoices |
| *Can you supply the bank or lockbox file?* | The starting point, and never in the books |
| *Is there remittance advice: email, EDI or PDF?* | The biggest driver of match quality |
| *What is the deduction policy: write-off limit and approver?* | Decides how short-pays are treated |
| *Which account holds unapplied cash, and how do payers' names differ from customer names?* | Needed for unidentified receipts |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Bank or lockbox file** for the dates | Required | CSV or XLSX |
| **Remittance advice** | Recommended | PDF, CSV or pasted |
| **Open receivables**, by invoice | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Deduction policy | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Proposes applications and entries for a person to post, and applies nothing.

- Applied cash register
- Unapplied cash aging
- Deduction case list with reason codes
- Daily tie-out to bank deposits

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never accepts a fuzzy match on its own.** It is proposed with its basis, for a person to confirm.
- **Never writes off a short-pay without the policy and the approver.**
- **Never calls a tie between two sets of book figures a tie to the bank.**
- **Never guesses between two invoices of the same amount.**
