# Invoice Data Extractor

> Pull structured data from supplier invoices.

**Skill:** `invoice-data-extractor`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 72%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Use this skill whenever you upload a vendor invoice (PDF, image, or scanned doc) and wants structured data extracted, validated against your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "extract the data from this invoice"
- "pull the fields from this bill"
- "parse these invoices"
- "turn this PDF into a row I can post"

Not for:

- OCR alone — extract semantically.
- Posting the JE — pair with [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a structured row per invoice with vendor, dates, amounts, line items, tax breakdown, validation results, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Invoices
- Line Items
- Tax Detail
- Source Reference
- Validation Results
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
