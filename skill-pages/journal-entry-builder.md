# Journal Entry Builder

> Build and validate any journal entry against your real chart.

**Skill:** `journal-entry-builder`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 79%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you construct, format, or validate a journal entry against your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "book a JE for"
- "create a journal entry to record"
- "how do I record this transaction"
- "make me an accrual JE"
- "reclass these entries"
- "reverse this entry"

Not for:

- GL coding decisions only — use [gl-coding-assistant](gl-coding-assistant.md).
- Full period close — use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs balanced journal entries with supporting narration, validation results, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- QuickBooks Online
- Xero
- NetSuite
- Sage Intacct / 50 / 200
- Microsoft Dynamics 365 / Business Central
- Zoho Books / FreshBooks / Wave / Other

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
