# Prepaid Amortization Schedule

> Build, maintain and audit the prepaid schedule.

**Skill:** `prepaid-amortization-schedule`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 70%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you build, maintain, or audit a prepaid expense amortization schedule against a connected Mosofin workspace.

## When to use it

Typical requests, or anything like them:

- "set up a prepaid amortization schedule"
- "amortize this prepaid"
- "build a schedule for [insurance / SaaS / rent / etc.] paid in advance"
- "monthly prepaid expense JE"
- "reconcile prepaid balance to schedule"
- "is my prepaid account right"
- "did we miss an amortization"
- "find prepaids we never set up"

Not for:

- Deferred revenue (the customer side of the same idea): use [revenue-recognition-asc606](revenue-recognition-asc606.md).
- A full close: use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md). Reads the live prepaid account and expense ledger through the Mosofin read-only gateway; proposes a schedule, the amortization entries, and a tie-out to the GL prepaid balance. Proposes only: Mosofin posts nothing.

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks which company and which period the work is for, whether to use your Mosofin connection or files you supply, and for the facts the books cannot hold, such as a policy, a threshold or a date. It reads the answers back and waits for your yes.

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| List of prepaid items (vendor, description, amount, payment date, coverage period) | Required | Typed in the chat |
| Currency per item (ISO code) | Required if multi-currency | CSV or XLSX |
| Functional currency (ISO code) | Required | CSV or XLSX |
| Chart of accounts (for prepaid asset and expense accounts) | Required | CSV or XLSX |
| Tax jurisdiction (for tax handling on the original invoice) | Recommended | CSV or XLSX |
| Materiality threshold (below which an item is expensed directly) | Recommended | Typed in the chat |
| Amortization frequency (monthly (most common), quarterly) | Optional | CSV or XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

A workpaper you can keep, as an Excel file if you want one or as tables in the chat, with every figure traced to where it came from. Anything that would change the books is a proposal for a person to approve.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Prepaid Schedule Master
- Period Detail
- GL Tie-Out
- Monthly JE
- Cancellations / Modifications Log
- Ledger Findings

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
