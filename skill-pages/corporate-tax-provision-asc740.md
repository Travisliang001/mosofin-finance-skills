# Tax provision: the tax charge in the accounts

> Current and deferred tax in the accounts; effective rate reconciliation.

**Skill:** `corporate-tax-provision-asc740`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 54%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Computes the income tax provision for the financial statements under ASC 740 or IAS 12: starts from pre-tax book income, identifies permanent and temporary differences, computes current and deferred tax at rates you supply, sets out the valuation allowance evidence and uncertain positions, allocates tax between components, and builds the effective tax rate reconciliation with tie-outs.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for the tax provision, current and deferred tax expense, an effective tax rate reconciliation, a valuation allowance assessment, or ASC 740.

Not for:

- The tax return (use [tax-return-prep-corporate](tax-return-prep-corporate.md)) or for the deferred tax schedule alone (use [income-tax-deferred-tax-rollforward](income-tax-deferred-tax-rollforward.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, period and framework: US GAAP or IFRS?* | Deferred tax asset treatment differs |
| *What are the statutory rates, and the enacted future rates, per jurisdiction, with source?* | Never built in |
| *Which differences are permanent, and what is the tax basis of each temporary difference?* | The books show candidates only |
| *What are the opening deferred tax balances by component, and the loss and credit carryforwards with expiry?* | From prior workpapers and returns |
| *Are there uncertain tax positions, and what supports future taxable income?* | Judgments for management |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rates** per jurisdiction with source | Required | Type it in the chat |
| **Prior period provision workpapers** | Required for opening balances | XLSX |
| Tax returns and attribute schedules | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

- Provision Summary
- Book-to-Tax Reconciliation
- Temporary Differences & Deferred Tax
- Valuation Allowance Assessment
- Uncertain Tax Positions Register
- ETR Reconciliation
- Deferred Tax Rollforward
- Disclosure-Ready Figures
- Open Items / Rate Confirmations
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never concludes on a valuation allowance or an uncertain tax position.** It sets out the evidence.
- **Never takes a tax basis from the ledger.**
