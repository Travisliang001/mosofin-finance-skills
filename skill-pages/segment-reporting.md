# Segment reporting: the business as management sees it

> Reportable segments, their figures, and the reconciliation to the group.

**Skill:** `segment-reporting`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 60%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Prepares operating segment disclosures under ASC 280 or IFRS 8: identifies operating segments from what the chief operating decision maker actually reviews, applies the aggregation criteria and the quantitative thresholds, settles the measure of segment profit, assembles the segment data, reconciles it to the consolidated totals, and adds the entity-wide disclosures and comparatives. Reads segment dimensions through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded segment data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for segment reporting, reportable segments, the ten percent tests, a segment note, or a reconciliation of segments to the consolidated figures.

Not for:

- A management dashboard (use [kpi-dashboard-builder](kpi-dashboard-builder.md)) or the notes as a whole (use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and ASC 280 or IFRS 8?* | Sets the scope |
| *Who is the chief operating decision maker, and which reports do they actually review?* | This defines the segments |
| *How do those reports map to the dimensions in the books?* | Decides whether figures can be read or must be supplied |
| *Which profit measure does the decision maker use, and which other items do they see?* | Only those are disclosed |
| *How are sales between segments priced?* | A policy, not a posting |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Segment figures**: revenue, profit measure, assets by segment | Required | XLSX |
| **Consolidated statements** for the period | Required for the reconciliation | XLSX or PDF |
| **The reports the decision maker reviews** | Recommended | PDF |
| Prior year segment note | Recommended, for comparatives | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the segment schedules, the reconciliation and a coverage sheet, and concludes on no judgment.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Segment Identification
- Reportable Segment Tests
- Segment Financial Data
- Reconciliations
- Entity-Wide Disclosures
- Disclosure Draft
- Data Quality and Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never defines segments from the dimensions in the books alone.** They follow what the decision maker reviews.
- **Never discloses more than the decision maker actually sees.**
- **Never forces the reconciliation to agree.** A difference is shown and named.
- **Never decides aggregation or the profit measure for you.**
