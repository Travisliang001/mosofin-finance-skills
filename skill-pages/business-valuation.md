# Business valuation: a range, and what drives it

> A value range from three approaches, and the bridge to equity.

**Skill:** `business-valuation`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 40%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Estimates the value of a business or an equity interest using the standard approaches: defines the purpose, standard of value and valuation date, runs a discounted cash flow on your forecast, trading comparables and precedent transactions on your market data, triangulates to an enterprise value range, bridges to equity value, and shows the sensitivities and a report structure. Reads the company's historical figures through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded statements.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask what a business is worth, for a valuation range, a DCF with comparables, or an enterprise-to-equity bridge.

Not for:

- A full analyst package with visible WACC and a football field (use [valuation-agent-dcf-comps-football-field](valuation-agent-dcf-comps-football-field.md)) or for an impairment test (use [goodwill-and-impairment-testing](goodwill-and-impairment-testing.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which business, for what purpose, and on which standard of value?* | Purpose decides the standard and who must sign |
| *What is the valuation date?* | Every figure is read as at this date |
| *Do you have a forecast and normalised earnings?* | Needed for the cash flow and the multiples |
| *What are the discount rate inputs and the comparable companies or transactions, with sources?* | Never supplied here |
| *What are the diluted share count and the dilutive instruments?* | Needed for value per share |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Historical financial statements**, three years | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Forecast** | Required for the cash flow approach | XLSX |
| **Market data**: rate inputs, comparables, transactions, with sources | Required for each approach | XLSX |
| Cap table | Required for per-share value | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

- WACC Build
- Equity Value Bridge
- Adjustments
- Sensitivities
- Open Items / Specialist
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Produces valuation analysis, not an opinion of value.** It is not a licensed valuation professional, and a qualified one signs before anyone relies on the figure.
- **Never supplies market data.** Discount rate inputs, multiples, comparables and prices come from you, each with its source and date.
- **Never generates the forecast.** The cash flows are your.
- **Never gives a single number without its range and its basis.**
