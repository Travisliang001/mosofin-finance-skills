# Personal tax return: one person's year, brought together

> Organise and compute an individual's return; saves no personal data.

**Skill:** `personal-tax-return-prep`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 37%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Organises and computes an individual income tax return: reads the business-sourced figures for an owner where they are in the books, gathers and categorises income, applies deductions, reliefs and credits you confirm, computes taxable income, tax and the balance due or refund, and produces a document checklist. Reads only the entities the taxpayer has an interest in through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded documents.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to prepare or organise a personal tax return, compute an individual's tax, or build a checklist of documents for one.

Not for:

- A company's return (use [tax-return-prep-corporate](tax-return-prep-corporate.md)) or a projection (use [tax-planning-and-projections](tax-planning-and-projections.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Who is the taxpayer, in which jurisdiction, and for which tax year?* | The personal year may differ from the business year |
| *What are the current rates, thresholds and rules, with their source and year?* | Never built in, never carried forward |
| *What is the filing status: single, married, dependants, residency?* | Changes the computation |
| *Which connected entities does the taxpayer have an interest in, and in what capacity?* | Scopes what may be read |
| *What income documents, deductions and tax already paid are there?* | Personal documents are not in any books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Income documents** for the year | Required | PDF |
| **Rates and allowances** with source and year | Required | Type it in the chat, or PDF |
| **Prior year return** | Recommended | PDF |
| Deduction and credit evidence | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing. Handles one person's tax data and saves none of it.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Tax Computation Summary
- Income Detail
- Deductions & Reliefs
- Capital Gains
- Credits & Payments
- Document Checklist
- Open Items / Confirmations
- Business-Sourced Figures
- Statutory Values Used

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never reads an entity the taxpayer has no stated interest in.**
- **Never saves personal tax data**, and never asks for a full tax identification number.
- **Cross-border residency is flagged for a specialist, not worked through.**
