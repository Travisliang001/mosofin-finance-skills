# 1099 and T4A preparation: who must be reported, and for how much

> Who must be reported, for how much, and who has no tax form on file.

**Skill:** `1099-and-t4a-prep`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 58%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Prepares the population for information returns such as 1099s in the US or T4As in Canada: builds the list of reportable vendors from the year's payments, classifies payments to the right form and box on the rules you supply, validates the fields each form needs, reconciles the totals to the ledger, produces the filing list, and flags vendors with no tax form on file. Reads vendors and payments through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded payment data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to prepare 1099s or T4As, find which vendors need an information return, check for missing W-9s before year-end, or reconcile reportable payments.

Not for:

- Auditing the vendor master in general (use [vendor-onboarding-and-w9-tin](vendor-onboarding-and-w9-tin.md)) or payroll forms (use [payroll-tax-filings](payroll-tax-filings.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which payer, which country's regime, and which reporting year, with exact dates?* | Never defaulted |
| *Which forms are in scope, and what are the reporting thresholds for that year, with source?* | Never built in |
| *Which vendor tax forms are on file?* | Usually the missing piece |
| *How were payments made: card and third-party network payments are often reported by others?* | Avoids double reporting |
| *What filing channel will be used, and were payments made in more than one currency?* | Affects format and conversion |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Payments by vendor** for the year, with method | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Vendor list** with entity type and form status | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Thresholds and form rules** with source | Required | PDF |
| Vendor tax forms | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing. Masks tax identifiers wherever it can.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Filing Summary
- Reportable Vendor List
- Excluded Vendors
- Validation Exceptions
- Per-Form Detail
- Payment Reconciliation
- Withholding Reconciliation

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never decides that a payee is or is not reportable where the rule is unclear.** It lists them for review.
- **Never displays a full tax identifier where a masked one will do**, and saves none.
- **Never files or issues a form.**
