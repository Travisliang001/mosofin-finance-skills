# KPI dashboard: a few figures that mean the same thing every month

> Choose, define and compute the KPIs; list what the books cannot supply.

**Skill:** `kpi-dashboard-builder`  ·  **Area:** Analysis and reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 60%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds a KPI dashboard for a business: picks the metrics that fit the industry and the audience, defines each one precisely, computes it with its trend, adds targets and alerts, and lists every metric it declined because the data lives in another system.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a KPI dashboard, a financial or metrics dashboard, a board KPI page, an executive scorecard, or help deciding which key metrics to track.

Not for:

- A variance report against budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)), for the statement set itself (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)) or for a multi-entity cash forecast dashboard (use multi-entity-forecast-dashboard).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Who is the dashboard for? Board, executives, a department or investors* | Decides the depth and the number of metrics |
| *What industry and business model? SaaS, retail, services, manufacturing or another* | The right metrics differ by model |
| *Which period, and how often will it be refreshed?* | Sets every read and the trend length |
| *Which operational systems do you run, connected or not? Billing, CRM, time tracking, production* | Decides whether industry metrics are possible at all |
| *Do you have targets, and how are your current KPIs defined?* | A target gives a metric its status; an existing definition shows where drift began |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly profit and loss**, 24–36 months | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Balance sheet at each month end**, same months | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Receivables and payables ageing | Recommended, for DSO and DPO | XLSX or CSV |
| Operational exports: billing, CRM, time or production | Optional. Without them the industry metrics are listed as not available | CSV |
| Audience, business model, targets and current definitions | Required for B, recommended for the rest | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces a dashboard, its definitions and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Dashboard tab
- Data tab
- Definitions tab
- Source tab
- Coverage tab
- Not Available tab

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never approximates an operational metric from the ledger.** Recognised revenue is not ARR, a revenue decline is not churn, and an invoice count is not a customer count. A metric with no source is left blank and listed.
- **Never invents a target, a benchmark or a metric definition.**
