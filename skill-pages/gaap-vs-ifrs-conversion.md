# GAAP to IFRS: one business, two rulebooks

> Differences for this business, quantified and reconciled.

**Skill:** `gaap-vs-ifrs-conversion`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Converts financial statements between US GAAP and IFRS or a local GAAP: scopes the conversion, identifies the differences area by area for this business, classifies each, quantifies the adjustments with their deferred tax, applies first-time adoption rules where relevant, builds the reconciliation between the two frameworks, and restates the statements and policies. Reads the source statements through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded statements and policies.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to convert accounts between US GAAP and IFRS, list GAAP differences for their business, prepare a reconciliation between frameworks, or plan first-time adoption of IFRS.

Not for:

- Translating currencies (use [foreign-currency-translation-asc830](foreign-currency-translation-asc830.md)) or for group consolidation (use [consolidation-and-eliminations](consolidation-and-eliminations.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and from which framework to which?* | Direction matters |
| *Why convert: group reporting, a listing, or first-time adoption? Will dual reporting continue?* | Decides whether first-time adoption rules apply, and the deliverable |
| *How are the key items accounted for today: revenue, leases, inventory, development costs, fixed assets, financial instruments?* | The core input |
| *Can you supply the schedules for the affected areas?* | Adjustments are computed from them |
| *What is materiality, and the transition date if this is first-time adoption?* | Sets the effort and the opening balance sheet |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Source financial statements** and trial balance | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Accounting policies** in use | Required | PDF, or type it in the chat |
| **Schedules** for the affected areas | Required to quantify | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never infers an accounting policy from a balance.**
- **Never states a standard's requirement from memory as settled.** You confirm the standard in force at the reporting date.
- **Never drops the deferred tax on an adjustment.**
- **Never decides an elective exemption for you.**
