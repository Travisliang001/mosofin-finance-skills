# Internal audit: program, workpapers, findings, report

> Audit program, workpapers, findings and report.

**Skill:** `internal-audit-workpaper`  ·  **Area:** Controls, audit, treasury and the chart of accounts  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 41%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Plans and documents an internal audit: scoping, understanding the area, the audit program, fieldwork with workpapers, findings written as condition, criteria, cause and consequence, risk ratings, management responses, the report and follow-up. Tests whole transaction populations where the books allow it, in place of a sample, and states which procedures need observation, interviews or documents.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to plan or run an internal audit, write an audit program, document workpapers, draft audit findings or an internal audit report, or follow up prior findings.

Not for:

- Preparing for the external audit (use [audit-prep-pbc-list](audit-prep-pbc-list.md)) or for fraud red-flag testing (use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which area is being audited, what are the objectives, and is it operational, financial, compliance or IT?* | Everything links back to the objectives |
| *Which company and period?* | Sets the population |
| *What are the criteria: the policies, regulations or standards the area must meet?* | The "should be" half of every finding |
| *How does the process work, and what did prior audits find?* | Process knowledge is not in a ledger; repeat findings rate higher |
| *Who is performing the audit, did they operate this area, and is completeness in scope?* | Independence, and whether an outside reference point is needed |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Transaction data** for the area and period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Criteria**: policies, procedures, regulations | Required for findings | PDF, or type it in the chat |
| Process documentation and walkthrough notes | Recommended | PDF |
| Prior audit report and open actions | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the program, workpapers, findings and a coverage sheet, and changes no record.

- Audit Plan
- Audit Program
- Workpapers Index
- Findings Register
- Follow-Up Tracker
- Procedure Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never writes a finding without criteria.** With no "should be", it is an observation.
- **Never presents an analytical result as a conclusion**, or a full-population test as full assurance.
- **Never rates a finding or accepts a management response for the auditor.** It proposes; the auditor decides.
