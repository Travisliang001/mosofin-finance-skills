# FX revaluation: what foreign balances are worth at period end

> Revalue foreign balances at period end; translation and proposed entries.

**Skill:** `multicurrency-fx-revaluation`  ·  **Area:** Quarter-end and year-end close, planning and foreign currency  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 60%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Handles foreign-currency accounting at period end: confirms each entity's functional currency, separates transaction differences from translation, revalues monetary balances at the closing rate, splits realised from unrealised gains and losses, translates foreign operations with the translation adjustment in equity, and proposes the revaluation entries and disclosures. Checks first whether the accounting system already revalues on its own, so nothing is counted twice.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about FX revaluation, unrealised or realised FX gains and losses, foreign-currency receivables or payables at period end, translating a foreign subsidiary, or the cumulative translation adjustment.

Not for:

- Intercompany mismatches (use [intercompany-reconciliation](intercompany-reconciliation.md)) or building a general journal entry (use [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company or companies, and which period end?* | Sets the balances and the closing date |
| *What are the rates: closing, average and transaction-date, with their source and dates?* | A rate is never taken from the skill |
| *What is each entity's functional currency, and what is the presentation currency?* | The home currency in the system usually agrees, and when it does not everything changes |
| *Does your accounting system revalue automatically, and has it run for this period?* | Prevents double counting |
| *US GAAP or IFRS? Any hedges, and which accounts are monetary?* | Decides treatment at the edges |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Foreign-currency balances** at the period end: account, currency, foreign amount, booked amount | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rate table**: closing, average and transaction-date rates, with source and dates | Required | XLSX, CSV or pasted |
| **FX gain and loss account detail** for the period | Required to check for entries already posted | XLSX or CSV |
| **Trial balance of each foreign operation** | Required for translation | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Proposes entries for a person to post, and never supplies an exchange rate.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Functional Currency Determinations
- Transaction FX
- Translation Workpaper
- CTA / Accumulated OCI Roll-Forward
- Journal Entries
- Hedge Accounting
- Disclosure Data
- Posted vs. Proposed

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal.
- **Never supplies an exchange rate**, and never reuses one from an earlier run.
- **Never proposes a revaluation entry before checking whether the system already posted one.**
- **Never revalues a non-monetary balance**, and never decides the functional currency for you.
