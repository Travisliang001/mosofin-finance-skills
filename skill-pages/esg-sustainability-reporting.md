# ESG reporting: the same discipline as the financial figures

> Scope, metric catalogue and data pipeline for ESG figures.

**Skill:** `esg-sustainability-reporting`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 39%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Designs an ESG and sustainability reporting process: the reporting scope and boundaries, the catalogue of metrics, the data pipeline and controls behind each one, and the reporting and narrative, built so the figures can stand up to assurance. Reads spend through a connected Mosofin workspace to show which activity data the books can point to, and states that spend is not consumption. Works from uploaded activity data when Mosofin is not used.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to set up ESG or sustainability reporting, choose ESG metrics, plan emissions data collection, prepare for assurance over ESG data, or map data sources for a sustainability report.

Not for:

- A financial KPI dashboard (use [kpi-dashboard-builder](kpi-dashboard-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What is the reporting for: a regulation, investors, customers, or voluntary?* | Decides what is mandatory |
| *Which entities and sites are in the boundary, and on what basis: control or equity share?* | Determines whose impact is counted |
| *Which metrics are tracked today, and under which framework, if any?* | The honest starting point |
| *Where does the activity data live: utility bills, travel, procurement, HR? Which emission factor source?* | Most of it is outside the books |
| *Will the figures be assured, and what was published before?* | Changes the controls needed |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Activity data**: energy, fuel, travel, headcount | Required for any metric | XLSX or CSV |
| **Emission factors** with source, version and year | Required for emissions | XLSX or PDF |
| Prior disclosures | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the scope, the catalogue and the data plan, and never supplies an emission factor.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never supplies an emission factor.** It asks for the source, the version and the year.
- **Never converts spend into a physical quantity without a stated factor**, and labels any spend-based figure as an estimate.
- **Never reports a metric with no named owner and no source.**
- **Never claims assurance readiness.** It lists what an assurer would ask for.
