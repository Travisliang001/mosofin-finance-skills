# Payroll tax filings: what was withheld, deposited and reported

> Withheld, deposited and reported, reconciled.

**Skill:** `payroll-tax-filings`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 57%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports payroll tax deposits and returns: identifies the filing obligations you confirm, the deposit schedule assigned for the year, computes the amounts due from the payroll register on rates you supply, reconciles deposits made to the liability, and prepares the figures for periodic returns and year-end wage statements. Reads the payroll liability accounts through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded payroll registers.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about payroll tax deposits, reconciling payroll tax liabilities to payments, quarterly payroll returns, or year-end wage statement totals.

Not for:

- The payroll journal entry (use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md)) or tying the register to the ledger (use [payroll-reconciliation](payroll-reconciliation.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which employer, which employment jurisdictions, and which period?* | The country on record is only a pointer |
| *What are the current rates, wage bases and deposit rules, with source and year?* | Never built in |
| *What deposit schedule were you assigned for this year?* | It decides the due dates |
| *Can you supply the payroll registers, and does a provider file and remit for you?* | The register is the missing half |
| *Is this a periodic return, year-end, or both?* | Year-end adds wage statements |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Payroll registers** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rates and deposit rules** with source | Required | PDF |
| **Deposit confirmations** | Required for the reconciliation | PDF or CSV |
| Prior filings | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing. Works on totals where it can and saves no employee data.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Filing Obligations Calendar
- Amounts Due Computation
- Deposit Reconciliation
- Periodic Return Figures
- Year-End Reconciliation
- Wage Statement Data
- Open Items / Professional Review
- Statutory Values Used

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never deposits, files or signs anything.**
- **Never shows employee-level pay where a total will do**, and saves no employee data.
- **Never assumes the deposit schedule.** It can change each year.
