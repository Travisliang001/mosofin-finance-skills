# Credit and collections policy: the system, not the chase

> Design the credit rules and collections cadence on the real customer base.

**Skill:** `customer-credit-and-collections-policy`  ·  **Area:** Stock, costing, credit policy and audit preparation  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 67%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Designs a credit and collections policy for a business: segments the actual customer base by revenue, payment behaviour, disputes and concentration, then sets the credit rules, the collections cadence day by day, the handling of disputes and deductions, who approves exceptions, and the KPIs with a measured baseline. Tests each rule against the real customer base before it is written down.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a credit policy, a collections policy or process, a dunning schedule, how to reduce DSO or bad debt as a system, or who should approve credit exceptions.

Not for:

- One customer's limit (use [credit-limit-and-customer-risk](credit-limit-and-customer-risk.md)) or for running this month's collections (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What problem is this solving: slow payment, bad debt, disputes, or no policy at all?* | It decides what the policy optimises for |
| *Which company, and as at what date?* | Sets the base that is segmented |
| *Which of today's terms are policy, and which have simply built up over time?* | Recorded terms mix both |
| *Can orders actually be put on hold, and how?* | A hold nobody can enforce is not a policy |
| *Who owns the customer relationship, who may approve an exception, and what are the write-off limits?* | Governance comes from people, never from the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer list with receivables ageing** | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Invoice and payment history**, 12 months or more | Required for segmentation by behaviour | XLSX or CSV |
| Credit notes and write-offs for the period | Recommended, for disputes and bad debt | XLSX or CSV |
| Current terms, hold process, roles and limits | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the policy, the segmentation and a coverage sheet, and changes no terms.

- Detailed policy / SOP
- Segmentation and scorecard example
- Templates for outreach
- Dispute reason code catalog
- KPI definitions and cadence

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never changes terms, limits or holds in any system, and never contacts a customer.**
- **Never writes a rule without testing it on the real base.** A hold rule that would stop a third of the customers is reported as that.
- **Never reports governance as verified.** Who approved what is not in the books.
- **Never reports DSO without its basis**, and never invents a target.
