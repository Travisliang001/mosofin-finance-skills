# Corporate tax computation: from book profit to taxable profit

> Book profit to taxable profit, with the workpapers.

**Skill:** `tax-return-prep-corporate`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Prepares the corporate income tax computation and its workpapers: starts from book profit and tests that it agrees with the accounts, adds back disallowable costs and sweeps the ledger for ones nobody listed, deducts items taxed differently, computes tax depreciation or capital allowances, applies loss relief and credits, and works out the tax, payments and supporting schedules.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to prepare a corporate tax computation, convert book income to taxable income, find disallowable expenses, or build tax return workpapers.

Not for:

- The tax charge in the accounts (use [corporate-tax-provision-asc740](corporate-tax-provision-asc740.md)) or for an individual (use [personal-tax-return-prep](personal-tax-return-prep.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, jurisdiction and tax year?* | Sets every rule that applies |
| *What are the current rates, thresholds and rules, with their source and year?* | Never built in, never carried forward |
| *Which categories are disallowed in this jurisdiction, and to what extent?* | Drives the add-backs |
| *What are the allowance pools, losses brought forward and credits, from the prior return?* | Tax attributes are not in the books |
| *Are there group or related party matters?* | Group relief and pricing adjustments |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance and accounts** for the year | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Rates and rules** with source and year | Required | Type it in the chat, or PDF |
| **Prior year return** | Required for attributes | PDF |
| Fixed asset additions and disposals | Required for tax depreciation | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

- Tax Computation Summary
- Book-to-Tax Reconciliation
- Capital Allowances / Tax Depreciation
- Loss Memorandum
- Credits
- Payments & Balance Due
- Reconciliations
- Open Items / Confirmations
- Disallowable Candidates Sweep
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never decides that a cost is deductible.** It lists candidates and applies the schedule you supply.
- **Never takes a loss carryforward or an allowance pool from the ledger.** They come from the prior return.
