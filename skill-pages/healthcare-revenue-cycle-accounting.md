# Healthcare revenue: the list price almost nobody pays

> Net patient revenue and reserves; billing tied to the ledger, totals only.

**Skill:** `healthcare-revenue-cycle-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 61%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Ties a healthcare provider's billing activity to the general ledger and estimates what the care actually earned: maps the revenue cycle, estimates net patient service revenue after contractual allowances, reconciles receivables and cash, sets out the reserves and key estimates, and lists the close controls. Works at aggregate level only and handles no patient detail.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about net patient revenue, contractual allowances, payer mix, denials, charity care against bad debt, or reconciling a billing system to the ledger.

Not for:

- General receivables ageing (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)) or the allowance in a non-healthcare business (use [bad-debt-and-write-offs](bad-debt-and-write-offs.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which provider and period?* | Sets the scope |
| *Can you supply billing totals by payer class: charges, payments, adjustments?* | The billing system holds them |
| *Are gross charges recorded in the ledger, or net only?* | Decides whether a realisation rate can be read |
| *Do you have the receivables ageing by payer class, and denials and write-offs with reasons?* | The billing system's ageing is the real one |
| *What is the charity care policy, and which payer contracts apply?* | Decides presentation and the allowance |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Billing summary by payer class**: charges, payments, adjustments | Required. Totals only, no patient detail | XLSX or CSV |
| **Receivables ageing by payer class** | Required | XLSX |
| **Ledger detail** for revenue, contra-revenue, receivables and cash | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Payer contract rates and charity care policy | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the reconciliation, the estimates and proposed entries, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Net Revenue Model
- Realization History
- Revenue Cycle Map
- AR Roll-Forward and Tie-Out
- AR Aging and Metrics
- Reserve Calculations
- Charity Care vs. Bad Debt
- Cash and Unapplied
- Close Controls Checklist and Calendar

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never takes, stores or displays patient-level information.**
- **Never reports gross charges as revenue.**
- **Never assumes a realisation rate when one can be computed**, and never invents a contractual rate.
- **Never treats charity care as bad debt, or the reverse.** The policy decides.
