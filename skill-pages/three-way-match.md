# Three-way match: order, receipt, invoice

> PO / receipt / invoice matching across the whole population.

**Skill:** `three-way-match`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 75%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Matches a vendor invoice to its purchase order and goods receipt before payment: header checks, then every line for quantity, price and item, your tolerance applied, fifteen exception tests from price creep to an unapproved order, and a Pay, Hold or Reject recommendation with the matched and disputed amounts. Counts the legs it actually has and says plainly whether it ran a three-way or a two-way match. Reads bills and purchase orders through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded POs, delivery records and invoices.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask whether an invoice should be paid, wants to match invoices to purchase orders or receipts, mentions overbilling, short shipments, duplicate invoices, received-not-invoiced or goods received not invoiced (GRNI), or uploads a PO, receipt or invoice.

Not for:

- Finding duplicate payments alone (use [duplicate-invoice-detection](duplicate-invoice-detection.md)) or for reconciling a vendor statement (use [vendor-statement-reconciliation](vendor-statement-reconciliation.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the documents yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What tolerance applies: price %, quantity %, and a total-amount limit?* | Decides what is a match and what is an exception |
| *Who may approve a purchase order, and up to what amount?* | Exceptions 11 and 12 (unapproved, or approved beyond authority) cannot run without it |
| *Are services purchase orders in scope, and what stands in for a goods receipt on them?* | A services PO with no receipt is a two-way match **by design**; a goods PO with no receipt is two-way **by omission**. They look the same in the output and are not |
| *Any orders or invoices in a foreign currency, and what rate policy applies?* | A price variance that is really an FX movement is the classic false exception |
| *Who owns exception resolution?* | Every exception row needs an owner |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase order(s)** | Required | PDF, XLSX, CSV, or pasted |
| **Goods receipt, proof of delivery, or signed service confirmation** | **Required for goods.** Without it the match is two-way and the output says so | PDF, XLSX, CSV, or pasted |
| **Vendor invoice(s)** | Required | PDF, XLSX, CSV, or pasted |
| Tolerance policy and approval matrix | Recommended | Type it in the chat |
| Vendor master, for payment-terms checks | Optional | CSV or pasted |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never pays, holds or releases money, and never changes data in any system.** Every recommendation is for a person to act on.
- **Never invents a tolerance.** With none given, it expects an exact match and flags every variance; how much overbilling is acceptable is the business's decision.
- **Never lets an absent test read as a pass, and never concludes fraud.** It names which match it ran and which tests did not; patterns are reported with counts and amounts, people conclude.
