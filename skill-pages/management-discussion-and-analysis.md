# MD&A: why the numbers moved, in management's voice

> Draft MD&A from the figures; forward-looking statements flagged.

**Skill:** `management-discussion-and-analysis`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 60%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Drafts the management discussion and analysis: the overview, results of operations with every variance computed from the books, non-GAAP measures where used, liquidity and capital resources, critical accounting estimates, and a section for known trends that management writes, then checks that every figure agrees with the statements. Flags every forward-looking statement for sign-off.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to draft or review MD&A, a management commentary, an explanation of results for a report or filing, or a liquidity discussion.

Not for:

- Variance against the budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)), a KPI dashboard (use [kpi-dashboard-builder](kpi-dashboard-builder.md)) or the notes (use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and what kind of report: 10-K, 10-Q, an IFRS annual report or a private report?* | Decides the sections and the legal weight |
| *What drove the changes in revenue, cost and margin?* | The heart of the document |
| *What are the known trends and uncertainties?* | Never authored here |
| *What are the facilities, capex plans and covenant status?* | Cash and debt are in the books; plans and terms are not |
| *Which non-GAAP measures are used, who signs off, and does legal review apply?* | Forward-looking wording needs sign-off |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial statements**, current and comparative | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Prior MD&A** | Recommended, for consistency | PDF |
| Drivers, trends, facilities and plans | Required for a complete draft | Type it in the chat |
| Non-GAAP definitions and reconciliations | Required if used | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces a draft for management and legal review, and publishes nothing.

- Variance Analysis Backing
- Number Tie-Out
- Non-GAAP Reconciliations
- Forward-Looking Statements Log
- Critical Estimates Summary
- Revenue Bridge
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never authors a forward-looking statement.** The section for trends is drafted as questions for management.
- **Never invents the reason for a variance.** With no driver given, the variance is stated and the reason is marked as needed.
- **Never defines a non-GAAP measure**, and never shows one without its reconciliation.
- **Never uses a comparative that has not been checked against what was issued.**
