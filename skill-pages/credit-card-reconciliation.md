# Credit Card Reconciliation

> Reconcile the card statement with receipt matching.

**Skill:** `credit-card-reconciliation`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 59%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you reconcile a corporate or business credit card statement to the GL with receipt matching, working from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "reconcile the corporate card"
- "match receipts to the credit card statement"
- "corp card rec"
- "reconcile [Amex / Visa / Mastercard / Brex / Ramp / Pleo / Spendesk] statement"
- "who spent what on the card"

Not for:

- Personal employee expense reports — use [expense-report-processor](expense-report-processor.md).
- Bank reconciliation — use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a reconciled credit card workpaper with receipt status, GL coding, exceptions, the posting JE, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Reconciliation Summary
- Transaction Detail
- Exceptions
- GL Posting
- Source Receipts Reference
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
