# Closing Entries and Trial Balance

> Close the period and produce the post-closing TB.

**Skill:** `closing-entries-and-trial-balance`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 69%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you prepare closing entries, lock the books for a fiscal period, or produce / audit a trial balance from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "close the books for the year"
- "closing entries"
- "roll income to retained earnings"
- "final trial balance"
- "reset the P&L for new year"
- "lock the period"
- "check the trial balance"
- "preliminary vs final TB"

Not for:

- The full year-end close — use [year-end-close](year-end-close.md).
- Monthly close orchestration — use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs the closing entries, post-closing trial balance, a tie-out workpaper, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Pre-Closing Trial Balance
- Closing Entries
- Post-Closing Trial Balance
- Income Statement (for the closed year)
- Tie-Outs
- Roll Forward to New Year
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
