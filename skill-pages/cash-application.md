# Cash Application

> Apply customer payments; short pays, overpays, FX.

**Skill:** `cash-application`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 63%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you apply customer payments to open invoices, working from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "apply these customer payments"
- "cash app this remittance"
- "match payments to invoices"
- "allocate this wire to invoices"
- "process the lockbox file"
- "short pay handling"

Not for:

- Processing refunds — use [credit-memo-and-refund-handler](credit-memo-and-refund-handler.md).
- The bank reconciliation itself — use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md).

## What it asks you first

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs an application schedule, the corresponding proposed JE, exceptions for unidentified or short payments, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Application Summary
- Payment-to-Invoice Mapping
- Exceptions
- GL Posting
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
