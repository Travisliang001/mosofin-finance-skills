# VAT and GST: output tax, input tax, and the return

> Output tax, reverse charge, input tax and the VAT account reconciliation.

**Skill:** `vat-gst-international`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports VAT and GST across countries: registration obligations, place of supply, output tax on sales, the reverse charge and a check that it nets to nil, input tax on purchases and the effect of partial exemption, special schemes, return preparation, and the reconciliation of the VAT accounts to returns. Reads sales, purchases and the VAT accounts through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about VAT or GST returns, reverse charge, input tax recovery, partial exemption, place of supply, or reconciling the VAT account.

Not for:

- US sales tax (use [sales-tax-us-multistate](sales-tax-us-multistate.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity and period, and in which countries are you registered, since when?* | Sets every rule that applies |
| *What are the current rates, thresholds and rules, with their source and year?* | Never built in, never carried forward |
| *Where is the business established, and are there fixed establishments elsewhere?* | Drives registration and place of supply |
| *Which supplies are zero-rated and which exempt? Is there a partial exemption method?* | Decides output tax and input recovery |
| *Which special schemes are in use, and are there e-invoicing obligations?* | Changes the return |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales and purchases with tax codes** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **VAT account detail** | Required for the reconciliation | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rates and thresholds** with source | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| Returns filed | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never decides whether a supply is standard-rated, zero-rated or exempt.**
- **Never decides the place of supply for you.** It sets out the facts that determine it.
- **Never registers, files or pays.**
