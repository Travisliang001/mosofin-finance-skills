# Percentage of completion: how done are you?

> Revenue over time on contracts; loss provisions and contract balances.

**Skill:** `construction-percentage-of-completion`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 48%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for long-term construction and similar contracts recognised over time under ASC 606 or IFRS 15: confirms the over-time criteria, identifies the performance obligations, sets the transaction price, applies the measure of progress, computes cumulative revenue, cost and margin, applies changes in estimate, provides for loss contracts, and tracks contract assets and liabilities against billings. Reads costs and billings by job through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded contract schedules.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about percentage of completion, cost-to-cost, over- and under-billings, work in progress on contracts, a loss-making contract, or revenue on a long-term project.

Not for:

- Costing jobs without revenue recognition (use [cost-accounting-job-order](cost-accounting-job-order.md)) or revenue contracts in general (use [revenue-recognition-asc606](revenue-recognition-asc606.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | Sets the scope |
| *Which contracts are in progress: customer, scope, price, term? How are jobs tagged in the books?* | This is how costs and billings are read by contract |
| *What is the total estimated cost at completion for each contract, and when was it last updated?* | The number everything depends on |
| *Which measure of progress applies: cost-to-cost, units or milestones?* | Output measures need site data |
| *Are there variations, claims, bonuses or penalties, and are they approved?* | Variable consideration is constrained |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract list**: price, term, status | Required | XLSX |
| **Estimated cost at completion** per contract, with date | Required | XLSX |
| **Costs to date and billings to date** per contract | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Variations and claims with approval status | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the contract schedule and proposed entries, and never estimates cost to complete.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Contract Register
- Cost & Estimate Tracking
- Revenue & Margin Recognition
- WIP Schedule
- Loss Contract Provisions
- Billings Reconciliation
- Modifications Log
- Disclosures

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never estimates the cost to complete.** A contract with no current estimate is listed as not computed.
- **Never recognises a variation or claim that has not been approved** without your judgment on it.
- **Never defers a loss.** A loss-making contract is provided for in full when it is known.
- **Never nets contract assets against contract liabilities across contracts.**
