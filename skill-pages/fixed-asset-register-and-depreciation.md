# Fixed Asset Register and Depreciation

> FA register, depreciation, capitalisation and disposals.

**Skill:** `fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 57%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you maintain a fixed asset register, compute depreciation, capitalize new acquisitions, dispose of assets, or reconcile FA to the GL from your books.

**In plain words:** when a business buys something that will last for years — a van, a machine, a building fit-out — the cost is not an expense all at once. It goes on the balance sheet and is written down a bit each month over the years it will be used. Doing that properly needs a list of every asset, what it cost, when it went into use, and how fast it is being written down.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for any of these, or something like them.

Typical requests:

- "add this asset to the FA register"
- "compute monthly depreciation"
- "depreciation schedule"
- "capitalize this purchase"
- "dispose of this asset"
- "reconcile fixed assets"
- "depreciation methods"
- "asset impairment review"

Not for:

- Intangibles — use [intangibles-and-amortization](intangibles-and-amortization.md).
- Leases — use [lease-accounting-asc842-ifrs16](lease-accounting-asc842-ifrs16.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for ledger detail and listings as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs an FA register with depreciation schedule, capitalization, disposal, reconciliation workpapers, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Asset Register Master
- Depreciation Schedule
- Acquisitions
- Disposals
- Roll-Forward by Class
- GL Tie-Out
- Impairment Review
- GL Posting
- Tax-Book Depreciation Differences

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.
