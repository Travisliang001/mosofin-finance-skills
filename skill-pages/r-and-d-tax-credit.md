# R&D tax credit: what qualifies, and what it cost

> Qualifying R&D cost by project and the claim documentation.

**Skill:** `r-and-d-tax-credit`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports an R&D tax credit or relief claim: confirms the regime you state for their jurisdiction, sets out the tests for qualifying activity project by project, draws the boundaries of qualifying cost, computes qualifying expenditure from the books where projects are tracked, computes the benefit on your rates, and builds the documentation a claim needs. Reads project and payroll costs through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded cost data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about an R&D tax credit, qualifying research expenditure, what R&D costs can be claimed, or documentation for an R&D claim.

Not for:

- Capitalising development costs in the accounts (use [intangibles-and-amortization](intangibles-and-amortization.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, jurisdiction and tax year?* | Each regime has its own rules |
| *Which regime and scheme apply, and what are the rates, with source and year?* | Never built in |
| *What are the projects, with enough technical detail to test them?* | The heart of the claim |
| *How was staff time split between projects, and on what basis?* | The largest cost and the least visible |
| *Is the company an SME or large, and were there grants or subcontractors?* | Changes the scheme and the boundaries |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Project descriptions** | Required | PDF or text |
| **Costs by project**: staff, materials, subcontractors | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Time allocation** by person and project | Required for staff cost | XLSX |
| Regime rules and rates with source | Required | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

- Claim Summary
- Project Qualification Assessment
- Cost Analysis
- Staff Time Apportionment
- Benefit Computation
- Documentation Index
- Open Items / Specialist Review
- Cost Population Completeness
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never decides that an activity qualifies.** It sets out the tests and records your answer.
- **Never estimates staff time on R&D.** The apportionment comes from you, with its basis.
- **Never includes a cost on the wrong side of the boundary to enlarge a claim.**
