# Year-end close: the most rigorous close of the year

> Annual procedures, filings, closing the year and verifying it stayed locked.

**Skill:** `year-end-close`  ·  **Area:** Quarter-end and year-end close, planning and foreign currency  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Runs the year-end close on top of the monthly and quarterly close: checks that all twelve months are closed, works through the annual procedures such as counts, impairment, tax true-up, estimates and disclosures, lists the statutory filings in scope, supports the audit, closes the year and opens the new one, then verifies that the books stayed locked and the new year opened clean. Marks which tasks the books can evidence and which need a person.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to run or plan a year-end close, prepare for annual accounts, check retained earnings rolled forward correctly, or confirm nothing was posted into a closed year.

Not for:

- The monthly close (use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md)), the quarter (use [quarter-end-close](quarter-end-close.md)) or the auditor's request list (use [audit-prep-pbc-list](audit-prep-pbc-list.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which fiscal year-end, and which framework: US GAAP, IFRS or local GAAP?* | Sets the period and the procedures |
| *Is there an audit, a review, a compilation or none? Which firm, and what timeline?* | Decides whether the audit steps apply, and sets the calendar backwards |
| *Which statutory filings are in scope?* | The filing list drives a whole step |
| *What major events happened this year: an acquisition, a disposal, new equity or debt, a restructuring?* | Each brings its own year-end work |
| *What did last year's auditor raise, and what is year-end materiality?* | Last year's findings come back |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance at the year end** and at the prior year end | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **General ledger detail** for the year, and for the new year to date | Required for the lock and opening-balance tests | XLSX or CSV |
| **Fixed asset register, count results, lease and equity schedules** | Recommended | XLSX |
| Filing list, events, prior-year findings | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the checklist, the schedules and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Year-End Calendar
- PBC List
- Lead Schedules Index
- Tax Provision Workpaper
- Estimates and Judgments Memo
- Going Concern Assessment
- Subsequent Events Log
- Statutory Filings Tracker
- Final Tie-Out and Books Locked
- Carry-Forward Items

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It locks nothing and posts no closing entry; every entry is a proposal.
- **Never drops a task it cannot perform.** It stays, marked for a person.
- **Never treats a locked year as an unchanged year.** It checks.
- **Never authors a going-concern conclusion or a forward-looking statement**, and never supplies materiality.
