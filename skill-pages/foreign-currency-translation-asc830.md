# Currency translation: a foreign subsidiary in the group's currency

> Translate a foreign subsidiary and prove the translation adjustment.

**Skill:** `foreign-currency-translation-asc830`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Translates foreign subsidiaries into the reporting currency under ASC 830 or IAS 21: determines each entity's functional currency, remeasures transactions in other currencies, translates the financial statements, proves the cumulative translation adjustment independently instead of treating it as a balancing figure, handles intercompany loans, and proposes the entries and controls. Reads each entity through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded trial balances and a rate table.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to translate a foreign subsidiary, about the cumulative translation adjustment, functional currency, remeasurement, or ASC 830 or IAS 21.

Not for:

- Period-end revaluation of foreign balances in one company (use [multicurrency-fx-revaluation](multicurrency-fx-revaluation.md)) or the full group consolidation (use [consolidation-and-eliminations](consolidation-and-eliminations.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entities, and what currency does the group report in?* | Decides which entities need translating at all |
| *Which period end?* | Sets the closing rate date |
| *What are the rates: closing, average and historical, with source and dates?* | A rate is never taken from the skill |
| *What is each entity's functional currency, and does it agree with the currency its books are kept in?* | When they differ, remeasurement comes first |
| *Are there intercompany loans, and are any designated as long-term investments? Is any entity in a hyperinflationary economy?* | Decides whether differences go to earnings or to equity, and can change the method |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance of each foreign entity**, in its own currency | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rate table**: closing, average and historical, with source and dates | Required | XLSX or pasted |
| **Prior period translated balances** and the adjustment brought forward | Required for the proof | XLSX |
| Intercompany loan list with designations | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the translation, the proof and proposed entries, and never supplies a rate.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a rate from memory**, and never reuses one from an earlier run.
- **Never accepts the translation adjustment because the statements balance.** It is proved.
- **Never decides the functional currency**, and never assumes it equals the home currency on record without asking.
- **Never approximates a historical rate without saying so.**
