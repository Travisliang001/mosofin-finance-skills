# Model review: the last pass before it goes out

> Last review of a model: tie-out, formulas, issue log, pass or hold.

**Skill:** `model-auditor-financial-model-review`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 44%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Performs the last review before a financial model is sent out: freezes the scope, ties the model's historical figures to the books, reviews formula integrity, checks that the statements and schedules agree with each other, reviews the scenarios and sensitivities, and delivers an issue log with a pass or hold call. Reads the source figures through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, and works from the model you upload.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to review, audit or sanity-check a financial model before it goes to a board, lender or investor, or to tie a model's actuals to the books.

Not for:

- Building a model (use [three-statement-financial-model](three-statement-financial-model.md) or [model-builder-integrated-three-statement](model-builder-integrated-three-statement.md)) or running scenarios (use [scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis](scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What is the model for, and who receives it?* | Sets how strict the review is |
| *Can you upload the model, and is the scope the whole model, selected tabs, or one issue?* | The model is not in the workspace |
| *Which entity or group does the model represent, and when were its actuals extracted?* | Decides what it is tied to |
| *What is the materiality threshold?* | Separates a finding from a rounding difference |
| *Are there known problem areas?* | Focuses the review |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **The financial model** | Required | XLSX |
| **Source statements** the history was drawn from | Required if Mosofin is not used | XLSX or PDF |
| Account-to-line mapping | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces an issue log and a recommendation, changes no model, and gives no opinion on the assumptions.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Review Scope
- Source Tie-Out
- Formula Integrity Exceptions
- Statement and Schedule Checks
- Scenario and Sensitivity Tests
- Issue Log

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never edits the model.** Every issue is logged for the owner.
- **Never judges whether an assumption is right.** It checks that it is visible, sourced and applied as stated.
- **Never calls a pass on tabs it did not review.** The scope is stated on the log.
- **Never treats a stale history as an error**, or the reverse. It reports when the actuals were extracted.
