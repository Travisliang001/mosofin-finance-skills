# Duplicate Invoice Detection

> Find duplicate payments before they go out.

**Skill:** `duplicate-invoice-detection`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 75%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you detect duplicate or fraudulent invoices in AP from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "check for duplicate invoices"
- "find duplicate payments"
- "scan AP for duplicates"
- "detect invoice fraud"
- "have we paid this twice"

Not for:

- General fraud detection across all transaction types — use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a duplicate-suspect list with confidence ratings, supporting evidence, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Summary
- Flagged Pairs (and Groups)
- Detail
- Vendor Aliases to Merge
- Recovery Plan
- Rules Run
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
