# Month-End Close Checklist

**Skill:** `month-end-close-checklist`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 61%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you plan, run, or audit a monthly close against your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "run the month-end close"
- "month-end checklist"
- "close calendar"
- "organize the close for [month]"
- "close coordination"

Not for:

- An individual close task (a specific reconciliation, accrual, or JE) — use the corresponding specific skill.

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a comprehensive close checklist with owners, deadlines, dependencies, status tracking, a final tie-out workpaper, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Close Calendar
- Task Detail
- Open Items / Blockers
- Variance Tracker
- Materiality Application
- Final Tie-Out
- Lessons Learned
- Scoping
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
