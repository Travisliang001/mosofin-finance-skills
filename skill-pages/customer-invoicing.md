# Customer Invoicing

> Generate invoices with their AR, revenue and tax entries.

**Skill:** `customer-invoicing`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 66%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you generate customer invoices, draft invoice templates, validate invoice content, or build batched invoices from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "create an invoice for [customer]"
- "generate invoices from this list"
- "invoice template"
- "invoice with multi-currency"
- "invoice with progress billing"

Not for:

- Parsing vendor invoices — use [invoice-data-extractor](invoice-data-extractor.md).
- Subscription scheduling — use [recurring-transaction-builder](recurring-transaction-builder.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs invoice documents, the corresponding AR / revenue / tax JEs, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Invoice Register
- Line Detail
- GL Posting
- Validation Results
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
