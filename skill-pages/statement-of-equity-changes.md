# Statement of changes in equity: every movement, explained

> Roll every equity component forward, with each movement sourced.

**Skill:** `statement-of-equity-changes`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 58%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds the statement of changes in equity: tests every movement in an equity account for a legitimate source, identifies the components for the entity type, rolls each one forward from opening to closing balance, details each movement, rolls forward the share counts from the cap table you supply, ties out to the balance sheet and income statement, and adds the comparative period.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a statement of changes in equity or stockholders' equity, a retained earnings roll-forward, an equity roll-forward, or why equity moved.

Not for:

- The full statement set (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)) or for the year-end closing entries (use [closing-entries-and-trial-balance](closing-entries-and-trial-balance.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which period, and which comparative?* | Sets the opening and closing dates |
| *What type of entity: corporation, partnership, LLC, sole proprietor or nonprofit? Which framework?* | Decides the components |
| *What are the share counts by class, opening and each movement?* | The cap table is never in the books |
| *What explains any unusual movement in equity this period?* | A direct entry to equity needs a reason |
| *Is treasury stock at cost or par, and what were the dividend dates and per-share amounts?* | Both change the detail shown |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** at the opening and closing dates | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Ledger detail for every equity account** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Cap table** with movements | Required for the share roll-forward | XLSX |
| Net income and other comprehensive income for the period | Required for the tie-out | XLSX or PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the statement, its tie-outs and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Statement of Changes in Equity
- Movement Detail
- Share Count Roll-Forward
- Tie-Outs
- Dividends / Distributions Detail
- Equity Movement Source Test
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never invents a share count.** With no cap table, the share roll-forward is listed as not done.
- **Never lets an unexplained entry to equity pass as a normal movement.** It is listed with its amount and asked about.
- **Never treats a tie-out that holds by construction as evidence.**
