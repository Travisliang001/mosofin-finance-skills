# Tax projection: what the year is likely to cost

> Project the year's tax, payments on account and the effect of options.

**Skill:** `tax-planning-and-projections`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Projects a tax liability for the year and shows the effect of decisions before they are made: the income base from the books plus your forward view, the projected tax on rates you supply, estimated and quarterly payments, how much cash to set aside, a comparison of options, and sensitivity to the assumptions. Reads year-to-date results through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded figures.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a tax projection, estimated tax payments, how much to set aside for tax, or the tax effect of a decision they are considering.

Not for:

- The return itself (use [tax-return-prep-corporate](tax-return-prep-corporate.md) or [personal-tax-return-prep](personal-tax-return-prep.md)) or for the provision in the accounts (use [corporate-tax-provision-asc740](corporate-tax-provision-asc740.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Who is the taxpayer, in which jurisdiction, and for which tax year?* | Sets every rule that applies |
| *What are the current rates, thresholds and rules, with their source and year?* | Never built in, never carried forward |
| *What do you expect for the rest of the year?* | The forward half is not in the books |
| *What book-to-tax adjustments do you expect, and what is the safe-harbour rule for estimates?* | Taxable income is not book income |
| *Which decisions are you considering?* | Each is modelled as an option |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Year-to-date profit and loss** | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Rates and rules** with source and year | Required | Type it in the chat, or PDF |
| Prior year return or computation | Recommended | PDF |
| Forward assumptions | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

- Tax Projection Summary
- Income/Profit Projection
- Estimated Payments Schedule
- Tax Set-Aside Plan
- Planning Options Comparison
- Sensitivity
- Open Items / Professional Advice
- Threshold Map
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never recommends a course of action.** It shows each option's effect and names the difference between a timing benefit and a saving.
- **Never presents an arrangement with no commercial purpose as planning.**
