# Credit limit: how much to lend each customer

> Recommend a limit and terms from your own payment experience.

**Skill:** `credit-limit-and-customer-risk`  ·  **Area:** Forecasting, customers, credit, debt and fraud risk  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 53%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Assesses a business customer's creditworthiness and recommends a credit limit and terms: the customer's actual days-to-pay and its trend, current exposure and overdue share, disputes and write-off history, concentration, a score, the limit, the conditions, and the triggers for review. Leads with your own payment experience of the customer and states that the customer's financials, bureau report and references must come from outside the books.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to set or review a credit limit, assess a new customer's credit risk, decide payment terms, check customer concentration, or monitor credit exposure.

Not for:

- Chasing overdue invoices (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)) or for applying customer payments (use [cash-application](cash-application.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which customer, and which legal entity are you contracting with?* | The contracting entity often differs from the trading name |
| *What is being requested: terms in days, expected monthly volume, currency? Is there a limit today?* | The limit is sized against the request |
| *Can you supply the customer's financial statements, a credit bureau report or trade references?* | Evidence about the customer is never in your books |
| *What is your credit policy: tier ceilings, concentration limit, who approves?* | The recommendation must fit the policy |
| *Is the customer covered by credit insurance?* | An insurer's limit is an independent view of the same customer |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Invoice and payment history** for the customer, with dates | Required for a returning customer | XLSX or CSV |
| **Receivables ageing**, whole ledger | Required for the concentration check | XLSX or CSV |
| Customer's financial statements, bureau report, trade references | Recommended | PDF |
| Credit policy | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Recommends a limit and terms, sets nothing, and places no hold.

- Portfolio Summary
- Customer Reviews
- Watchlist
- Concentration Analysis
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never sets a limit, places a hold or changes terms in any system.** Every recommendation is a proposal for the credit function.
- **Never computes the customer's financial ratios from your own books.** They need the customer's statements.
- **Never invents a score, a bureau rating or a policy ceiling.** A missing input is listed as missing.
- **No sanctions screening happens here.** The output says so.
