# Vendor master: who you pay, and what you hold on them

> Vendor master audit and tax-form validation, identifiers masked.

**Skill:** `vendor-onboarding-and-w9-tin`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Audits the vendor master and supports onboarding of new vendors: measures completeness weighted by what each vendor is actually paid, detects changes to bank details, finds duplicate vendors and vendors that are also customers or related parties, then sets out the documents to collect and the tax-form validation for the payer's jurisdiction, such as a W-9 or W-8 in the US. Reads vendor and payment records through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded vendor list.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to audit the vendor master, onboard a vendor, check for missing tax forms before year-end, detect bank detail changes, or find duplicate vendors.

Not for:

- Matching invoices (use [three-way-match](three-way-match.md)) or for duplicate payments (use [duplicate-invoice-detection](duplicate-invoice-detection.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and is this an audit of the vendor master, onboarding of new vendors, or both?* | The two halves use different inputs |
| *What is the payer's jurisdiction?* | It determines every validation rule |
| *What does your onboarding policy require: documents, form expiry, a threshold for one-time payees?* | The standard gaps are measured against |
| *Who runs sanctions screening, on what service?* | Never done here |
| *Does the system record changes to vendor records, and who can edit vendors?* | Needed to detect bank detail changes |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Vendor list**: name, address, tax identifier status, terms | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Payments by vendor** for the year | Required to rank the gaps | XLSX or CSV |
| **Tax forms** for vendors being onboarded | Required for onboarding | PDF |
| Onboarding policy | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Changes no vendor record, and masks bank and tax identifiers wherever the analysis allows.

- Vendor Record
- Validation Results
- Screening Checklist
- Required Documents
- Vendor Master Import
- Master Audit
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never creates, edits or deactivates a vendor**, and never changes bank details.
- **Never displays a full bank account number or tax identifier where a masked one will do.**
- **Never performs or claims a sanctions screen.** It records who ran it and when.
- **Never treats a bank detail change as fraud.** It is reported for verification by call-back.
- **Gives no tax advice.** Withholding rules are your or their adviser's to confirm.
