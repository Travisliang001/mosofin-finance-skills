# Standard costing: is it performance, or a stale standard?

> Variances and the cost bridge; stale standard or real performance.

**Skill:** `standard-costing-and-variance-analysis`  ·  **Area:** Stock, costing, credit policy and audit preparation  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 66%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Analyses standard cost variances: checks whether the books run standard costing at all, whether the variance accounts clear, and whether a variance is one-sided every period, then computes price, usage, labour and overhead variances, builds the bridge from standard to actual cost, investigates root causes, and reviews the accounting treatment. Separates a stale standard from a real performance problem. Reads actual costs through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded standards and actuals.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for variance analysis on standard costs, purchase price variance, usage or efficiency variance, labour or overhead variance, a cost bridge, or whether standards need resetting.

Not for:

- Variance against the budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)) or for reconciling inventory to the ledger (use [inventory-to-gl-reconciliation](inventory-to-gl-reconciliation.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and which period?* | Sets every read |
| *Can you supply the standards: bill of materials, routings, standard prices and rates, each with its effective date?* | The standard half is never in the books, and its age matters as much as its value |
| *What were the production volumes, runs and scrap for the period?* | Usage and efficiency variances need quantities |
| *Is inventory valued at standard or actual, and what is the policy for capitalising variances?* | It governs the accounting treatment |
| *Which overhead rates are fixed and which variable, and on what basis are they absorbed?* | Splits the overhead variance |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Standards**: bill of materials, routings, prices and rates, with effective dates | Required for any variance | XLSX or CSV |
| **Actual costs** for the period: purchases, labour, overhead | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Production data**: volumes, runs, scrap | Required for usage and efficiency variances | XLSX or CSV |
| **Variance account history**, as long as available | Recommended, for the stale-standard test | XLSX or CSV |
| Valuation policy, overhead basis | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the variances, the bridge and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Variance Report by Product / Line / Plant
- Explanations and Root-Cause Hypotheses
- Recommended Operational Actions
- Proposed Accounting Entries and Close Approach
- Standards Health
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Any entry is a proposal.
- **Never invents a standard**, and never sets one from recent actuals.
- **Never forces the bridge to tie.** A residual is carried and named.
- **Never blames a person or a supplier for a variance** before the standard's age and the FX effect have been checked.
