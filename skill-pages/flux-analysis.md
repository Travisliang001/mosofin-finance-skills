# Flux analysis: which accounts moved, and why

> Month-on-month and year-on-year movements over two thresholds, each with a driver and evidence, and a cash proof.

**Skill:** `flux-analysis`  ·  **Area:** Flux analysis and customer deductions  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 78%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Flux analysis of the income statement and balance sheet: compares each account with the prior month and the same month last year, flags movements that pass both a percentage and an amount threshold, sorts each into a driver (volume, price, mix, timing, one-time, or error), and drafts the explanation and the follow-up for each account's owner. Treats an account that drops to nil as a possible missing posting, not a saving, and proves the cash movement from the balance sheet changes.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a flux, fluctuation or variance analysis, month-over-month or year-over-year review, or explanations of account movements for the close or a review.

Not for:

- Comparison against a budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms |
| *Which company, and which period?* | Sets every read |
| *Compare with what: the prior month, the same month last year, or both?* | Decides the comparison columns |
| *What thresholds? A percentage and an amount, for the income statement and the balance sheet* | Decides what gets flagged |
| *Who owns which accounts?* | Who each question goes to |
| *Anything you already know happened? A price change, a campaign, a one-off* | Explanations with evidence the books do not hold |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Income statement by account** for the period and each comparison period, or a monthly trend report | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Balance sheet by account** at the same dates | Recommended. Without it the cash proof and balance sheet flux are not run, and the output says so | XLSX or CSV |
| Ledger detail or journal list for the period | Recommended. It turns a movement into an explanation | XLSX or CSV |
| Owner list and known events | Optional | Any, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the flux schedule, explanations, proposed entries and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Flux schedule
- Explanations
- Cash proof
- Proposed entries
- Owner questions

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It posts no entry and sends no question. Every entry and every owner request is a proposal.
- **Both thresholds must trip** before a movement is flagged, and the thresholds are stated on the face of the output.
- **Never invents an explanation.** Each one cites its evidence or is marked "unexplained: asked".
