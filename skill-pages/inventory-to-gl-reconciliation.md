# Inventory to GL Reconciliation

> Reconcile inventory to the GL; shrinkage and cleanup.

**Skill:** `inventory-to-gl-reconciliation`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 59%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you reconcile inventory subledger or physical counts to the GL using your Mosofin workspace.

**In plain words:** the accounts say there is a certain value of stock. The stock system says there is a certain quantity. The warehouse, when someone actually walks round and counts it, says something else again. This finds out where the three disagree and why.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for any of these, or something like them.

Typical requests:

- "reconcile inventory to the GL"
- "physical count variance"
- "inventory subledger vs GL"
- "investigate inventory shrinkage"
- "tie inventory to balance sheet"
- "cycle count variance"

Not for:

- Inventory costing methodology — use [inventory-costing-fifo-lifo-wavg](inventory-costing-fifo-lifo-wavg.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for ledger detail and listings as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a reconciled inventory workpaper with variances explained, shrinkage analysis, the cleanup JEs, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Reconciliation Summary
- GL to Subledger Detail
- Physical Count Variances
- In-Transit and Cut-Off Items
- Write-Downs and Reserves
- GL Posting
- Ledger Checks
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
