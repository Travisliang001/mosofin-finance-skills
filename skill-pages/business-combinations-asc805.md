# Business combinations: the acquisition, recorded at fair value

> Purchase accounting from the valuer's fair values; goodwill and the entry.

**Skill:** `business-combinations-asc805`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 44%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for a business combination under the acquisition method of ASC 805 or IFRS 3: identifies the acquirer and the acquisition date, confirms it is a business and not an asset purchase, measures the consideration, records the identifiable assets and liabilities at the fair values a valuation specialist supplies, computes goodwill or a bargain purchase, builds the proposed acquisition entry, and tracks the measurement period and disclosures.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about purchase accounting, a purchase price allocation, goodwill on an acquisition, contingent consideration, or ASC 805.

Not for:

- The group consolidation afterwards (use [consolidation-and-eliminations](consolidation-and-eliminations.md)) or for the later impairment test (use [goodwill-and-impairment-testing](goodwill-and-impairment-testing.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Who bought whom, on exactly which date, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | Everything is measured on that date |
| *What was paid: cash, shares, contingent amounts?* | The consideration |
| *Do you have the valuation report for the assets, liabilities and intangibles?* | Fair values are never computed here |
| *Was less than 100% bought, or was an interest already held?* | Changes the goodwill computation |
| *Is there an earnout, and how is it classified?* | Decides later remeasurement |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchase agreement**, or a summary of terms | Required | PDF |
| **Valuation report** | Required | PDF |
| **Target's balance sheet** at the acquisition date | Required | XLSX |
| Deal cost invoices | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the allocation schedule and a proposed entry, and never supplies a fair value.

- Deal Summary & Acquirer/Date Determination
- Consideration Transferred
- Acquired Net Assets at Fair Value
- Identifiable Intangibles
- Goodwill Computation
- Acquisition Journal Entry
- Contingent Consideration
- Measurement Period Tracker
- Disclosures
- Open Items / Valuation Specialist

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a fair value**, and never uses a book value in its place without saying so.
- **Never decides whether the purchase is a business or a group of assets.** It sets out the test.
- **Never capitalises deal costs into the purchase price.**
- **Never finalises a provisional amount.** The measurement period stays open until you close it.
