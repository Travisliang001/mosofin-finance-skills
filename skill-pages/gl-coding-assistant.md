# GL Coding Assistant

> Code transactions using your own posting history.

**Skill:** `gl-coding-assistant`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 72%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you assign GL account codes, expense categories, or tax codes to transactions in your Mosofin workspace.

**In plain words:** every payment has to be filed under the right heading so the accounts mean something. Most of them are obvious once you know how this business files things. This works out which heading each one belongs under, says how sure it is, and separates the easy ones from the ones a person should look at.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for any of these, or something like them.

Typical requests:

- "what account should this go to"
- "code these expenses"
- "categorize these transactions"
- "suggest a GL account for [vendor/description]"

Not for:

- Building the chart of accounts itself — use [chart-of-accounts-designer](chart-of-accounts-designer.md).
- Posting actual journal entries — use [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for ledger detail and listings as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a coded transaction list with confidence ratings, reasoning, evidence, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
