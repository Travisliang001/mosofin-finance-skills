# Model builder: from your actuals to a working model

> A working, formula-driven model from your actuals and assumptions.

**Skill:** `model-builder-integrated-three-statement`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 61%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds a working, formula-driven financial model from historicals plus your assumptions: reads the historical income statement and balance sheet, maps accounts to model lines, shows what each driver has actually been, builds driver-based forecast schedules, links the three statements, and adds checks. Never generates the assumptions. Reads historicals through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded statements.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to build a working financial model quickly from their actuals, map historicals into a model, or set up drivers and checks.

Not for:

- The full build of debt, tax and circularity is the point (use [three-statement-financial-model](three-statement-financial-model.md)), for an operating budget (use [budget-builder](budget-builder.md)) or testing assumptions (use [scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis](scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and what is the model for: fundraising, the board, a lender or internal use?* | Sets the standard of evidence |
| *What horizon, and monthly, quarterly or annual?* | Sets the model's shape |
| *What are your assumptions for growth, margin, working capital, capex, debt, tax and headcount?* | Never generated |
| *Must the historicals match published or audited statements?* | The model's history should agree with them |
| *Do you have the debt, lease, equity and fixed asset schedules?* | Balances are in the books; schedules are not |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Historical income statements and balance sheets**, by period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Chart of accounts**, or an account-to-line mapping | Required | XLSX |
| **Assumptions** | Required for the forecast | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| Debt, lease, equity and fixed asset schedules | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the working model and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never fills in an assumption.** A missing one stays blank and is listed.
- **Never delivers a static export in place of a working model**, unless you ask for one.
- **Never builds on historicals that do not tie.** An unmapped account or a balance sheet that does not roll forward is fixed first.
- **Never uses an open period as the opening balance sheet without saying so.**
