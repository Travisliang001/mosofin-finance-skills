# Impairment testing: is the asset still worth its carrying amount?

> Scope, triggers and the impairment test on your projections.

**Skill:** `goodwill-and-impairment-testing`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 54%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Tests goodwill, indefinite-lived intangibles and long-lived assets for impairment under US GAAP or IFRS: scopes what must be tested from the balance sheet, identifies the unit of account, screens for triggering events, sets out the qualitative assessment, runs the quantitative test on the cash flows and discount rate you supply, and proposes the impairment entry and disclosures.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about a goodwill impairment test, triggering events, recoverable amount, value in use, or whether an asset is impaired.

Not for:

- The purchase accounting that created the goodwill (use [business-combinations-asc805](business-combinations-asc805.md)) or for inventory write-downs (use [inventory-obsolescence-and-reserve](inventory-obsolescence-and-reserve.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Carrying amounts** by unit | Required | XLSX |
| **Cash flow projections** | Required for the quantitative test | XLSX |
| **Discount rate** with its derivation | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| Market data for the market approach | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the test and a proposed entry, concludes nothing on whether an asset is impaired, and supplies no cash flow or discount rate.

- Test Scope
- Qualitative Assessment
- Reporting Unit / CGU Identification
- Fair Value / Recoverable Amount Computation
- Impairment Computation
- Journal Entries
- Reversal Assessment
- Disclosures
- Open Items / Valuation Specialist
- Triggering Event Monitoring

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never fabricates cash flows or a discount rate.**
- **Never concludes that an asset is or is not impaired.** It shows the comparison and the sensitivity.
- **Never reverses an impairment under US GAAP**, and never reverses goodwill impairment under either framework.
- **Never allocates goodwill to units for you.**
