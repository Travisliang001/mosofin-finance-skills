# Journal Entry Review

> Risk-score a journal entry population for anomalies.

**Skill:** `journal-entry-review`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 67%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you review journal entries for risk, propriety, or anomalies from your books — a control/audit lens on JEs.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "review the journal entries"
- "JE testing"
- "analyze journal entries for risk"
- "find unusual journal entries"
- "manual journal entry review"
- "journal entry controls"
- "test JEs for fraud indicators"

Not for:

- Building entries — use [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md).
- Full forensic investigation — use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a risk-scored journal entry review with flagged anomalies, characteristics tested, follow-up items, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Population Profile
- Risk Criteria & Screening
- Flagged Entries (Risk-Scored)
- Detailed Review
- Patterns & Trends
- Follow-Up & Escalation
- Control Observations
- Criteria Not Run
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
