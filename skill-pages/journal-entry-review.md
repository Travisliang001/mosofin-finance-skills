# Journal Entry Review

> Risk-score a journal entry population for anomalies.

**Skill:** `journal-entry-review`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 67%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you review journal entries for risk, propriety, or anomalies from your books — a control/audit lens on JEs.

**In plain words:** most accounting entries come from an invoice, a payment, a payroll run. A few are typed in by hand, and those are the ones that bypass the normal checks. This looks through them for the patterns that tend to accompany mistakes or manipulation, and puts the ones worth a closer look at the top.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for any of these, or something like them.

Typical requests:

- "review the journal entries"
- "JE testing"
- "analyze journal entries for risk"
- "find unusual journal entries"
- "manual journal entry review"
- "journal entry controls"
- "test JEs for fraud indicators"

Not for:

- Building entries — use [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md).
- Full forensic investigation — use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for ledger detail and listings as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a risk-scored journal entry review with flagged anomalies, characteristics tested, follow-up items, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Population Profile
- Risk Criteria & Screening
- Flagged Entries
- Detailed Review
- Patterns & Trends
- Follow-Up & Escalation
- Control Observations
- Criteria Not Run
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
