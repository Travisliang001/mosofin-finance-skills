# Transfer pricing: showing that group prices are arm's length

> Map intercompany flows and assemble the documentation draft.

**Skill:** `transfer-pricing-documentation`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports transfer pricing documentation for a group: maps the intercompany transactions from the books, records the functional analysis you supply and checks it against where costs and people actually sit, sets out method selection and the tested party, lays out benchmarking against comparables you provide, flags a loss-making tested party, and assembles the documentation and the list of intercompany agreements. Reads intercompany flows through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about transfer pricing documentation, a local file or master file, intercompany pricing support, or a benchmarking study outline.

Not for:

- Matching intercompany balances (use [intercompany-reconciliation](intercompany-reconciliation.md)) or consolidation (use [consolidation-and-eliminations](consolidation-and-eliminations.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which group, which year, and which entities and jurisdictions?* | Bounds the study |
| *What are the documentation requirements in each jurisdiction, with source?* | Thresholds and deadlines are never built in |
| *What does each entity do: its functions, assets and risks?* | The heart of the study |
| *Which intercompany agreements exist?* | Documents, not ledger entries |
| *Do you have comparable data and last year's documentation?* | Benchmarking and consistency |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Group structure** | Required | PDF |
| **Intercompany transactions** by type and pair | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Intercompany agreements** | Required | PDF |
| Comparable data and prior documentation | Recommended | XLSX or PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Transaction Inventory
- Functional Analysis Matrix
- Method Selection
- Benchmarking
- Economic Analysis
- Documentation Compliance
- Open Items / Specialist Review
- Risk Indicators

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies comparable data, a range or a local rule.** Each comes from you, with its source.
- **Never selects the method or concludes that a price is arm's length.**
- **Never changes an intercompany price or proposes a year-end adjustment as settled.**
