# Fraud detection: patterns, not people

> Red-flag tests across the population; patterns, never accusations.

**Skill:** `fraud-detection-and-forensics`  ·  **Area:** Forecasting, customers, credit, debt and fraud risk  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Screens accounting records for fraud indicators and structures an investigation: a fraud risk assessment, analytical red-flag tests across the whole transaction population, a framework for following up flagged items, loss measurement, and a report with control fixes. Reports patterns with counts and amounts and never concludes that a person committed fraud.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a fraud risk assessment, red-flag or anomaly testing, a check for ghost vendors or duplicate payments, a Benford's Law test, forensic accounting support, or help structuring an investigation.

Not for:

- Duplicate payments alone (use [duplicate-invoice-detection](duplicate-invoice-detection.md)) or for routine journal entry review (use [journal-entry-review](journal-entry-review.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What is this: a risk assessment, a screen for red flags, or support for an investigation?* | Decides everything that follows |
| *Is there a specific allegation, and is legal counsel engaged?* | An investigation is led by counsel and forensic professionals, and may be privileged |
| *Who may see the output, and who approves access to the data?* | The output names people, and an approval prompt can tip someone off |
| *Which company, which period, and which area of concern if any?* | Focuses the tests |
| *What are the approval limits and the relevant policies?* | The just-under-the-limit test needs the limit |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **General ledger detail** or the journal entry listing for the period, with user and date posted | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Payments and bills** for the period | Required for the vendor tests | XLSX or CSV |
| **Vendor list** and **employee list**, with addresses and bank details where held | Required for the ghost-vendor and conflict tests | XLSX or CSV |
| Approval limits and policies | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |
| Bank statements, payroll register | Optional. Without them those tests are listed as not run | PDF or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces indicators, a workplan and a coverage sheet, accuses no one, and changes no record.

- Engagement Scope & Boundaries
- Fraud Risk Assessment
- Analytical Red Flags
- Flagged Items for Investigation
- Fund Tracing / Timeline
- Loss Quantification
- Control Failures & Remediation
- Escalation & Professional Involvement
- Tests Not Run
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system**, and never suggests that anyone change a record while a concern is open.
- **Never concludes that a person committed fraud**, and never infers motive from the data. Patterns are reported with counts and amounts; people conclude.
- **Never lets a test that could not run read as a clean result.**
- **Never saves findings, flagged items or names** for a later run.
- **Where a specific allegation exists, stops and escalates before analysing.**
