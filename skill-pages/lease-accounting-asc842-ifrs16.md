# Leases: the right to use an asset, and the debt to pay for it

> Right-of-use asset, lease liability, schedules; finds unrecorded leases.

**Skill:** `lease-accounting-asc842-ifrs16`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 55%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for leases under ASC 842 or IFRS 16: confirms there is a lease, sets the term and payments, takes the discount rate from you, classifies the lease where the framework requires it, measures the right-of-use asset and lease liability, builds the amortisation schedules, and proposes the periodic entries, reassessments and disclosures. Also searches the books for recurring rent-like payments with no lease on the balance sheet.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about lease accounting, a right-of-use asset, a lease liability, operating against finance leases, a lease amortisation schedule, a lease modification, or ASC 842 or IFRS 16.

Not for:

- Owned assets (use [fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation](fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation.md)) or for prepaid rent alone (use [prepaid-amortization-schedule](prepaid-amortization-schedule.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and are you the lessee or the lessor?* | This workflow centres on the lessee |
| *ASC 842 or IFRS 16?* | Decides whether classification happens at all |
| *Can you supply the lease agreements: term, payments, escalations, options, start date?* | The core input |
| *What discount rate applies to each lease, and how was it derived?* | It drives both balances |
| *Which expedients are elected, is there a complete lease register, and was any lease modified?* | Short-term and low-value elections remove leases from the balance sheet |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Lease agreements**, or the key terms of each | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| **Discount rate** per lease, with its derivation | Required | Type it in the chat |
| **Lease balances and payments** in the ledger | Recommended, to tie the schedule to the books | XLSX or CSV |
| Existing lease register and schedules | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedules and proposed entries, and posts nothing.

- Lease Register & Classification
- Initial Measurement
- Lease Liability Schedule
- ROU Asset Schedule
- Journal Entries
- Modifications / Reassessments
- Disclosures
- Completeness
- Tie-Out
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never fabricates a discount rate.** It asks for the rate and how it was derived.
- **Never classifies a lease under IFRS 16**, where a lessee has one model, and never skips classification under ASC 842.
- **Never decides that an option is reasonably certain to be exercised.** That judgment is your, and the schedule states it.
