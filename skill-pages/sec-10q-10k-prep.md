# 10-Q and 10-K preparation: from close to filing

> Close-to-file calendar, tie-outs and the disclosure checklist.

**Skill:** `sec-10q-10k-prep`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 37%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Organises the preparation of a 10-Q or 10-K: builds the calendar from close to filing, sets up the tie-out of every number in the draft to its source, checks the comparatives against what was actually filed, runs the disclosure checklist, and lays out the drafting, review and sign-off workflow.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to prepare a 10-Q or 10-K, plan an SEC filing calendar, tie out a filing draft, check comparatives against a prior filing, or run a disclosure checklist for an SEC filer.

Not for:

- The close itself (use [quarter-end-close](quarter-end-close.md) or [year-end-close](year-end-close.md)), the notes (use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md)) or the narrative (use [management-discussion-and-analysis](management-discussion-and-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which form, and which period?* | A 10-K is not a fourth 10-Q |
| *What is the filer status, and what are the filing and earnings dates?* | Sets every deadline |
| *Can you supply the prior filings and the current draft?* | The filed document is the authority |
| *What significant events happened in the period?* | Each brings disclosures |
| *Who reviews what, does XBRL apply, and which non-GAAP measures are used?* | The reviewer matrix and consistency of measures |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** for the period and comparatives | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Prior filings** | Required for the comparative check | PDF |
| **Current draft** | Required for the tie-out | PDF or DOCX |
| Reviewer matrix, calendar, non-GAAP definitions | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the calendar, tie-outs and checklist, and files nothing.

- Reporting Calendar with Owners
- Tie-Out Checklist
- Disclosure Checklist
- Drafting / Review Workflow SOP
- Risk List and Mitigation Plan
- Ledger Control Tests
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never files, submits or signs anything**, and never gives a legal view on a disclosure.
- **Never takes a comparative from the books without checking it against the prior filing.**
- **Never sets a deadline without the filer status.** It depends on public float, which is not in the books.
- **Never authors a forward-looking statement or a non-GAAP definition.**
