# Payment processor ↔ books reconciliation

> Tie Stripe, Square or PayPal payouts to the books; catch revenue recorded net of fees.

**Skill:** `merchant-and-payment-processor-rec`  ·  **Area:** Payment processors  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 64%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Reconciles a payment processor (Stripe, Square, PayPal, Braintree, Adyen, Shopify Payments) to the general ledger: gross sales, fees, refunds, chargebacks and payouts, with the clearing account rolled forward and aged. Tests the most common error, revenue recorded net of processor fees.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to reconcile Stripe or Square to the books, process processor settlements, explain why payouts do not match revenue, check merchant fees, or upload a processor export.

Not for:

- Bank reconciliation alone (use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)) or applying customer payments (use [cash-application](cash-application.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which processor? Stripe, Square, PayPal or another* | Each has its own clearing account and export |
| *Which period? Start and end date.* | Sets every read and every cut-off |
| *Are sales recorded one by one, or as one entry per payout?* | The reconciliation is built differently for each, and the workpaper must say which |
| *Does the processor hold a reserve or delay payouts?* | Held money is your asset and is shown separately |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Processor **payout / settlement** report for the period | Required | CSV export from the processor dashboard |
| Processor **transactions or balance** report (charges, fees, refunds, disputes) | Required | CSV export |
| Your books: **GL detail** for the clearing, revenue and fee accounts, or a **trial balance** plus GL export | Required | CSV or XLSX |
| Clearing account name, base currency and country | Required | Type it in the chat |
| **Bank statement** for the period | **Required** (the only proof the payouts actually arrived. Without it the skill still runs, ties payouts to the deposits recorded in your books instead, and **says so in the output)** | PDF or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces a reconciliation, proposed entries and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Reconciliation Summary
- Transaction Detail
- Payout Tie-Out
- Exceptions
- GL Posting
- Presentation Checks

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry it produces is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never estimates a figure** that should come from your books or the processor. A gap is reported as a gap.
