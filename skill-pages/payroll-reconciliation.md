# Payroll Reconciliation

> Tie the payroll register to GL, bank and filings.

**Skill:** `payroll-reconciliation`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 61%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you reconcile payroll against your books — payroll register to GL, payroll to bank, payroll liabilities to amounts remitted, or quarter/year-end payroll tie-outs.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "reconcile payroll"
- "payroll register to GL"
- "does payroll tie to the bank"
- "reconcile payroll liabilities"
- "year-end payroll reconciliation"
- "reconcile wages to the tax filings"

Not for:

- Building the payroll JE — use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md).
- Clearing-account zeroing only — use [payroll-clearing-reconciliation](payroll-clearing-reconciliation.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a payroll reconciliation workpaper tying register, GL, bank, and filings with flagged exceptions and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Reconciliation Summary
- Register-to-GL Bridge
- Net Pay to Bank
- Liability Roll-Forward
- Filing Reconciliation
- Taxable Wage Base Reconciliation
- Exceptions & Corrections
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
