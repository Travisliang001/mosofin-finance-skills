# Bank fees and interest: what the bank costs per transaction

> Categorise bank charges, cost per transaction, sized savings.

**Skill:** `bank-fee-and-interest-analysis`  ·  **Area:** Analysis and reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 63%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Analyses bank fees and interest: sorts every charge into a category, ties it to the general ledger, finds miscoded and missing postings, computes the cost per wire, payment and deposit against real transaction volumes, shows the trend, and sizes each way to bring the cost down. States plainly when it saw only the fees recorded in the books and not the bank statement.

## When to use it

Use it when you say bank fees are too high, asks to analyse bank charges, wire or merchant fees, tie out interest income, check fee coding, or prepare to renegotiate banking services.

Not for:

- Reconciling the bank account (use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)) or for payment processor payouts (use [merchant-and-payment-processor-rec](merchant-and-payment-processor-rec.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and which period? Thirteen months or more shows the year-on-year trend.* | Sets every read |
| *Can you supply the bank statements, or the bank's account analysis statement?* | The only evidence of fees that never reached the books |
| *Which banking products do you use? Accounts, wires, ACH, lockbox, merchant services* | A product with no fees recorded against it is a finding |
| *Do you have the bank's fee schedule or rate sheet?* | Compares what was paid with what was promised |
| *Does the bank apply an earnings credit rate?* | It changes the true cost of the account |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Ledger detail** for the bank fee, interest income, interest expense and cash accounts | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Bank statements**, or the **account analysis statement**, for the period | **Required to find fees missing from the books.** Without them the skill still runs on posted fees and says so | PDF or CSV |
| Payment, deposit and receipt listings for the period | Recommended. They supply the transaction counts behind each unit rate | XLSX or CSV |
| Fee schedule, product list, earnings credit rate | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the analysis, proposed entries and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Categorized fee schedule
- Tie-out to GL (matched vs unmatched)
- Root-cause list for mismatches
- Cost reduction recommendations
- Proposed JEs (if needed)

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It recodes nothing, closes no account and contacts no bank. Every entry is a proposal.
- **Never calls a total from the books "total bank fees"** unless the statements were supplied.
- **Never estimates a fee or a rate.** A recommendation carries its saving, the count it rests on and the assumption behind it.
