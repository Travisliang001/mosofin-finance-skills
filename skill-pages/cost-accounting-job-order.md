# Job costing: what each job really cost

> Cost and margin by job; overhead applied; untagged cost shown apart.

**Skill:** `cost-accounting-job-order`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 65%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Costs work by job, project or order: tests how much direct cost in the books carries no job tag, defines the job cost structure, sets overhead pools and rates, accumulates materials and labour by job, applies overhead, rolls work in progress forward, and treats under- or over-applied overhead, with margin by job. Reads job-tagged costs through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded job cost data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for job costing, project or job profitability, work in progress by job, overhead allocation to jobs, or an overhead rate.

Not for:

- Continuous production (use [cost-accounting-process-costing](cost-accounting-process-costing.md)), for standard cost variances (use [standard-costing-and-variance-analysis](standard-costing-and-variance-analysis.md)) or for coding a single transaction (use [gl-coding-assistant](gl-coding-assistant.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period?* | Sets every read |
| *How are jobs tagged: customer, class, project or department?* | This is how costs are read by job |
| *Which jobs are open and which are closed?* | Decides what stays in work in progress |
| *Which accounts form the overhead pool, what is the allocation base, and is the rate predetermined?* | A policy decision |
| *What are the labour rates, and how do jobs earn revenue: time and materials, fixed fee, milestones?* | Hours may be in the books; rates usually are not |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Job list** with status | Required | XLSX |
| **Costs by job**: materials and labour hours, with dates | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Overhead accounts and the budget** for a predetermined rate | Required to apply overhead | XLSX |
| Labour rates and revenue by job | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces job cost sheets, the roll-forward and proposed entries, and posts nothing.

- Job cost ledger model
- Overhead rate and pool documentation
- WIP rollforward by job
- Job profitability dashboard definitions

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never spreads untagged cost across jobs to make them add up.** It is reported as untagged.
- **Never invents a labour rate or an overhead rate.**
- **Never reports job margin without saying how much cost was left out of it.**
