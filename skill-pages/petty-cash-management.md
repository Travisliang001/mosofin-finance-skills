# Petty cash: cash plus receipts equals the float

> Cash plus vouchers against the float; replenishment and differences.

**Skill:** `petty-cash-management`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 57%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Reconciles a petty cash float: tests that cash counted plus vouchers equals the authorised float, reads the vouchers, codes each to a ledger account, computes the replenishment, and reports any shortage or overage, with the proposed entry. Checks that the petty cash balance in the books equals the authorised float and only moves on replenishment.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to reconcile petty cash, replenish a float, process petty cash vouchers, or explain a petty cash shortage.

Not for:

- A bank account (use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md)) or for employee expense claims (use [expense-report-processor](expense-report-processor.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which float, and which period?* | Sets the scope |
| *What is the authorised float amount?* | The figure everything must equal |
| *What was the cash count, who counted it, and when?* | Nothing else can supply it |
| *Can you supply the vouchers and receipts for the period?* | The other half of the equation |
| *Who is the custodian, and what are the receipt threshold and the shortage tolerance?* | Policy, not data |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash count**, with date and counter | Required | Type it in the chat |
| **Vouchers and receipts** | Required | PDF, images, or a listing |
| **Petty cash account detail** for the period | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the reconciliation and a proposed entry, and never stands in for the physical count.

- Reconciliation Summary
- Voucher Detail
- Exceptions
- GL Posting Inputs
- Fund Health
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never accepts a book balance in place of a physical count.**
- **Never assumes a receipt threshold or a shortage tolerance.**
- **Never concludes theft.** A shortage is reported with its amount, the custodian and the count date.
