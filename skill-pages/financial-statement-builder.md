# Financial Statement Builder

> Produce the statement set with its tie-outs.

**Skill:** `financial-statement-builder`  ·  **Area:** Everyday payables, receivables and close  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you build a complete set of financial statements from your books — Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Statement of Cash Flows, with comparatives.

**In plain words:** the accounting system holds a long list of account balances. This turns that list into the three statements a bank, an investor or an auditor expects to see — in the order, the groupings and the wording their framework requires — and then proves the statements agree with each other.

## When to use it

Typical requests, or anything like them:

- "build financial statements from this TB"
- "produce a P&L and balance sheet"
- "monthly financials package"
- "GAAP financial statements"
- "IFRS financial statements"
- "investor financials"

Not for:

- Just the cash flow statement — use [cash-flow-statement-indirect-method](cash-flow-statement-indirect-method.md).
- Footnotes — use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for the ledger detail and listings the work needs as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a complete statement package with comparatives, classifications, tie-outs, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Trial Balance
- Balance Sheet
- Income Statement
- Statement of Comprehensive Income
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Statement of Changes in Equity
- Cross-Statement Tie-Outs
- Reclassifications and Restated Prior Period

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
