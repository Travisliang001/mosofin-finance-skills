# Quarter-end close: the monthly close, plus the quarter's own work

> The monthly close plus the quarter's own tasks, and late-posting checks.

**Skill:** `quarter-end-close`  ·  **Area:** Quarter-end and year-end close, planning and foreign currency  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 53%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Runs the quarter-end close on top of the monthly close: the quarter-specific tasks such as the tax provision, estimates review, subsequent events, going-concern facts and covenant tests, the reports and filings in scope, coordination with an interim reviewer, the year-to-date roll-forward, and a check that nothing was posted into a closed quarter. Marks which tasks the books can evidence and which need a person.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to run or plan a quarter-end close, prepare quarterly reporting, a board pack or a lender certificate, or check a closed quarter for late postings.

Not for:

- The monthly close (use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md)) or computing loan covenants in depth (use [debt-and-covenant-tracking](debt-and-covenant-tracking.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and which quarter, on what fiscal calendar?* | A quarter is not always three calendar months |
| *Which reports are due this quarter: a filing, a board pack, a lender certificate, a management report?* | Each needs different work |
| *What do the covenants say, in the agreement's own definitions?* | Book figures are not covenant figures |
| *Is an auditor performing an interim review, and what is materiality for the quarter?* | Decides whether the reviewer steps apply |
| *Are segments tracked in the books, and did anything unusual happen in the quarter?* | Segment work and one-off events need to be known up front |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance at the quarter end**, and at the prior quarter end and prior year end | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **General ledger detail** for the quarter | Required for the late-posting and cut-off tests | XLSX or CSV |
| Covenant definitions, reporting obligations | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |
| Schedules held outside the books: leases, equity awards, tax | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the checklist, the schedules and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Quarter Close Calendar
- Quarter-Specific Procedures
- Estimates Review
- Roll-Forward Summary
- Subsequent Events Log
- Covenant Compliance
- Filing / Report Checklist
- Final Tie-Out and Sign-Off
- Ledger Diagnostics

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Entries are proposals.
- **Never drops a task it cannot perform.** It stays, marked for a person.
- **Never computes a covenant on book figures as if they were the agreement's definitions.**
- **Never authors a forward-looking statement or a going-concern conclusion.** It presents the facts; management assesses and signs.
