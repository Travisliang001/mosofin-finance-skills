# QuickBooks receivables: ageing and a collections plan

> Receivables ageing and collections plan for a QuickBooks Online file.

**Skill:** `quickbooks-ar-aging-collections`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 64%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Produces a receivables ageing and a collections plan from a QuickBooks Online company file: pulls the ageing summary and detail before asking you for anything, normalises the invoice table, reconciles summary to detail, asks only for what is materially missing, ranks customers into action bands, and drafts collection messages for a person to send. Reads QuickBooks through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded QuickBooks ageing exports.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for QuickBooks receivables ageing, who owes money in QuickBooks, a collections priority list from QuickBooks, or draft reminder emails for overdue QuickBooks invoices.

Not for:

- The books are not in QuickBooks (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)) or applying payments (use [cash-application](cash-application.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which QuickBooks company, and as at what date?* | Sets every read |
| *Is the request the whole ledger, or particular customers?* | Sets the population |
| *Are any invoices disputed, on hold, or covered by a promise to pay?* | Not accounting fields |
| *What are your collection stages and tone, and who signs the messages?* | The drafts follow your practice |
| *Are there customers who must not be contacted?* | Relationships the books cannot show |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **A/R Aging Summary** export from QuickBooks | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **A/R Aging Detail** export, same date | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Customer contact list | Recommended, for the drafts | XLSX or CSV |
| Disputes, holds and promises to pay | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

A workpaper you can keep, as an Excel file if you want one or as tables in the chat, with every figure traced to where it came from. Anything that would change the books is a proposal for a person to approve.

Writes nothing to QuickBooks and sends no message.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes to QuickBooks**, and never sends, schedules or logs a message to a customer.
- **Never forces the summary to agree with the detail.** A difference is shown and named.
- **Never chases a customer before checking for unapplied payments and credits.**
