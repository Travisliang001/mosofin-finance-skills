# AML and KYC: knowing who you are dealing with

> AML and KYC framework documents and review lists for the responsible officer.

**Skill:** `aml-kyc-procedures`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 48%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports the design and documentation of an anti-money-laundering and know-your-customer programme: frames the regulatory context you state, sets up the risk-based approach, customer due diligence and enhanced due diligence, records screening done on your own tool, describes ongoing monitoring of transactions in the books, and sets out escalation to the responsible officer. Reads transaction activity through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded customer and transaction data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about AML or KYC procedures, customer due diligence, risk rating customers, beneficial ownership, transaction monitoring rules, or documenting an AML programme.

Not for:

- Fraud red-flag testing (use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md)) or vendor tax forms (use [vendor-onboarding-and-w9-tin](vendor-onboarding-and-w9-tin.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, and under which jurisdictions and regulatory regime?* | Never inferred from the country on record |
| *What products or services are offered?* | Decides whether the entity is covered at all |
| *Who is the responsible officer, by name and role?* | Every escalation goes to a person |
| *Is there an existing AML policy, and which screening tool do you use?* | Build on it, do not replace it |
| *Is monitoring in scope, and for which period?* | Sets the transaction read |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing AML policy** | Recommended | PDF |
| **Customer list** with risk attributes | Required for risk rating | XLSX |
| **Transaction activity** for the period | Required for monitoring | XLSX or CSV |
| Screening results from your tool | Required to record screening | PDF or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces framework documents and review lists for the responsible officer, is not legal or compliance advice, screens no one and files nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Risk Assessment Model
- CDD/EDD Checklist
- Customer Risk Rating
- Screening Log
- Transaction Monitoring
- Escalation Register
- Regulatory Confirmations Needed

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never performs sanctions, PEP or adverse-media screening.** It records the tool used, the date and the result supplied.
- **Never decides that activity is suspicious, and never drafts a filing as final.** It refers to the responsible officer by name.
- **Never tips off.** Nothing in the output is written for the customer, and nothing suggests contacting them.
- **Never states a legal threshold or requirement from memory as settled.** You confirm the regime.
- **Never saves identity or screening data.**
