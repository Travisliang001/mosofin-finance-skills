# IPO readiness: getting finance ready to be public

> Finance roadmap to public-company readiness, with the close measured.

**Skill:** `ipo-readiness-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 41%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds an IPO readiness roadmap for the finance function: measures how long the close actually takes and how much of it rests on manual journals, assesses gaps against public-company requirements, plans close acceleration, lists the accounting policy memos needed, and sets out equity and earnings-per-share readiness, internal control readiness and the reporting process.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about getting finance ready for an IPO, a public-company readiness assessment, shortening the close, or which years need audits before listing.

Not for:

- Preparing a filing (use [sec-10q-10k-prep](sec-10q-10k-prep.md)) or for testing controls (use [sox-controls-design-and-testing](sox-controls-design-and-testing.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, what is the target timeline, and which entity will be the registrant?* | Sets the sequence |
| *Is a reorganisation planned before the IPO?* | The structure being assessed may change |
| *Which years must be audited, and what is their status?* | Usually the binding constraint |
| *What is the equity history: cap table, grants, valuations?* | The whole equity section rests on it |
| *What is the state of the control environment, and which systems are in use?* | Sets the control workstream |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Close calendar and task list** | Required | XLSX |
| **Financial statements** for the years to be presented | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| **Cap table and grant history** | Required for the equity section | XLSX |
| Control assessment and systems list | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the roadmap and a gap list, and is not legal or securities advice.

- Readiness Roadmap with Timeline
- Measured Baseline
- Gap Assessment by Workstream
- Workstream Deliverables and Owners
- Close Calendar + RACI
- Memo Library Index
- SOX Readiness Plan and Evidence Framework
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Not legal, securities or listing advice.** Requirements are confirmed with counsel and the auditors.
- **Never asserts close speed.** It measures it from posting dates.
- **Never states a filing requirement from memory as settled.**
- **Never assesses a group structure that is about to change without saying so.**
