# Nonprofit accounting: who restricted what, and was it honoured

> Net assets by restriction, releases and the functional expense statement.

**Skill:** `nonprofit-fund-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Handles accounting for a not-for-profit under ASC 958 or a local equivalent: classifies net assets by donor restriction, recognises contributions and grants, tracks and releases restrictions, accounts for endowments and in-kind gifts, allocates expenses by function, and builds the nonprofit statement set with the statement of functional expenses.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about nonprofit or charity accounting, net assets with and without donor restrictions, releasing restrictions, functional expense allocation, endowments, or nonprofit financial statements.

Not for:

- Tracking one grant's budget and allowable costs (use [grant-accounting-nonprofit-restricted-funds](grant-accounting-nonprofit-restricted-funds.md)) or for a government body (use [government-fund-accounting-gasb](government-fund-accounting-gasb.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which organisation and period, and which framework?* | Sets the scope |
| *What are the programmes, and do the classes in the books represent programmes or functions?* | Decides whether the functional matrix can be read |
| *For each significant contribution: is it restricted, by purpose or by time, and is it conditional?* | Not in the ledger |
| *How are shared costs split between programmes, management and fundraising?* | Time studies and floor area are not in the books |
| *Is there an endowment, and were there gifts in kind?* | Each has its own rules |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** at the opening and closing dates | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Contribution records**: donor, amount, date, restriction | Required | XLSX |
| **Expenses by natural class and by programme** | Required for the functional statement | XLSX or CSV |
| Grant agreements and the allocation basis | Recommended | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the statements, schedules and proposed entries, and posts nothing.

- Statement of Financial Position
- Statement of Activities
- Statement of Functional Expenses
- Statement of Cash Flows
- Net Assets Detail and Roll-Forward
- Contribution & Grant Register
- Endowment Schedule
- Functional Allocation Methodology
- Disclosure Data
- Missing Releases

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never decides that a gift is restricted, conditional or neither.** It asks, gift by gift where it matters.
- **Never invents an allocation basis for shared costs.**
- **Never releases a restriction without evidence that it was met.**
