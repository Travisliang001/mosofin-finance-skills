# Inventory reserve: stock that will not sell for what it cost

> Slow and obsolete stock, the value test, and the reserve.

**Skill:** `inventory-obsolescence-and-reserve`  ·  **Area:** Stock, costing, credit policy and audit preparation  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 67%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Finds excess, slow-moving and obsolete inventory and sizes the reserve: movement by item, days on hand, every item with no sales or usage in a set number of days, the lower of cost and net realisable value test, the reserve by ageing bucket, a roll-forward tied to the ledger, and the proposed entry. Derives reserve rates from what past slow stock actually realised where the history allows.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about obsolete or slow-moving stock, an inventory reserve or write-down, excess inventory, net realisable value, or dead stock.

Not for:

- Reconciling inventory to the ledger (use [inventory-to-gl-reconciliation](inventory-to-gl-reconciliation.md)) or for freeing cash from stock levels (use [working-capital-optimization](working-capital-optimization.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Stock listing**: item, quantity, cost, location, and lot or date where held | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Movement history**: sales and usage by item by month, 12 months or more | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Recent selling prices** by item | Required for the net realisable value test | XLSX or CSV |
| **Reserve account detail** for the period | Required for the roll-forward | XLSX or CSV |
| Policy, condition, special items, forecast | C required, the rest recommended | Type it in the chat, or XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** The reserve entry is a proposal.
- **Never treats no movement as obsolete on its own.** Seasonal, end-of-life and customer-specific stock are checked first.
- **Never reserves the same item twice**, once as slow-moving and again as below cost.
- **Never assumes a reserve rate without saying so.** The policy is your; a derived rate is shown as a proposal.
