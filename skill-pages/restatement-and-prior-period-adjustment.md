# Restatement: correcting an error in issued accounts

> Quantify a prior-period error and lay out its correction.

**Skill:** `restatement-and-prior-period-adjustment`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Handles the correction of an error in previously issued financial statements: sweeps the books for entries posted into closed periods, sets out whether the item is an error or a change in estimate, quantifies it across every affected period, lays out the materiality assessment for management and the auditors, works through the correction method, computes the corrections, and prepares the disclosures and control follow-up.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about a prior period error, a restatement, a prior period adjustment, a revision, or entries posted to a closed year.

Not for:

- A change in estimate handled prospectively (use [accruals-and-deferrals](accruals-and-deferrals.md)) or for routine closing entries (use [closing-entries-and-trial-balance](closing-entries-and-trial-balance.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and what is the error and how was it found?* | How it was found feeds the root cause |
| *Can you supply the issued statements for each affected period?* | The authority for what was reported |
| *ASC 250 or IAS 8, and is the entity public or private?* | Decides the method and the regulatory consequences |
| *What was known at the time?* | Separates an error from a change in estimate |
| *What materiality benchmarks do you and your auditors use?* | The assessment is measured against them |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Issued financial statements** for each affected period | Required | PDF |
| **Ledger detail** for the accounts affected, all periods | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Materiality benchmarks | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the quantification, proposed corrections and disclosures, and concludes on no materiality.

- Error Assessment
- Quantification
- Materiality Analysis
- Correction Method Determination
- Correction Entries
- Restated Statements
- Disclosure Draft
- Control / Governance / Regulatory
- Prior-Period Posting Sweep
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never concludes that an error is material or immaterial.** It sets out the quantitative and qualitative factors.
- **Never decides between a full restatement and a revision.**
- **Never treats a change in estimate as an error, or the reverse, on its own judgment.**
- **Never takes the issued figures from the ledger.** They come from the issued statements.
