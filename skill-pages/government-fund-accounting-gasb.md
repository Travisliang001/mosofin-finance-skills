# Government fund accounting: many pots, reported twice

> Fund statements, government-wide statements and the reconciliation.

**Skill:** `government-fund-accounting-gasb`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 46%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Handles accounting for state and local governments and similar public bodies under GASB or IPSAS: establishes the basis the books are kept on and whether funds are a dimension, identifies the fund structure, applies the right measurement basis to each fund type, builds the fund statements with fund balance classifications, builds the government-wide statements and the reconciliation between the two, and determines major funds.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about governmental fund accounting, modified accrual, fund balance classification, government-wide statements, major fund determination, or GASB or IPSAS reporting.

Not for:

- A charity (use [nonprofit-fund-accounting](nonprofit-fund-accounting.md)) or a company's statements (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, which fiscal year, and GASB, IPSAS or another framework?* | Sets the scope |
| *On which basis are the books kept: cash, modified accrual or full accrual?* | Everything else is read through it |
| *Which funds are in use, of which types, and how are they tagged in the books?* | The fund structure |
| *What constrains each fund balance: external restrictions, board commitments, assignments?* | Nothing in a ledger records it |
| *Can you supply the capital asset and debt registers and the pension actuarial reports?* | Needed for the government-wide view |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance by fund** | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Budget** by fund | Recommended | XLSX |
| **Capital asset and long-term debt registers** | Required for government-wide statements | XLSX |
| Pension and OPEB actuarial reports | Required where applicable | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the statements, reconciliations and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Fund Structure
- Governmental Fund Statements
- Proprietary Fund Statements
- Fiduciary Fund Statements
- Fund Balance Classifications
- Government-Wide Statements
- Reconciliations
- Capital Assets, Long-Term Debt, Pensions / OPEB
- Budgetary Comparison
- Disclosures & RSI

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never assumes the basis of accounting.** Nothing downstream can be read without it.
- **Never classifies fund balance without the constraint behind it.**
- **Never fabricates a pension or OPEB figure.** Those come from the actuarial report.
- **Never states a current standard from memory as settled.** They change often.
