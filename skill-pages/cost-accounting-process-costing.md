# Process costing: cost per unit in a continuous flow

> Equivalent units and cost per unit by department.

**Skill:** `cost-accounting-process-costing`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 54%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Costs continuous production by department or process: maps the production flow, builds the physical units schedule, computes equivalent units under weighted average or FIFO, the cost per equivalent unit, and the cost assigned to completed units and to work in progress, then reconciles the cost roll-forward to the ledger. Reads departmental costs through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded production and cost data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for process costing, equivalent units, cost per unit in continuous production, a production cost report, or work in progress valuation by department.

Not for:

- Costing discrete jobs (use [cost-accounting-job-order](cost-accounting-job-order.md)) or standard cost variances (use [standard-costing-and-variance-analysis](standard-costing-and-variance-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period?* | Sets every read |
| *What are the departments or processes, in order, and how are they tagged in the books?* | Costs are split by that tag |
| *What are the units: opening work in progress, started, completed, closing, and how complete?* | Production data, never in the books |
| *Weighted average or FIFO, and where are materials added: at the start, part way, or evenly?* | Changes the equivalent units |
| *What spoilage or yield is normal?* | Needed to tie units in to units out |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Costs added by department**: materials, labour, overhead | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Production report**: units and completion by department | Required | XLSX |
| **Work in progress account detail** for the period | Required for the reconciliation | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the production cost report and proposed entries, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Departmental cost of production report
- Equivalent unit schedule
- Cost per equivalent unit
- WIP / FG rollforward
- Variance and reasonability checks

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never infers units or completion from a work in progress balance.**
- **Never picks weighted average or FIFO**, and never changes it between periods without saying so.
- **Never lets the model agree with itself and not with the ledger.** The roll-forward is tied to the books.
