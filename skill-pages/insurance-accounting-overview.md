# Insurance close: money now, a promise about later

> Close map for premium, claims, reinsurance and commissions.

**Skill:** `insurance-accounting-overview`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Maps the accounting close for an insurance business: settles whether the entity is a carrier, a managing general agent or a broker, then lays out the schedules and controls for premium and unearned premium, claims and loss reserves, reinsurance, acquisition costs and commissions, and investment income, with the close deliverables.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask how to close the books of an insurer, broker or agent, about unearned premium, loss reserves, reinsurance recoverables, deferred acquisition costs, or an insurance close checklist.

Not for:

- A company's own insurance prepayments (use [prepaid-amortization-schedule](prepaid-amortization-schedule.md)) or for the general close (use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity and period, and is it a carrier, a managing general agent or a broker?* | Decides everything below |
| *Is the entity regulated, and under which regime?* | Decides whether client money rules apply |
| *Can you supply the premium and claims data from the policy and claims systems?* | Not in the accounting system |
| *What reinsurance is in place: treaties, cessions, recoverables?* | Contracts |
| *Who is the actuary and how often is the valuation done? Do you have last close's schedules?* | Reserves and the opening positions |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Premium and policy data** for the period | Required for a carrier or agent | XLSX or CSV |
| **Claims data** and the actuary's reserve report | Required for a carrier | XLSX and PDF |
| **Reinsurance terms** | Recommended | PDF |
| **Prior close schedules** | Recommended | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the close map, schedules tied to the ledger and a coverage sheet, and never estimates a reserve.

- Close Checklist Tailored to the Business Model
- Required Data Sources and Owners
- Schedule Templates and Tie-Out Approach
- Risk List
- GL Reconciliations
- Client Money Reconciliation
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never estimates a loss reserve or incurred-but-not-reported claims.** Those are actuarial.
- **Never assumes the business model.** It asks first.
- **Never treats a reserve balance as evidence that the reserve is adequate.**
- **Never recomputes unearned premium from the ledger alone.** It needs the policy data.
