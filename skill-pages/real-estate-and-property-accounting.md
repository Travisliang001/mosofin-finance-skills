# Property accounting: what each property is, and what it does

> Classify and measure property; rental income and sales.

**Skill:** `real-estate-and-property-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Sector-specific accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for real estate held as investment property, owner-occupied property or property under development: reads what each property has actually done before classifying it, sets initial measurement including capitalised interest, applies the cost or fair value model, handles rental income on the lessor side, sales, impairment indicators and disclosures.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about investment property, capitalising development costs or interest, land and building allocation, straight-lining rent as a landlord, a property sale, or IAS 40.

Not for:

- Leases as a tenant (use [lease-accounting-asc842-ifrs16](lease-accounting-asc842-ifrs16.md)) or other fixed assets (use [fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation](fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | The framework governs everything |
| *What is each property used for: rented out, occupied, under development, held for sale?* | Decides classification |
| *Under IFRS, cost model or fair value model for investment property? Do you have valuations?* | An election, and a valuer's figure |
| *How was the price split between land and buildings, and on what basis?* | Land is not depreciated |
| *What are the lease terms for tenanted property: term, escalations, rent-free periods, incentives?* | The books have the cash, not the contract |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Property schedule**: cost, date, use, land and building split | Required | XLSX |
| **Ledger detail** for the property accounts and rental income | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Tenant leases**, or a summary of terms | Required for rental income | PDF or XLSX |
| Valuations | Required for the fair value model | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedules and proposed entries, and never supplies a valuation.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Property Register
- Classification & Use
- Initial Cost Build
- Land/Building Split & Depreciation
- Fair Value Workbook
- Rental Income Schedule
- Sales & Disposals
- Impairment Assessments
- Disclosures
- Ledger Contradictions

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a fair value or a valuation input.**
- **Never picks the measurement model.** Under IFRS the election is your and close to irreversible.
- **Never classifies a property against how it actually behaves without saying so.**
- **Never concludes on impairment.** It lists the indicators.
