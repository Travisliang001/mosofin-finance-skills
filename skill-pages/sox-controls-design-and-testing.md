# SOX controls: did the control operate?

> Risk and control matrix; controls tested at full population where traced.

**Skill:** `sox-controls-design-and-testing`  ·  **Area:** Controls, audit, treasury and the chart of accounts  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 46%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Designs, documents and tests internal controls over financial reporting: scoping, walkthroughs, what could go wrong, the risk and control matrix, segregation of duties tested across every transaction, design assessment, test plans, test results, deficiency evaluation and remediation. Says for each control whether its operation leaves a trace in the books, tests those at full population, and marks the rest for evidence a person must supply.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about SOX, ICFR, internal controls over financial reporting, a risk and control matrix, control testing, segregation of duties, or control deficiencies.

Not for:

- A general internal audit (use [internal-audit-workpaper](internal-audit-workpaper.md)) or for fraud red-flag testing (use [fraud-detection-and-forensics](fraud-detection-and-forensics.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which processes are in scope, for which company and period, and why those?* | Sets the control environment |
| *Is the objective design, operating effectiveness, or both?* | Decides which steps run |
| *Which controls exist, and for each: does it leave a trace in the accounting system, and where is its evidence?* | This one answer routes each control to a full-population test or to evidence a person supplies |
| *How does each process actually work, and which systems are involved?* | Walkthroughs are not in a ledger |
| *What are materiality and the scoping thresholds, and which prior deficiencies are open?* | Deficiencies are judged against them |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Process descriptions and the existing control list** | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| **Transaction data** for the in-scope processes, with user and approval fields | Required for population tests | XLSX or CSV |
| **Control evidence**: sign-offs, review records, reconciliations | Required for controls outside the books | PDF |
| Materiality, prior deficiencies | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the matrix, test results and a coverage sheet, and concludes on no severity.

- Scoping
- Risk and Control Matrix (RACM)
- Process Documentation
- Design Assessment
- Test Plans
- Test Results
- Deficiency Evaluation
- Remediation Tracker
- Full-Population Test Detail
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never treats clean data as proof that a control operated.**
- **Never fills in a severity rating or concludes on a material weakness.** It quantifies the exposure and leaves the rating to management and the auditors.
- **Never lets a control that could not be tested read as effective.**
- **Never invents materiality or a sample size.**
