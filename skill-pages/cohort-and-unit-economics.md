# Cohorts and unit economics: is each customer worth winning?

> Retention by cohort, CAC, LTV on margin, payback.

**Skill:** `cohort-and-unit-economics`  ·  **Area:** Forecasting, customers, credit, debt and fraud risk  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 66%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Analyses customers by acquisition cohort and computes unit economics: the retention table and curves, customer lifetime, CAC, LTV on margin, payback, LTV to CAC, and a view by segment. Derives each customer's start date from the first invoice and reads revenue per customer per month, and states plainly that channel and per-customer cost-to-serve are not in the books.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for cohort analysis, retention or churn by cohort, CAC, LTV, payback period, LTV to CAC, unit economics, or whether the business model works per customer.

Not for:

- A general KPI dashboard (use [kpi-dashboard-builder](kpi-dashboard-builder.md)) or for ageing receivables (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What is the business model? Subscription, transactional, marketplace or a mix* | It changes what retention means |
| *Which period, and monthly or quarterly cohorts?* | Sets the window and the cohort size |
| *When does a customer count as acquired, and when as lost?* | "Active" is not a field in the books |
| *Which accounts make up sales and marketing spend: fully loaded or paid only?* | It changes CAC materially |
| *Do you have channel data, cost-to-serve by customer, or a discount rate?* | None of these is in the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Revenue by customer by month**, or the invoice list with customer, date and amount | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Sales and marketing spend by month** | Required for CAC | XLSX or CSV |
| **Gross margin**, company level or by customer | Required for LTV | XLSX, CSV or typed |
| Channel or plan per customer | Optional. Without it there is no view by channel | CSV |
| Business model, definitions, account set | Required | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never computes LTV on revenue.** LTV is on margin, with the margin basis stated.
- **Never guesses the business model, the acquisition definition, the sales and marketing accounts or a discount rate.** Each is your answer.
- **Never presents blended CAC as the whole picture.** It says that channel detail is missing.
