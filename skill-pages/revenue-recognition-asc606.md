# Revenue recognition: an invoice is not revenue

> Five-step revenue model for a contract; schedule and proposed entries.

**Skill:** `revenue-recognition-asc606`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 53%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Applies the five-step revenue recognition model of ASC 606 and IFRS 15 to a customer contract: tests first whether the books apply the standard at all, then identifies the contract and its performance obligations, sets and allocates the transaction price, recognises revenue over time or at a point in time, and builds the schedule, the contract asset and liability balances, the proposed entries and the disclosure data. Reads billing and recognised revenue through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded contract and billing history.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask how to recognise revenue on a contract, about deferred revenue, performance obligations, standalone selling prices, variable consideration, contract modifications, or ASC 606 or IFRS 15.

Not for:

- Raising an invoice (use [customer-invoicing](customer-invoicing.md)) or for period-end deferrals in general (use [accruals-and-deferrals](accruals-and-deferrals.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **The contract**, or a summary of its key terms | Required | PDF or pasted |
| **Invoices and payments** on the contract to date | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Revenue and deferred revenue ledger detail** for the customer | Recommended. | XLSX or CSV |
| **Chart of accounts** | Required for the proposed entries. Without it entries use descriptive account names, labelled as such | XLSX or CSV |
| Standalone selling prices with sources | Required for more than one obligation | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never treats billed as earned.** Recognition follows the obligations, not the invoice dates.
- **Never picks the framework, an expedient or a standalone selling price.** An estimated price states its method and source.
- **Never concludes on a judgment for you**: principal or agent, distinct or not, over time or point in time. It sets out the indicators and asks.
