# Three-statement model: three statements, linked, and it must balance

> Linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow, with drivers measured from history.

**Skill:** `three-statement-financial-model`  ·  **Area:** Quarter-end and year-end close, planning and foreign currency  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 62%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds an integrated three-statement financial model: the income statement, the supporting schedules for working capital, fixed assets, debt and equity, the cash flow statement that links them, and a balance sheet that must balance in every period, with circularity resolved, checks built in and scenarios on top. Measures each driver from the business's own history before any assumption is set. Reads history through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded financial statements.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to build a three-statement model, an integrated financial model, a linked income statement, balance sheet and cash flow projection, or a model for fundraising or planning.

Not for:

- An operating budget alone (use [budget-builder](budget-builder.md)), for a re-forecast (use [rolling-forecast](rolling-forecast.md)) or for testing assumptions on an existing model (use [scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis](scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What is the model for: fundraising, diligence or internal planning? Which company?* | Sets how rigorous it must be |
| *What horizon, and monthly, quarterly or annual?* | Sets the model's shape |
| *What are the operating drivers: revenue build, margins, operating costs?* | The forecast is yours |
| *What is planned for capex, borrowing and repayment, and equity?* | None of it is in the books; debt terms are in the loan agreements |
| *Tax by a single effective rate or in detail? How should interest circularity be handled?* | Both change the build |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Income statements and balance sheets**, two to three years, by period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Fixed asset and debt schedules** | Recommended. Without them those schedules start from balances only | XLSX |
| Drivers, capex, debt and equity plans, tax approach | Required for the forecast | XLSX, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the model and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never inserts a hardcoded plug** to make the balance sheet balance.
- **Never sets an assumption for you**: not a growth rate, a capex plan, a borrowing, an FX rate or a tax rate. It shows the history and asks.
- **Never mixes history and forecast without marking which is which.**
