# Chart of accounts: the buckets every transaction goes into

> Design or clean up the chart, with usage measured and old-to-new mapping.

**Skill:** `chart-of-accounts-designer`  ·  **Area:** Controls, audit, treasury and the chart of accounts  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 68%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Designs or restructures a chart of accounts: measures how each existing account is actually used, sets the structure and numbering, builds the account list by section with descriptions and intended use, adds industry and tax accounts, handles multi-entity needs, and maps every old account to its new one. Fits the account types of the accounting system you name. Reads the existing chart through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded chart.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to design, clean up, simplify or restructure a chart of accounts, renumber accounts, merge duplicate accounts, or set up accounts for a new company or system.

Not for:

- Coding individual transactions (use [gl-coding-assistant](gl-coding-assistant.md)) or for building a journal entry (use [journal-entry-builder](journal-entry-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Is this a new chart, or a restructure of the existing one? For which company?* | A restructure needs a mapping from old to new |
| *Which accounting system will the chart live in: the connected one, or another?* | Account types and numbering rules differ by system |
| *Which reporting framework: US GAAP, IFRS or local GAAP?* | Decides the sections and some required accounts |
| *What industry and size is the business, and which dimensions do you report by: class, department, location, project?* | Industry adds accounts; dimensions replace many sub-accounts |
| *Which reports must the chart serve: investors, lenders, the board, segments? Any multi-entity needs?* | Reporting needs drive how fine the accounts are |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Existing chart of accounts**: number, name, type, active status | Required for a restructure | XLSX or CSV |
| **Trial balance, or activity by account**, 12 months or more | Recommended. Without it usage cannot be measured | XLSX or CSV |
| Target system, framework, industry and dimensions, reporting needs | C and D required | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the proposed chart, the mapping and a coverage sheet, and changes no account.

- Chart of Accounts
- System-Specific Import File
- Migration Map
- Dimensions / Classes
- Account Usage Analysis
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never creates, renames, merges or deactivates an account in any system.** The chart and the mapping are proposals.
- **Never infers the reporting framework from the country.**
- **Never proposes removing an account that still holds a balance** without saying where the balance goes.
- **Never mistakes a balance for usage.** An account is judged by its activity.
