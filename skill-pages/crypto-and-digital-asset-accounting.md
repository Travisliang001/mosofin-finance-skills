# Digital assets: not cash, not a share, not inventory

> Classify, measure and record digital assets; sweep for unlisted holdings.

**Skill:** `crypto-and-digital-asset-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 54%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for cryptocurrencies and other digital assets: sweeps the books for payments to exchanges that point to holdings not on the list, classifies each asset under the framework, measures it initially and afterwards, recognises purchases, sales, rewards and payments, considers custody and control, tests impairment where the cost model applies, and prepares disclosures. Reads the cash side through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded wallet and exchange records.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about accounting for bitcoin or other crypto, digital asset fair value, staking or mining rewards, crypto received as payment, cost basis on disposal, or stablecoins.

Not for:

- Foreign currency balances (use [multicurrency-fx-revaluation](multicurrency-fx-revaluation.md)) or for tax treatment.

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and reporting date, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | Decides the measurement model |
| *What is held and transacted: asset, quantity, dates, and in which wallet or exchange?* | The holdings are not in the books |
| *Why is it held: investment, payment, trading, or something else?* | Drives classification |
| *What are the fair values at the relevant dates, from which market?* | Never supplied here |
| *How is it held in custody, and which cost basis method applies on disposal?* | Custody decides whether there is an asset at all |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Wallet and exchange records**: holdings and transactions | Required | CSV |
| **Fair values** at the relevant dates, with venue | Required | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| **Ledger detail** for the digital asset and related accounts | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedules and proposed entries, and never supplies a price.

- Digital Asset Inventory
- Classification & Measurement Policy
- Lot-Level Cost Basis
- Fair Value Measurement
- Transactions Register
- Impairment
- Mining / Staking Revenue
- Disclosures
- Open Items / Specialist
- Completeness Sweep

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a price.** It asks for the value, the venue and the time.
- **Never asserts which standard applies without you confirming it for the reporting date.**
- **Never picks the cost basis method on a disposal.**
- **Gives no tax treatment.**
