# Cash flow statement: from profit to the change in cash

> Statement of cash flows, tied exactly to the change in cash.

**Skill:** `cash-flow-statement-indirect-method`  ·  **Area:** Analysis and reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 63%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds the statement of cash flows by the indirect method: net income reconciled to operating cash, then investing and financing, the effect of exchange rates, the supplemental disclosures, and a tie-out to the change in cash that must be exact. Reads capex, disposals, debt and equity movements from the general ledger, so they are read and not solved for. Follows US GAAP (ASC 230) or IFRS (IAS 7), with the direct method on request.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to build a cash flow statement or statement of cash flows, reconcile net income to operating cash, explain why profit and cash differ, or find why the cash flow does not tie to the balance sheet.

Not for:

- A forward-looking cash forecast (use [cash-flow-forecast-13-week](cash-flow-forecast-13-week.md)) or for the full statement set (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Balance sheet at the period end** | Required | XLSX, CSV or PDF |
| **Balance sheet at the comparative date**, at the same level of detail | Required. If the detail differs, the changes will not map | XLSX, CSV or PDF |
| **Income statement** for the period | Required | XLSX, CSV or PDF |
| **Ledger detail** for fixed assets, accumulated depreciation, intangibles, debt and equity | **Required for accuracy.** Without it capex, disposals and depreciation cannot be separated, and the output says so | XLSX or CSV |
| Framework, cash definition, acquisitions and disposals | Required | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never inserts a plug.** The statement ends at exactly the change in cash. A difference that cannot be explained is reported as unexplained, with its amount.
- **Never infers the reporting framework from the country.** US GAAP or IFRS is your answer.
