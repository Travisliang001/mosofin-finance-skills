# Employee benefits: what you owe beyond pay

> Retirement match, accrued time off and unremitted contributions.

**Skill:** `benefits-and-401k-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for employee benefits: sorts them into defined contribution plans, compensated absences, short-term benefits such as health cover, and defined benefit plans, then computes the cost and the liability for each, handles vesting and forfeitures, and reconciles what was withheld to what was remitted. Checks for employee contributions still sitting in the employer's books.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about 401(k) or retirement plan accounting, employer match, accrued vacation or PTO, a health benefit accrual, or unremitted contributions.

Not for:

- The payroll entry itself (use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md)), payroll tie-outs (use [payroll-reconciliation](payroll-reconciliation.md)) or a defined benefit valuation in depth (use [pensions-and-postretirement-asc715](pensions-and-postretirement-asc715.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | The framework decides the defined benefit treatment |
| *Which benefits are offered: retirement, health, insurance, paid time off, others?* | Sets what is in scope |
| *What are the plan terms: contribution formula, match, eligibility, vesting?* | Never in the ledger |
| *What are the paid-time-off policy and the balances per employee?* | Hours and rates come from payroll or HR |
| *Is there a defined benefit plan with an actuarial report, and what are the remittance deadlines?* | Needed for that plan, and to judge unremitted balances |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Payroll and contribution data** by period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Plan documents** or a summary of terms | Required | PDF |
| **Paid-time-off balances** by employee | Required for that accrual | XLSX |
| Actuarial report | Required for a defined benefit plan | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedules and proposed entries, and remits nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Benefit Cost Summary
- Defined Contribution
- Compensated Absences
- Short-Term Benefits
- Defined Benefit
- Journal Entries
- Disclosures
- Open Items / Actuary Required

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never values a defined benefit obligation.** Without an actuarial report, that part is listed as not done.
- **Never assumes a match formula, an accrual rate or a vesting schedule.**
- **Never remits or moves a contribution.** An unremitted balance is reported with its age.
