# Share-based pay: how much it cost, and when

> Stock compensation expense schedule from the cap table you supply.

**Skill:** `equity-compensation-accounting`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 41%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Accounts for share-based compensation under ASC 718 or IFRS 2: classifies each award as equity-settled or cash-settled, takes the grant-date fair value you supply, analyses the vesting conditions, sets the recognition pattern, handles forfeitures, builds the expense schedule and proposed entries, and prepares the award roll-forward and disclosures. Compares the expense recorded in the books with the schedule.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about stock compensation expense, options, RSUs, an ESPP, vesting schedules, forfeitures, a modification or repricing, or ASC 718 or IFRS 2.

Not for:

- The statement of equity (use [statement-of-equity-changes](statement-of-equity-changes.md)) or for the payroll entry (use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and ASC 718 or IFRS 2?* | Decides attribution and forfeiture treatment |
| *What are the awards: type, grant date, quantity, strike price, vesting terms, and equity- or cash-settled?* | From the cap table |
| *What is the grant-date fair value of each award, and where does it come from?* | Never computed here |
| *What are the vesting conditions: service, performance or market? What is the forfeiture policy?* | They change the accounting entirely |
| *Were any awards modified, cancelled or exercised in the period?* | Repricings and accelerations are complex |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Award data from the cap table**: grants, vests, exercises, forfeitures | Required | XLSX |
| **Fair values** per award, with source and date | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Ledger detail** for stock compensation expense and equity | Recommended, for the comparison | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedule and proposed entries, and never values an award.

- Expense Summary
- Award-by-Award Expense Schedule
- Vesting Condition Analysis
- Forfeiture Treatment
- Award Roll-Forward
- Journal Entries
- Disclosures
- Open Items / Specialist Review
- Recorded vs. Schedule
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never values an award or picks a valuation input.** It asks for the value, its source and its date.
- **Never decides whether a performance condition is probable.**
- **Never picks the forfeiture policy**, and never changes it between periods without saying so.
