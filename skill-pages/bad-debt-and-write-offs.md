# Bad debt: the allowance, and the write-offs

> Allowance from your own loss history; write-off candidates and entries.

**Skill:** `bad-debt-and-write-offs`  ·  **Area:** Controls, audit, treasury and the chart of accounts  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 56%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Assesses whether receivables will be collected: derives loss rates from the business's own history, identifies specific write-off candidates, computes the allowance for doubtful accounts under the framework you name (CECL, IFRS 9 expected credit loss, or an aged-percentage method), works out the adjustment to the existing allowance, and proposes the entries with the roll-forward.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about bad debt, the allowance for doubtful accounts, expected credit losses, a bad debt reserve, writing off a receivable, or recovering a written-off balance.

Not for:

- Chasing overdue invoices (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)) or for setting a customer's credit limit (use [credit-limit-and-customer-risk](credit-limit-and-customer-risk.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and which period end?* | Sets the ageing date and every read |
| *Which framework: US GAAP (CECL), IFRS 9, or local GAAP with an aged-percentage method?* | Decides the whole method |
| *Do you know of any customer in insolvency, in dispute, or with a changed credit position?* | The books cannot see this |
| *Who may approve a write-off, and up to what amount?* | A write-off without approval is just a loss |
| *Under CECL or IFRS 9: what forward-looking view applies? And which tax jurisdiction?* | The forecast is yours; tax relief differs by country |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Receivables ageing by customer** at the period end | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Write-offs and recoveries**, three to five years, by ageing bucket where possible | Required for CECL and IFRS 9, recommended otherwise | XLSX or CSV |
| **Allowance account detail** for the period | Required for the adjustment | XLSX or CSV |
| Framework, customer information, approval limits | C required | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Proposes entries for a person to approve and post, and writes off nothing.

- Allowance Calculation
- Specific Write-Offs
- Methodology Documentation
- Roll-Forward
- GL Posting
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal, and no balance is written off by this skill.
- **Never picks the reporting framework**, and never infers it from the country.
- **Never assumes a loss rate.** Rates come from history, or are stated as your.
- **Never proposes a write-off without the approval authority named**, and never claims tax relief that has not been confirmed.
