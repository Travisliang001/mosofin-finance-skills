# Working capital: where the cash is trapped

> Cash conversion cycle, where cash is trapped, each lever sized.

**Skill:** `working-capital-optimization`  ·  **Area:** Analysis and reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 61%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Finds the cash tied up in receivables, inventory and payables: the cash conversion cycle month by month, growth separated from seasonality and from real deterioration, where collection is slow, where stock is stuck, which bills are paid early, and each lever sized in days and cash next to the trade-off it carries.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to optimise working capital, improve the cash conversion cycle, reduce DSO or DIO, extend DPO, free up cash from operations, find where cash is trapped, or check whether suppliers are paid early.

Not for:

- A weekly cash forecast (use [cash-flow-forecast-13-week](cash-flow-forecast-13-week.md)) or for chasing overdue invoices (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, and how far back? Twenty-four months or more is best.* | Seasonality and trend need two full years |
| *What payment terms did you agree with customers and with suppliers, including the exceptions?* | Recorded terms and agreed terms differ, and the gap is the diagnostic |
| *Which relationships are off limits, and which service levels must hold?* | These are the guardrails every lever is tested against |
| *What is your cost of capital?* | Turns days released into a value |
| *Do you have industry benchmarks for DSO, DIO and DPO?* | External data, never in the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly balance sheets**, 24 months or more: receivables, inventory, payables, accruals | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Monthly revenue and cost of sales** for the same months | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Receivables detail** with invoice dates and payment dates | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Payables detail** by vendor, with terms and payment dates | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Inventory detail** by category or SKU, with quantities and last movement | **Required for the inventory diagnostic.** Without it that section is value-only and says so | XLSX or CSV |
| Agreed terms, guardrails, cost of capital, benchmarks | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces a diagnostic, a sized plan and a coverage sheet, changes no terms and contacts no one.

- Executive Summary
- Cash Conversion Cycle Diagnostic
- AR Diagnostic
- Inventory Diagnostic
- AP Diagnostic
- Lever-by-Lever Opportunity Sizing
- Trade-Offs / Guardrails
- Implementation Roadmap
- KPI Dashboard
- Cash Forecast Integration

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never changes data, payment terms or credit limits in any system, and never contacts a customer or supplier.** Every lever is a proposal for a person to act on.
- **Never shows an opportunity figure without its trade-off.** The goal is optimisation, not maximisation.
- **Never invents a benchmark or a days target.** With none given, it compares the business to its own history and says so.
