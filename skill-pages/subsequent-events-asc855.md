# Subsequent events: what happened after the balance sheet date

> Sweep post-period activity; classify events before issue.

**Skill:** `subsequent-events-asc855`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 44%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Performs a subsequent events review under ASC 855 or IAS 10: sets the window from the balance sheet date to the issue date, sweeps the books for post-period activity that bears on period-end estimates, asks management by category for events the books cannot show, classifies each event as recognised or disclosed only, sets out the treatment, and documents the conclusions.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a subsequent events review, events after the balance sheet date, whether something after year-end needs adjusting or disclosing, or to refresh the review before issuing accounts.

Not for:

- The notes as a whole (use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md)) or contingencies in depth (use [contingencies-and-litigation-asc450](contingencies-and-litigation-asc450.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and balance sheet date?* | The start of the window |
| *When will the accounts be issued, or be available to be issued, and which definition applies?* | The end of the window |
| *Which period-end estimates matter most: reserves, impairments, contingencies?* | These are what the sweep tests |
| *Has management had any of these since the period end: litigation, financing, an acquisition or disposal, a casualty, a major customer loss?* | The books cannot show these |
| *Are there board minutes or legal updates since the period end?* | Evidence outside the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Ledger detail from the balance sheet date to today** | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Period-end balances for the estimates being tested** | Required | XLSX |
| Board minutes and legal updates | Recommended | PDF |
| Management's list of known events | Required for the events the books cannot show | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the event list, classifications and a coverage sheet, and concludes for no one.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Subsequent Events Log
- Recommended Adjusting Entries
- Draft Disclosure Language
- Sign-Off Checklist
- Ledger Sweep Detail

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never treats a clean sweep as a complete review.** Events outside the books are asked for.
- **Never decides whether an event is recognised or disclosed only without the facts of when the condition arose.**
- **Never closes the review before the issue date.** The window runs until then.
- **Never authors the disclosure of a legal matter.**
