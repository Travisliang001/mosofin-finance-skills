# Budget: next year's plan, line by line

> Annual operating budget by month, every line traced to a driver.

**Skill:** `budget-builder`  ·  **Area:** Quarter-end and year-end close, planning and foreign currency  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds an annual operating budget by month: revenue, cost of sales, operating expenses and operating income, each line traced to a driver and a written assumption, with seasonality taken from the business's own history and a reconciliation to the targets. Works incrementally, zero-based or driver-based, by area. Reads history through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded prior-year results.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to build or draft a budget, an annual plan or an operating plan, budget headcount or departmental spend, or document budget assumptions.

Not for:

- Comparing actuals with a budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)), for a re-forecast during the year (use [rolling-forecast](rolling-forecast.md)) or a balance sheet and cash projection (use [three-statement-financial-model](three-statement-financial-model.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which fiscal year, and monthly or quarterly?* | Sets the budget period |
| *Which method for each area: incremental, zero-based or driver-based? Does a budget already exist?* | It is often a mix, and extending beats replacing |
| *What are the revenue drivers for next year: units, price, customers, churn, pipeline?* | The plan is not in the books |
| *What is the headcount plan: current roles, planned hires, timing and pay?* | Usually the largest cost |
| *What are the targets, the known commitments, and any escalation assumptions?* | Needed to reconcile the budget to the goal |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly profit and loss by line**, 24–36 months | Required for incremental and driver-based methods | XLSX or CSV |
| **Existing budget**, if there is one | Recommended | XLSX |
| Revenue drivers, headcount plan | Required | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| Targets, commitments, escalation | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the budget, its assumptions and a coverage sheet, and loads nothing into any system.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Budget Summary
- Monthly Budget
- Revenue Build
- Headcount Plan
- OpEx Detail
- Assumptions Register
- Flex / Sensitivity
- Reconciliation to Targets

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It does not load a budget into the accounting system.
- **Never assumes a growth, inflation or pay rate.** With none given, the line stays flat and the output says so.
- **Never presents a line without its driver and its assumption.**
- **Where a budget already exists, extends it.** It does not replace it unseen.
