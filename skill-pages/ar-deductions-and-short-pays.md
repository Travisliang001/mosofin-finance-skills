# Customer deductions and short pays: valid, invalid, or needs backup

> Customer deductions decided valid, invalid or needs backup on their evidence; credits, write-offs and disputes proposed.

**Skill:** `ar-deductions-and-short-pays`  ·  **Area:** Flux analysis and customer deductions  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 62%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Works the open customer deductions and short payments: lists every amount a customer held back, ages it, sorts it by reason (damage, shortage, pricing, promotion, freight, unexplained), checks each against its evidence (proof of delivery, carrier report, promotion agreement, contract terms) and the write-off policy, and decides valid, invalid or needs backup. Catches the same invoice deducted twice. Proposes credit memos for valid deductions, small-balance write-offs within policy, and dispute letters for invalid ones.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about deductions, short pays, chargebacks from retailers, customer disputes, or clearing old deductions out of receivables.

Not for:

- Matching full payments to invoices (use [cash-application](cash-application.md)) or chasing overdue invoices (use [ar-aging-and-collections](ar-aging-and-collections.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms |
| *Which company, and as at what date?* | Sets the ageing and every read |
| *Where are open deductions kept? A deduction log, a separate receivables account, or open invoice balances* | Decides where the population comes from |
| *What is the write-off policy? The limit, and who approves* | Decides which small items can be cleared |
| *What backup do you have? Proofs of delivery, carrier reports, promotion agreements, contracts* | The evidence that decides valid or invalid |
| *How long do customers allow for a dispute? Any customer-specific rules* | Old deductions are hard to recover |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Open deductions**: customer, invoice, payment date, amount held back, reason given | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Backup**: proofs of delivery, carrier damage reports, promotion or pricing agreements, contract terms | Required to decide valid or invalid. Without it every item is "needs backup" | PDF, text, or type it in the chat |
| Remittance advices for the short payments | Recommended. They carry the customer's own reason | PDF or text |
| Open receivables and customer list | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |
| Write-off and dispute policy | Recommended | Any, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the deduction log, proposed credit memos, write-offs and dispute drafts, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Deduction log
- Duplicates and repeats
- Proposed credit memos and write-offs
- Dispute and backup-request drafts
- Proposed entries

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** It issues no credit memo, writes nothing off and sends no letter. Every one is a proposal.
- **Never writes off above the policy limit**, and never proposes a write-off without naming who approves it under the policy.
- **Never calls a deduction valid or invalid without evidence.** No evidence is "needs backup".
