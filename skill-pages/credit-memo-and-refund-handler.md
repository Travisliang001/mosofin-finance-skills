# Credit Memo and Refund Handler

> Credit memos and refunds with the offsetting entry.

**Skill:** `credit-memo-and-refund-handler`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 69%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you process a credit memo, customer refund, vendor credit, or sales return from your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "issue a credit memo"
- "process a refund to a customer"
- "record a vendor credit note"
- "apply this credit against an invoice"
- "how do I record a sales return"

Not for:

- Bad-debt write-offs as policy — use [bad-debt-and-write-offs](bad-debt-and-write-offs.md).
- Normal invoice extraction — use [invoice-data-extractor](invoice-data-extractor.md).

## What it asks you first

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a credit memo record, the offsetting JE, application instructions, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Credit Memos Register
- GL Posting Inputs
- Application Tracking
- Unapplied Credits
- Validation Exceptions
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
