# Payroll Journal Entry Builder

> Payroll entries with gross-to-net, employer costs and accruals.

**Skill:** `payroll-journal-entry-builder`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 72%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you build the journal entries that record payroll in the general ledger, against your books.

## When to use it

Typical requests:

- "book the payroll journal"
- "payroll journal entry"
- "record this payroll run"
- "payroll GL entries"
- "accrue payroll"
- "split payroll across departments/cost centers"

Not for:

- Reconciling payroll to the GL after posting — use [payroll-reconciliation](payroll-reconciliation.md).
- Payroll tax filings — use [payroll-tax-filings](payroll-tax-filings.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs balanced payroll journal entries with gross-to-net breakdown, employer costs, accruals, clearing, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Journal Entries
- Gross-to-Net Reconciliation
- Employer Cost Breakdown
- Department / Cost Center Allocation
- Liability Summary
- Validation
- Run Comparison
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
