# Treasury: how cash is held, moved and protected

> Map every cash account; structure, liquidity and payment-control policies.

**Skill:** `cash-management-and-treasury`  ·  **Area:** Controls, audit, treasury and the chart of accounts  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Reviews how a business holds and moves its cash and designs the treasury set-up: a map of every cash account with its balance and activity, the bank account structure, a liquidity policy, a short-term investment policy, payment controls, FX and borrowing facility management, KPIs and governance. Finds dormant accounts, idle balances and concentration with one bank.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about treasury or cash management, too many bank accounts, cash pooling or concentration, where to hold surplus cash, a liquidity or investment policy, payment controls, or wire-fraud prevention.

Not for:

- A weekly cash forecast (use [cash-flow-forecast-13-week](cash-flow-forecast-13-week.md)), for bank charges (use [bank-fee-and-interest-analysis](bank-fee-and-interest-analysis.md)) or for loan covenants (use [debt-and-covenant-tracking](debt-and-covenant-tracking.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which legal entities, countries and currencies are in scope, and is any missing from what I can see?* | An entity with no connected file still holds cash |
| *For each bank account: which bank, what is it for, and who can sign?* | None of this is in the books |
| *Is any cash trapped or restricted, and can cash move between entities?* | It changes every liquidity figure, and decides whether pooling is possible |
| *What is your risk appetite: the most you will hold with one bank, what you may invest in, how much FX exposure?* | Policy limits are your decision |
| *Which policies and facilities exist today, and what must liquidity cover: debt service, capex?* | Extend what exists; do not replace it unseen |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash account list** with balances, by entity and currency | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Movements per account**, 12 months | Required for the activity and liquidity view | XLSX or CSV |
| Bank, purpose and signatories per account | Required for the structure design | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| Existing policies and facility terms | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the map, the policies and a coverage sheet, and moves no money.

- Bank Account Map
- Recommended Structure
- Liquidity Policy
- Investment Policy
- Payment Controls
- FX Exposure & Policy
- Treasury KPIs
- Counterparty Risk
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never moves money, opens or closes an account, or changes anything in any system.**
- **Never presents a designed control as a tested one.**
- **Never names an investment product, a bank or a rate as a recommendation.** It sets the policy criteria; the choice is your.
- **Never counts cash that cannot be moved as available.** Trapped cash is shown apart.
