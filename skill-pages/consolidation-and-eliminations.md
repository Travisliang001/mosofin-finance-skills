# Consolidation: the group as one business

> Group workpaper, eliminations and non-controlling interest.

**Skill:** `consolidation-and-eliminations`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Consolidates the trial balances of a group into group financial statements: sets the consolidation scope, aligns framework and currency, translates foreign entities at rates you supply, builds the consolidation workpaper, proposes intercompany eliminations, computes non-controlling interest and equity-method investments, and ties the consolidated statements out. Lists any group member whose books it could not read. Reads each entity through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded trial balances.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to consolidate entities, prepare group or consolidated accounts, eliminate intercompany balances, compute non-controlling interest, or build a consolidation workpaper.

Not for:

- Matching intercompany balances pair by pair (use [intercompany-reconciliation](intercompany-reconciliation.md)) or for a single company's statements (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entities are in the group, who owns what percentage, and which is the parent?* | Drives scope and non-controlling interest |
| *What consolidation date and period, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | Applied identically to every entity |
| *What is the presentation currency, and what are the closing, average and historical rates, with source and dates?* | No accounting system supplies rates |
| *Were there acquisitions: fair value adjustments, goodwill? Any equity-method investments?* | Not in any entity's books |
| *Do you have the prior period's consolidated balances?* | Needed for continuity and the opening non-controlling interest |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance for each entity** at the same date | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Group structure** with ownership percentages | Required | PDF, or type it in the chat |
| **Rate table** with source and dates | Required for more than one currency | XLSX or pasted |
| **Intercompany balances and transactions** per entity | Required for eliminations | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the workpaper, proposed eliminations and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

- Consolidation Workpaper
- Currency Translation
- Elimination Entries
- NCI Calculation and Roll-Forward
- Equity-Method Investments
- Consolidated Statements
- Tie-Outs
- Intercompany Mismatch
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never presents summed ledgers as a consolidation.** An entity it could not read is named as missing.
- **Never supplies an exchange rate or an ownership percentage.**
- **Never forces an elimination.** An intercompany difference is carried and shown, not absorbed.
- **Never decides functional currency or control for you.**
