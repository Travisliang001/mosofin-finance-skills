# Deferred tax: the future tax already on the balance sheet

> Temporary differences and the deferred tax roll-forward.

**Skill:** `income-tax-deferred-tax-rollforward`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 54%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds and maintains the deferred tax schedule under ASC 740 or IAS 12: the inventory of temporary differences between book and tax basis, deferred tax assets and liabilities by jurisdiction at the enacted rates you supply, the roll-forward from opening to closing, the valuation allowance evidence, the tie to the total provision, and the proposed entries and disclosures. Reads book balances through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded trial balance and tax schedules.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a deferred tax roll-forward, deferred tax assets and liabilities, temporary differences, or to tie deferred tax to the provision.

Not for:

- The whole provision (use [corporate-tax-provision-asc740](corporate-tax-provision-asc740.md)) or for the tax return (use [tax-return-prep-corporate](tax-return-prep-corporate.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity, period and tax jurisdiction?* | Sets the scope |
| *What are the enacted rates by jurisdiction, with source, and when is each difference expected to reverse?* | Deferred tax uses the rate for the reversal period |
| *What is the tax basis of each item: fixed assets, accruals, reserves?* | The book side is read; the tax side is not |
| *What are the loss and credit carryforwards, with expiry dates?* | Expiry matters as much as amount |
| *Do you have last period's deferred tax workpapers?* | The opening balances |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial balance** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Tax basis schedules**, including tax depreciation | Required | XLSX |
| **Prior deferred tax workpapers** | Required for opening balances | XLSX |
| Enacted rates with source | Required | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

- Temporary Difference Inventory
- Inventory Mapping Checks
- DTA / DTL Calculation
- Rollforward
- Rollforward Tie-Out
- Valuation Allowance Assessment
- Tie to Provision
- Journal Entries
- Disclosure Tables
- Coverage

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never puts a permanent difference in the schedule.**
- **Never concludes on the valuation allowance.** It sets out the evidence.
