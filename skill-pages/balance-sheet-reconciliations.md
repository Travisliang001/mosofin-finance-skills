# Balance Sheet Reconciliations

> Tie each balance sheet account to its support.

**Skill:** `balance-sheet-reconciliations`  ·  **Area:** The close cycle and the sub-ledgers  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 63%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you reconcile balance sheet accounts to supporting subledgers or external documents, working from your books.

**In plain words:** the balance sheet shows one number per account. Behind most of those numbers there should be a detailed list that adds up to exactly that number: the customers who owe you, the bills you haven't paid, the assets you own. **Reconciling** is proving the two agree, and explaining every difference when they don't. It is the routine that catches errors before they become audit findings.

## When to use it

Typical requests, or anything like them:

- "reconcile our balance sheet accounts"
- "reconcile [AR/AP/prepaid/accrued/intercompany/etc.] to GL"
- "monthly BS rec workpaper"
- "reconcile this account to its supporting schedule"
- "identify unreconciled differences"

Not for:

- Bank reconciliation: use [bank-reconciliation](bank-reconciliation.md).
- Full period close: use [month-end-close-checklist](month-end-close-checklist.md).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it confirms these with you, in one message:

| It confirms | Needed? |
|---|---|
| Account(s) being reconciled (number + name) | Recommended |
| Reporting period (date range) | Required |
| GL balance at period end | Required |
| Supporting subledger / schedule / external doc | Required |
| Prior period reconciliation | Recommended |
| Materiality threshold for unreconciled differences | Recommended |
| Chart of accounts, for posting cleanup JEs | Required |
| Who prepares and who reviews | Recommended |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, each item marked Required above comes from you: Reporting period, GL balance at period end, Supporting subledger / schedule / external doc, Chart of accounts. Ledger detail and listings go in as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a reconciliation workpaper per account with tie-out, identified items, unreconciled differences, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
