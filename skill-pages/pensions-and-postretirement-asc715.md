# Defined benefit pensions: from the actuary's report to the books

> From the actuary's report to roll-forwards and entries.

**Skill:** `pensions-and-postretirement-asc715`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 55%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Turns an actuarial report for a defined benefit pension or other postretirement plan into the accounting under ASC 715 or IAS 19: identifies the plan and its components, rolls the obligation and plan assets forward, determines funded status and the net periodic cost, and proposes the journal entries and disclosures. Compares what the books recorded with what the report says.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about defined benefit pension accounting, funded status, net periodic benefit cost, actuarial gains and losses, or pension disclosures.

Not for:

- Defined contribution plans such as a 401(k) (use [benefits-and-401k-accounting](benefits-and-401k-accounting.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |

| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which plan, and which period end?* | Sets the scope |
| *Is this a defined benefit plan, and ASC 715 or IAS 19?* | The two differ on expected return and net interest |
| *Can you supply the actuarial valuation report, and what is its measurement date?* | Everything flows from it |
| *Were there plan amendments, curtailments or settlements?* | Amendments create prior service cost and are easy to miss |
| *Do you have last year's roll-forwards?* | The opening position |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Actuarial valuation report** at the period end | Required | PDF |
| **Prior year roll-forwards** | Recommended | XLSX |
| **Ledger detail** for the pension liability, other comprehensive income and contributions | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the roll-forwards and proposed entries from the actuary's figures, and never supplies an assumption.

- Recorded vs. Actuarial
- Obligation Roll-Forward
- Plan Asset Roll-Forward
- Funded Status
- Net Periodic Benefit Cost
- AOCI Roll-Forward
- Journal Entries
- Disclosure Tables
- Controls Checklist
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a discount rate or any actuarial assumption.**
- **Never computes the obligation.** With no report, nothing is measured.
- **Never treats a defined contribution plan as a defined benefit plan.** The plan type is confirmed first.
