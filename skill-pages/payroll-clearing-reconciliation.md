# Payroll Clearing Reconciliation

> Itemise what is left in the payroll clearing accounts.

**Skill:** `payroll-clearing-reconciliation`  ·  **Area:** Payroll, entry review, forecasting and fixed assets  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 71%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Helps you reconcile payroll clearing, payroll suspense, payroll liability, or any payroll-related control account against your books.

**In plain words:** when payroll runs, the business owes several different people money at once — the staff, the tax authority, the pension provider, sometimes a court. Each of those obligations sits in its own account until it is paid. This checks that every one of them was paid, and finds the ones that have been sitting there since nobody quite knows when.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for any of these, or something like them.

Typical requests:

- "reconcile payroll clearing"
- "payroll suspense balance"
- "clear payroll liabilities"
- "why is payroll clearing not zero"
- "reconcile payroll to bank"

Not for:

- The payroll JE itself — use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md).
- Payroll tax filings — use [payroll-tax-filings](payroll-tax-filings.md).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself, it asks for ledger detail and listings as CSV or XLSX exports, statements and third-party documents as PDF or CSV, and short facts typed into the chat.

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Outputs a payroll clearing reconciliation with itemized differences, clearing JEs, and a coverage sheet. Every entry and output is a proposal for a person to act on, and nothing is posted, sent or paid.

- Account number and name
- Period
- GL balance
- Expected balance
- Difference
- Status

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry, message or change it suggests is a proposal for a person to approve.
- **Says what it could not check.** Anything it could not verify is listed, with what you would need to supply.
