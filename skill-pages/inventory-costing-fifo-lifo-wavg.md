# Inventory costing: which cost belongs to what was sold

> FIFO, LIFO or weighted average; ending inventory and cost of sales.

**Skill:** `inventory-costing-fifo-lifo-wavg`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 52%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Applies an inventory costing method to compute ending inventory and cost of goods sold: establishes which method the accounting system itself applies, confirms the method is allowed under the framework, sets the cost basis, builds the FIFO or LIFO layers or the weighted average, tests the lower of cost and net realisable value, proposes the period-end entries, and compares methods where that helps.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about FIFO, LIFO, weighted-average or specific identification, cost of goods sold, inventory layers, a costing method change, or valuing ending inventory.

Not for:

- Slow or obsolete stock (use [inventory-obsolescence-and-reserve](inventory-obsolescence-and-reserve.md)), for tying inventory to the ledger (use [inventory-to-gl-reconciliation](inventory-to-gl-reconciliation.md)) or for standard cost variances (use [standard-costing-and-variance-analysis](standard-costing-and-variance-analysis.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Purchases by item**: date, quantity, unit cost | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Sales or issues by item**: date, quantity | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Opening inventory**: quantity and cost, or layers | Required | XLSX |
| Selling prices and costs to complete and sell | Recommended, for the value test | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

- Journal Entries
- Method Comparison
- Tie-Out
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never picks the costing method**, and never applies LIFO under IFRS.
- **Never rebuilds opening layers by assumption.** Without them, layer-based costing starts from what you supply.
- **Never reports a method comparison as a recommendation to change.**
