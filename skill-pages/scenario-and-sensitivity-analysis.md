# Scenarios and sensitivities: which assumptions matter

> Scenarios, tornado and breakeven, with ranges from observed history.

**Skill:** `scenario-and-sensitivity-analysis`  ·  **Area:** Stock, costing, credit policy and audit preparation  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Tests how a financial outcome changes as assumptions move: best, base and worst scenarios, single- and two-variable sensitivity tables, a tornado ranking of the drivers, breakeven and threshold analysis, and probability weighting, on top of a model or plan you already has. Proposes each driver's range from what that driver has actually done in the books, so the ranking reflects the business and not a blanket plus or minus ten percent. Reads history through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded model and history.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for scenario analysis, sensitivity analysis, a tornado chart, a stress test, a what-if, breakeven, or which assumptions matter most.

Not for:

- Building the forecast itself (use [rolling-forecast](rolling-forecast.md)) or tracking covenants (use [debt-and-covenant-tracking](debt-and-covenant-tracking.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *What decision is this for, and which model or plan does it sit on?* | The analysis needs the model's structure, not only its outputs |
| *Which outcome matters: net income, EBITDA, cash runway, a covenant ratio?* | The choice changes the ranking |
| *Which drivers should vary, and do you have a range in mind for each?* | The drivers are your choice |
| *Do you want named scenarios, and what is the story behind each?* | A coherent scenario is authored, not computed |
| *Any thresholds to test: a covenant level, minimum cash, breakeven? Any probability weights?* | Sets the breakeven and expected-value work |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **The model or plan**, with its assumptions visible | Required | XLSX |
| **Monthly history** for the drivers, 24 months or more | Recommended. Without it ranges are your judgment, and the output says so | XLSX or CSV |
| Scenario definitions, thresholds and weights | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the analysis and a coverage sheet, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Summary of Findings
- Scenarios
- Single-Variable Sensitivities
- Tornado Analysis
- Two-Variable Data Tables
- Threshold / Breakeven
- Probability-Weighted / Monte Carlo
- Driver History and Ranges

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never applies a mechanical range without saying so.** Every range states its source: observed history, or your judgment.
- **Never invents a probability, a distribution or a covenant definition.**
- **Never authors a forward-looking statement.** Scenarios are your stories; the skill computes them.
