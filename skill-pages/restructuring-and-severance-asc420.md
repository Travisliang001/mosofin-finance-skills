# Restructuring: the liability before anyone is paid

> Restructuring accrual and reserve roll-forward, by cost type not by name.

**Skill:** `restructuring-and-severance-asc420`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 38%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports accounting for a restructuring under ASC 420 or IAS 37: identifies the cost types and when each is recognised, builds the accrual for severance, facility exit and contract termination costs, rolls the reserve forward, challenges it against what has actually been paid, and proposes the journal entries and disclosures. Works by cost type and headcount, not by name, wherever the accounting allows.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about a restructuring provision, severance accrual, exit or disposal costs, a restructuring reserve roll-forward, or ASC 420.

Not for:

- Routine accruals (use [accruals-and-deferrals](accruals-and-deferrals.md)) or for payroll entries (use [payroll-journal-entry-builder](payroll-journal-entry-builder.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and ASC 420 or IAS 37?* | The triggers differ in wording |
| *What is the plan: approval date, scope, expected completion?* | Defines what is in the reserve |
| *What was communicated, to whom, when, and how is it evidenced?* | The recognition trigger |
| *What are the affected groups, by headcount and cost, and the severance terms?* | Sizes the accrual. Names only where needed |
| *Is future service required of any group? Which contracts and leases are being exited?* | Decides the recognition pattern and the other cost types |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **The approved plan** | Required | PDF |
| **Cost by group**: headcount, severance terms, dates | Required. By group, not by name | XLSX |
| **Contracts and leases being exited**, with penalties | Required for those costs | PDF or XLSX |
| **Reserve account detail** | Required for the roll-forward | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the accrual model and proposed entries, and handles no more personal data than the standard needs.

- Accrual Model by Employee / Cost Type
- Reserve Rollforward
- JE Package
- Disclosure-Ready Summary
- Reserve Challenge
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never takes or stores names of affected employees unless the accounting requires it**, and never saves them.
- **Never recognises a cost before its trigger is evidenced.** The communication date comes from you.
- **Never decides whether future service is required.** That judgment sets the pattern and is your.
- **Never shares the output beyond the people you name.**
