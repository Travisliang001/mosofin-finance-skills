# Derivatives and hedging: the instrument, the designation, the value

> Instrument inventory, hedge designation checks and entries.

**Skill:** `derivatives-and-hedging-asc815`  ·  **Area:** Specialist technical accounting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 47%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports accounting for derivatives and hedges under ASC 815 or IFRS 9: inventories the instruments, checks whether hedge accounting was elected and documented at inception, records the fair values you supply, sets out effectiveness testing, proposes the journal entries, and prepares disclosures and controls. Runs checks on the books for settlements with no recorded instrument and for amounts left in other comprehensive income.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about derivative accounting, an interest rate swap, a forward or option, hedge accounting, hedge documentation, effectiveness testing, or ASC 815.

Not for:

- Period-end revaluation of foreign balances (use [multicurrency-fx-revaluation](multicurrency-fx-revaluation.md)) or loan covenants (use [debt-and-covenant-tracking](debt-and-covenant-tracking.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and reporting date?* | Sets the valuation date |
| *What derivatives are held: type, notional, dates, counterparty?* | The instruments themselves |
| *Is hedge accounting applied, and what are the designation date and documentation for each relationship?* | Without both, changes in value go to earnings |
| *What is each instrument's fair value at the reporting date, from whom and by what method?* | Never computed here |
| *What are the hedged items, and is any forecast transaction no longer expected?* | Decides what stays in other comprehensive income |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Derivative confirmations** or a summary of terms | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| **Valuations** at the reporting date, with source and method | Required | PDF |
| **Hedge documentation** and the prior period memo | Required if hedge accounting applies | PDF |
| **Ledger detail** for derivative and related accounts | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces schedules and proposed entries, and never values an instrument.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Hedge accounting memo per hedge relationship
- Period-end JE package
- Effectiveness test results
- Disclosure tables
- Exception log

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never values a derivative**, and never takes a recorded balance as a current value.
- **Never assumes hedge accounting applies.** It is elective and needs documentation dated at inception.
- **Never concludes that a hedge is effective.** It sets out the test and the inputs.
