# Related parties: who they are, and what passed between you

> Compile transactions and balances with each related party.

**Skill:** `related-party-disclosures-asc850`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 43%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Prepares related party disclosures under ASC 850 or IAS 24: takes the list of related parties from you, runs completeness procedures against the vendor, customer and employee records, compiles every transaction and balance with each related party from the books, drafts the disclosures, and sets out the controls.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for related party disclosures, a list of related party transactions, a related party completeness check, or balances with directors, shareholders or affiliates.

Not for:

- Intercompany matching (use [intercompany-reconciliation](intercompany-reconciliation.md)) or for the notes as a whole (use [notes-to-financial-statements](notes-to-financial-statements.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and ASC 850 or IAS 24?* | The two differ on key management and close family |
| *Who are the related parties: owners, directors, officers, affiliates, and the family members the framework requires?* | This is the universe, and it is never in the books |
| *What changed since last time: board joiners and leavers, new subsidiaries, share transfers?* | A party can be related for part of the year |
| *Are there guarantees, or transactions at no charge?* | Neither leaves a normal trace in the ledger |
| *Who may see the output?* | It names individuals |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Related party list** | Required | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| **Vendor and customer transaction detail** for the period | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Period-end receivable and payable balances** by party | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Prior year disclosure | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces the schedules and draft disclosure, collects only what the standard requires, and posts nothing.

- Related-Party Register
- Completeness Evidence
- Transactions and Balances
- Disclosure Tables
- Management Representation Draft
- Ledger Candidates
- Coverage and Provenance

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never decides that someone is a related party.** It shows the lead and asks.
- **Never collects personal information beyond what the standard requires**, and saves no family detail.
- **Never judges whether a transaction was at arm's length.**
- **Never treats no transactions as no disclosure.** A relationship can need disclosing on its own.
