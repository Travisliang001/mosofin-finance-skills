# Valuation package: DCF, comps and the football field

> DCF with visible WACC, comparable screens and a football field.

**Skill:** `valuation-agent-dcf-comps-football-field`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 49%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds a valuation package from forecast cash flows and market evidence, with every assumption visible and editable: anchors the valuation date, builds the discounted cash flow, lays out the WACC inputs, calculates terminal value two ways, builds the comparable company and transaction screens, draws the football field, and bridges enterprise value to equity value. Reads the company's net debt items through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from an uploaded model.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a DCF with a WACC build, a comps table, a football field chart, or a valuation package for a deal or a board.

Not for:

- A short valuation range (use [business-valuation](business-valuation.md)) or for testing a model's formulas (use [model-auditor-financial-model-review](model-auditor-financial-model-review.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which business, what valuation date, and for whom?* | The date controls everything; the audience sets whether a specialist is needed |
| *Can you supply the forecast model, with its structure and not only its outputs?* | The cash flows are yours |
| *What are the WACC inputs, with sources and dates?* | Market data |
| *Which comparable companies and transactions should be screened?* | The universe is a judgment |
| *What are the diluted share count and your definition of net debt?* | No cap table in the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Forecast model** | Required | XLSX |
| **WACC inputs** with sources | Required | XLSX |
| **Comparable and transaction data** | Required for the screens | XLSX |
| Balance sheet at the valuation date and cap table | Required for the bridge | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces valuation analysis, not an opinion of value, and supplies no market data.

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Produces valuation analysis, not an opinion of value.** It is not a licensed valuation professional, and a qualified one signs before anyone relies on the figure.
- **Never supplies market data.** Discount rate inputs, multiples, comparables and prices come from you, each with its source and date.
- **Never hides an assumption inside a formula.** Each is shown with its source.
- **Never lets a terminal value pass without showing its share of the total.**
