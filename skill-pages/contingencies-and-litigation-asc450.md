# Contingencies: likely, and can it be measured?

> Apply management's assessment; accrual and draft disclosure.

**Skill:** `contingencies-and-litigation-asc450`  ·  **Area:** Public reporting  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 55%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Evaluates and accounts for contingencies such as litigation, claims, assessments and warranties under ASC 450 or IAS 37: sweeps the books for legal spend and settlements that point to matters missing from the list, classifies each matter on the likelihood and estimate management supplies, sets out the treatment, measures the accrual and proposes the entry, and drafts the disclosure for legal review.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about a loss contingency, a litigation accrual, a legal reserve, a warranty provision, or contingent liability disclosure.

Not for:

- General period-end accruals (use [accruals-and-deferrals](accruals-and-deferrals.md)) or events after year-end (use [subsequent-events-asc855](subsequent-events-asc855.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and US GAAP or IFRS?* | The thresholds differ between the two |
| *What matters are open: name, type, status, counsel?* | The population is not in the ledger |
| *For each matter, what is management's view of likelihood and of the amount or range, and who gave it?* | Never supplied here |
| *Which vendors are your lawyers, and is any matter privileged or restricted?* | Needed for the sweep and for what may be written down |
| *Do you have counsel's letters, the insurance terms and last year's disclosure?* | The support for the assessment |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Matter list** with management's assessment | Required | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| **Ledger detail** for legal expense, accruals and settlements | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| Counsel's letters and insurance terms | Recommended | PDF |
| Prior year disclosure | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

A workpaper you can keep, as an Excel file if you want one or as tables in the chat, with every figure traced to where it came from. Anything that would change the books is a proposal for a person to approve.

Applies management's assessment, never supplies a probability, and posts nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Contingency log with classification and treatment
- Accrual calculation support
- JE package
- Draft disclosure language and checklist

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never supplies a probability or an estimate of loss.**
- **Never infers the likelihood of loss from the amount of legal spend.**
- **Never writes a privileged matter into the workpaper without your say-so.**
- **Never nets an insurance recovery against the liability**, and never records a gain contingency.
