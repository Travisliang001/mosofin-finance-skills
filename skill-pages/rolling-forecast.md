# Rolling forecast: actuals locked, the horizon moved on

> Lock actuals, refresh the horizon, score the last forecast.

**Skill:** `rolling-forecast`  ·  **Area:** Forecasting, customers, credit, debt and fraud risk  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 59%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Builds and refreshes a rolling forecast that always looks twelve or eighteen months ahead: locks the closed months as actuals, updates the drivers, challenges the projection with the business's own run-rate and seasonality, rebuilds the full-year view, scores the last forecast against what happened, and bridges every change since the prior version. Reads actuals through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded monthly results.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for a rolling forecast, a re-forecast, a forecast refresh after month-end, a full-year outlook, or forecast accuracy tracking.

Not for:

- A weekly cash forecast (use [cash-flow-forecast-13-week](cash-flow-forecast-13-week.md)) or for variance against the budget (use [budget-vs-actual-analysis](budget-vs-actual-analysis.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, what horizon (12 or 18 months), and monthly or quarterly?* | Sets the rolling window |
| *Which is the last fully closed month?* | Only closed months are locked as actuals |
| *What are the updated drivers: pipeline and bookings, hiring plan, price changes, known events?* | This is the forecast itself |
| *Do you have the prior forecast and the original budget?* | The change bridge and the accuracy score need them |
| *Any foreign currency, and what rate do you assume?* | A rate is an assumption, never a fact from the books |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly profit and loss by line**, actuals to date, ideally 24 months or more | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Prior forecast** | Recommended. Without it there is no change bridge | XLSX or CSV |
| **Original budget** | Recommended | XLSX or CSV |
| Drivers and assumptions: pipeline, headcount plan, known events | Required for any projection | XLSX, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

The forward assumptions are your, and the skill posts nothing.

- Rolling Forecast Summary
- Monthly Detail
- Current Year Outlook
- Driver Assumptions
- Forecast Bridge
- Forecast Accuracy Tracker
- Scenario Range
- Forecast Challenge
- Coverage and Provenance

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never presents a run-rate as a forecast.** A run-rate is a challenge to the forecast, shown beside it.
- **Never locks a month that is not closed.** Unfinished actuals corrupt the forecast and the accuracy record.
- **Never supplies an FX rate or a forward-looking statement for external guidance.**
