# Intangibles: what has no physical form, and how long it lasts

> Intangibles register, amortisation and what may be capitalised.

**Skill:** `intangibles-and-amortization`  ·  **Area:** Accounting standards  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 59%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Maintains intangible assets and their amortisation: checks that amortisation is being charged at all, identifies the asset classes, sets useful lives and methods from your policy, tests which internal development costs qualify for capitalisation, scans for costs expensed that should be capitalised and the reverse, computes amortisation, and proposes the entry, the roll-forward and the disclosure inputs.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about intangible assets, amortisation, capitalised software or development costs, patents, customer lists, useful lives, or impairment indicators for intangibles.

Not for:

- Tangible assets (use [fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation](fixed-asset-register-and-depreciation.md)) or for prepaid expenses (use [prepaid-amortization-schedule](prepaid-amortization-schedule.md)).

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Intangibles register**: asset, cost, date, life, method | Recommended. The core gap when missing | XLSX |
| **Ledger detail** for the intangible and accumulated amortisation accounts | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Development spend by project**, with stage dates | Required if capitalising development | XLSX |
| Asset class policy and capitalisation policy | Required | PDF, or type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

- GL Tie-Out

- 5-Year Amortization Forecast
- GL Posting
- Capitalization Scan

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.** Every entry is a proposal for a person to post.
- **Never picks a useful life.** The standard ranges are ranges, not a policy.
- **Never capitalises a development cost without the policy and the project stage dates.**
- **Never concludes on impairment.** It lists the readable indicators and leaves the test to you.
