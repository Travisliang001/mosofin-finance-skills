# US sales tax: where you owe it, and whether it reconciles

> Sales by state against thresholds you supply; liability reconciled to returns.

**Skill:** `sales-tax-us-multistate`  ·  **Area:** Tax, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 50%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Supports US sales and use tax across states: joins sales by state from the books to the thresholds and registration status you supply to show where there is exposure, then works through registration, taxability, rates, exemptions, use tax, return preparation, the reconciliation of the liability account to returns, and marketplace sales. Reads sales by ship-to state through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded sales data.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask about sales tax nexus, which states they should be registered in, use tax on purchases, reconciling sales tax payable to returns, or exemption certificates.

Not for:

- VAT or GST (use [vat-gst-international](vat-gst-international.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which entity and period?* | Sets the sales read |
| *In which states are you registered, since when, and where do you have physical presence?* | The join that turns sales into exposure |
| *What are the current economic nexus thresholds and rates, or which tax engine do you use?* | Never built in |
| *What do you sell, and which items have you determined to be taxable or exempt?* | Taxability is a determination |
| *Which exemption certificates are on file, and which marketplaces do you sell through?* | Both reduce what you must collect |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales by ship-to state**, with tax collected | Required | XLSX or CSV |
| **Registration list** with dates | Required | XLSX, or type it in the chat |
| **Thresholds and rates** with source | Required | PDF or XLSX |
| Returns filed and exemption certificate register | Recommended | PDF or XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces workpapers for a qualified professional to review, is not tax advice, and files nothing.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Nexus Map
- Taxability Matrix
- Return Figures by State
- Reconciliation
- Exemption Certificate Register
- Use Tax
- Open Items / Confirmations Needed
- Exposure Summary

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system, and files nothing.** Every entry and every figure is a proposal.
- **Produces computations and illustrations, not tax advice.** A qualified tax professional reviews before anything is filed or acted on.
- **Never supplies a rate, threshold, bracket or rule.** Each comes from you, with its source and its year, and is never carried over from an earlier run.
- **Never concludes that nexus exists.** It shows sales against the threshold supplied and names the states to review.
- **Never decides whether a product is taxable.**
- **Never registers, files or remits.**
