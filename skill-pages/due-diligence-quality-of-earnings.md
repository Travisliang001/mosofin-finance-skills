# Quality of earnings: what the business really earns

> Adjusted EBITDA bridge, working capital and net debt for a deal.

**Skill:** `due-diligence-quality-of-earnings`  ·  **Area:** Valuation and transactions, for a qualified professional to review  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 64%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Performs financial due diligence on a target: validates the base numbers, builds the bridge from reported to adjusted EBITDA with each adjustment evidenced, tests whether one-time items recur, assesses revenue quality, working capital with a normal level, net debt and debt-like items, maintenance against growth capex, and the forecast, and lists the risks. Reads the target's books through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree and the target is connected, or works from the data room files you upload.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask for quality of earnings, adjusted or normalised EBITDA, a working capital peg, net debt for a deal, or financial due diligence.

Not for:

- Valuing the business (use [business-valuation](business-valuation.md)) or freeing cash from working capital in your own company (use [working-capital-optimization](working-capital-optimization.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which target, and is this buy-side, sell-side or refinancing?* | Sets the lens |
| *What is the cut-off date for the last twelve months, and which years are covered?* | Sets every read |
| *Do you have the ledger and trial balances, or only the statements?* | Decides how deep the work can go |
| *What adjustments has management proposed?* | The starting bridge to test |
| *Who are the related parties, and what are the owner's arrangements?* | Essential in a private company |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial statements**, three years plus the last twelve months | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Trial balances and ledger** | Required for depth | XLSX or CSV |
| **Management's adjusted EBITDA bridge** | Recommended | XLSX |
| Monthly working capital and the debt schedule | Required for those sections | XLSX |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces analysis for the deal team, not an audit and not investment advice.

The workpaper has these sheets:

- Executive Summary
- Adjusted EBITDA Bridge
- Recurring "One-Time" Test
- Revenue Quality
- Margin Analysis
- Working Capital Analysis
- Net Debt and Debt-Like Items
- Capex Analysis
- Forecast Assessment
- Quality Observations & Risks

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Not an audit, and not a recommendation to buy, sell or lend.**
- **Never accepts a management adjustment without evidence**, and treats adjustments the same way in both directions.
- **Never calls an item one-time that recurs.** The recurrence test is run first.
- **Never sets the working capital peg.** It shows the average, the seasonality and the methods.
