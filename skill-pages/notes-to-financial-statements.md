# Notes to the accounts: what the numbers mean

> Disclosure checklist and draft notes, with every gap flagged.

**Skill:** `notes-to-financial-statements`  ·  **Area:** Costing, notes, segments and modelling  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 51%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Drafts and reviews the notes to the financial statements: builds the disclosure checklist for the framework and the entity, drafts the accounting policy notes and the account-detail notes from the figures, prepares commitments, contingencies, related party and subsequent events notes for a person to complete, then checks completeness and that every figure agrees with the statements.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to draft or review notes or footnotes to the accounts, a disclosure checklist, an accounting policies note, or to check that notes agree with the statements.

Not for:

- Building the statements themselves (use [financial-statement-builder](financial-statement-builder.md)) or for the audit request list (use [audit-prep-pbc-list](audit-prep-pbc-list.md)).

## What it asks you first

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company, which period, and when will the accounts be issued?* | The issue date ends the subsequent events window |
| *Which framework, and is the entity public or private?* | Decides the whole checklist and its volume |
| *Which accounting policies are in use, and what are the significant judgments and estimates?* | Not in the data |
| *Are there commitments, contingencies or related parties, and who performs legal review?* | Legal territory; relationships are not in the books |
| *Do you have last year's notes and the supporting schedules for debt, leases and fixed assets?* | For consistency and the detail notes |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Financial statements** for the period and the comparative | Required | XLSX or PDF |
| **Trial balance** at the period end | Required for the detail notes | XLSX or CSV |
| **Schedules**: debt, leases, fixed assets | Required for those notes | XLSX |
| Prior year notes | Recommended | PDF |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Produces draft notes with every gap flagged, and issues nothing.

- Disclosure Checklist
- Supporting Data References
- Tie-Out
- Open Items
- Subsequent Events Candidates
- Policy Consistency
- Coverage

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never writes, updates or deletes anything, in any system.**
- **Never states an accounting policy you have not confirmed.**
- **Never drafts a legal conclusion on a contingency.** That is for whoever performs legal review.
- **Never fills a gap with plausible wording.** A missing disclosure is flagged where it belongs.
- **Never treats the checklist as complete without the framework and whether the entity is public or private.**
