# AP invoice processing: from intake to ready-to-pay

> The whole AP invoice process; wider than [three-way-match](three-way-match.md).

**Skill:** `ap-invoice-processing-and-3way-match`  ·  **Area:** Further payables, receivables and compliance skills  ·  **Automated with Mosofin connected:** 52%  ·  **Read-only**

## What it does

Designs and runs the accounts payable invoice workflow end to end: intake and hygiene, vendor and compliance checks, field validation, two-way or three-way match within the tolerance you set, exception handling, proposed coding and posting, and the audit trail, with the approval matrix applied. Says plainly whether a two-way or a three-way match was possible. Reads bills and purchase orders through a connected Mosofin workspace when you agree, or works from uploaded invoices and orders.

## When to use it

Use it when you ask to set up or review the AP invoice process, define approval routing and coding standards, process a batch of supplier invoices through all checks, or write an AP procedure.

Not for:

- The match alone (use [three-way-match](three-way-match.md)), for extracting fields (use [invoice-data-extractor](invoice-data-extractor.md)) or for duplicates alone (use [duplicate-invoice-detection](duplicate-invoice-detection.md)).

## What it asks you first

Before it reads anything, it asks, in one message:

| It asks | Why |
|---|---|
| *Use Mosofin as the data source for this run, or upload the data yourself?* | Decides the path. Mosofin is the connection to your books and other linked platforms; with it the skill reads from there, without it you upload and the checks are the same |
| *Which company and period, and are these bills already entered, or invoices not yet in the books?* | Decides what can be read |
| *Where are receipts logged?* | Without them the match is two-way |
| *What tolerance applies, as a percentage and an amount?* | Decides what is an exception |
| *Who may approve what, up to which amount?* | The books do not record it |
| *Is this a design of the process, a run of a batch, or both?* | A written procedure and a batch run are different outputs |

## With or without Mosofin

With a Mosofin workspace connected, and only after you agree, the skill reads the figures from your own books. Without a connection it runs the same analysis on files you upload, and the output says which figures came from where.

If you upload the data yourself:

| Upload | Needed? | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Invoices** for the period | Required | PDF, XLSX or CSV |
| **Purchase orders** with lines | Required for matching | XLSX or CSV |
| **Receiving records** | Required for a three-way match | XLSX or CSV |
| Approval matrix and tolerances | Recommended | Type it in the chat |

Every uploaded file is checked before any work starts: that it is the right kind of document, for the right company and period, and usable.

## What you get

Proposes coding and exceptions, and posts and pays nothing.

- SOP with roles, SLAs, evidence
- Match rules and tolerance table
- Exception matrix
- Recommended entries and controls
- KPIs

Every run ends with a coverage sheet: what was checked, what was estimated, and what could not be done and why.

## What it never does

- **Asks before using Mosofin.** A connected account is not permission to read it.
- **Never posts, approves, pays or holds an invoice, in any system.** Every step is a proposal.
- **Never invents a tolerance or an approval limit.**
- **Never presents a two-way match as a three-way match.**
- **Never treats an attachment as an approval**, or a change to bank details as routine.
