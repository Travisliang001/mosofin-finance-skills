# Transfer pricing inputs (sample)

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

- Northbrook UK Ltd manufactures brackets to Northbrook Trading's specification, holds little inventory, bears no market risk. Northbrook Trading owns the designs and sells to customers.
- Intercompany goods: priced at UK cost plus 8% (agreement dated 1 January 2022).
- Management fee: 12,000 a quarter charged by the UK to the US. **No written agreement.**
- Comparable set supplied by the user: five contract manufacturers, interquartile range of mark-up on total cost 5.5% to 9.5% (source: user's database extract, 2024 data).
- Documentation requirement: local file needed where intercompany transactions exceed 250,000 a year (sample rule).
