## Coverage and Provenance sheet (mandatory)

**Section A — Environment**
- Workspace name(s) (never the numeric id)
- **Every company file read, by `display_name`**, and **which group entities could not be read**
- Jurisdictions in scope
- Years read
- **Which flows were read from both sides and which from one**
- **Fixture flag** — any `mock: true`? In bold.

**Section B — Capability map as observed today**
**Per entity**: tools used, `effective_policy`, resulting verdict. Note where policies differed between entities.

**Section C — Steps by verdict**
Every step. **A reader should see that Steps 2 and 5 were entirely human.**

**Section D — What was not done, and why**
- **The functional analysis** — **functions, assets and risks are facts about how work is done**
- **Comparables and the arm's-length range** — **commercial databases; not on this surface**
- **Jurisdiction thresholds, deadlines, safe harbours** — current-sourced
- **Intercompany agreements** — documents
- **Flows involving entities outside the workspace**
- **Free use of group intangibles**, which generates no transaction
- **Guarantees**, where no fee is charged
- **Employee numbers for CbCR**
- **Borrower credit ratings** for financing benchmarking
- **DEMPE analysis, restructuring exit charges, profit split allocations**
- **The arm's-length conclusion — a TP specialist's**
- **Filing.** The gateway is read-only

**Section E — Unknowns carried forward**
Flows without an identified agreement. Agreements not matching conduct. Loss-making entities without a documented defence. Entities whose characterisation the numbers contradict. Interest-free intercompany balances. Approaching CbCR thresholds and documentation deadlines.

**Section F — Parameters used**
Entities and years read. PLI definitions and segmentation basis. Comparability adjustments applied. Method selected. **A TP position is defended years later against the documentation as filed**, so reproducibility is the point.
