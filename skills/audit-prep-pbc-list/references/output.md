## Output Quality Standards

All of the original standards, plus the Mosofin ones.

- PBC list tailored to the entity's areas, scaled by materiality and risk
- Each item specifies the tie-out and support standard
- Readiness assessed, with gaps and owners identified
- Foundational items (TB, reconciliations) sequenced first
- Judgmental-area memos planned and tracked
- Prior-year adjustments and management letter points followed up
- Realistic timeline to fieldwork
- Cross-references the close and reconciliation skills that produce the support
- File naming consistent
- **No requests irrelevant to the entity's actual operations**

**Mosofin additions:**

- The workspace was confirmed **by name** and the user said yes before any data was read
- Every in-scope company file is named by `display_name`; every excluded one is named **as
  excluded**, with the readiness consequence stated
- The capability map was discovered **this run** via Gate 2 — never recalled from a previous
  conversation or from this file
- Every PBC item carries its verdict (`[auto]` / `[gated]` / `[manual]`), the tool used, and
  that tool's policy, in the coverage sheet
- **Every `[manual]` item has a named human owner** — an item with a gap and no owner will not
  get prepared
- **Every `[auto]` schedule records its provenance**: tool, parameters, as-of timestamp, `mock`
  status
- **No near-substitute is handed over as the requested schedule** — a balance report is not an
  aging, and a total is not a reconciliation
- A customer or vendor list is never presented as a related-party listing
- A book balance is never presented as a confirmed balance
- `mock` status is reported wherever it applies, and **no schedule resting on mock data is
  offered as audit evidence**
- The readiness summary reports the critical path in a group audit, not a blended average
- Every figure traces to a tool result in this conversation or to labelled user-supplied
  evidence; the answer ends with a single **Data sources** line
- Every jargon term carries a plain-language gloss at first mention, with the professional term
  kept alongside
- No internal numeric ids and no raw `data_source_id` values appear in anything the user sees
- No audited figures, schedules, or client documents are persisted into a skill bundle; every
  persisted preference states the datasource and `display_name` it covers
- Nothing was written back to any system — every schedule and memo is a proposal for a human to
  review, approve, and deliver
