# Term loan — summary of terms

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

Lender: Calder Bank. Original principal 580,000. Repayment 5,000 a month plus interest. Rate: fixed 5.7%. Matures 31 December 2029.

**Covenants, tested quarterly on the trailing twelve months:**
1. *Leverage*: Total Debt / Covenant EBITDA not above 2.50x.
2. *Interest cover*: Covenant EBITDA / Interest Expense not below 4.0x.

**Definitions.** *Covenant EBITDA* = net income + interest + depreciation, **plus** one-time professional fees up to 15,000 a year, **less** any gain on asset sales. *Total Debt* = term loan + corporate card balance + any lease liabilities.
Compliance certificate due 45 days after each quarter end.
