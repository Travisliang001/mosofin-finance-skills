## Output Quality Standards

**The original has no Output Quality Standards section. Everything below is a Mosofin addition**, drawn
from the original's own workflow and outputs and from the workspace model.

**From the original workflow and outputs:**

- **Gaps are assessed against public-company requirements** across all six workstreams: close speed and
  discipline, audit and PCAOB readiness, ICFR / SOX, accounting memos, disclosure readiness, and equity
  and EPS
- **A close acceleration plan** covers standardised reconciliations, automated recurring entries, tightened
  cut-off, improved subledger-to-GL tie-outs, and a close calendar with RACI
- **A memo library is prioritised** across revenue, stock compensation, leases, business combinations,
  income taxes, and debt and derivatives
- **Equity and EPS readiness** covers cap table validation, complex instruments, 409A history and a diluted
  EPS model
- **SOX / ICFR readiness** covers process and control identification, narratives and walkthroughs,
  segregation-of-duties remediation, and evidence retention
- **The reporting process** covers tie-out discipline, disclosure checklists, and a review and certification
  workflow
- The deliverables include the **roadmap with timeline, the workstream deliverables and owners, the close
  calendar and RACI, the memo library index, and the SOX readiness plan with its evidence framework**

**Mosofin additions:**

- The workspace was confirmed **by name** and the user said yes before any data was read
- **The intended registrant is identified by `display_name`**, every excluded entity is named **as
  excluded**, and **any pending reorganisation is stated**
- The capability map was discovered **this run** via Gate 2 — never recalled from a previous conversation
  or from this file
- **Close speed is measured, not asserted**, with the working-day distribution and the last adjusting
  entry's date, across several periods
- **The manual-entry profile is measured** by count and value, and **used to size the ICFR workstream**
- **Comparable-data depth is measured** against the years the filing requires, with any discontinuity named
- **Period integrity is tested** — entries into reported periods, prior-period adjustments, and comparatives
  that no longer match what was presented
- **Suspense and uncategorized balances are reported with ages**
- **Every gap is stated with a size and a basis**, never as a colour or a rating alone
- **The memo library is scoped from the accounts**, with areas ruled out and the evidence for ruling them out
- **The equity workstream is flagged as `[manual]` and as the most common cause of delay**, with existing
  complex instruments flagged from the balance sheet
- **Segregation-of-duties observations are surfaced as patterns, never as conclusions**
- **Close acceleration is sequenced by what actually holds the close open**, per the measured last-entry
  dates
- In a multi-entity run, **close speed is measured per entity**, the group close is recognised as bounded by
  the slowest subsidiary, consolidation is treated as a workstream, and **comparable-data depth is checked
  across the group**
- Every task carries its verdict (`[auto]` / `[gated]` / `[manual]`), the tool or external source used, and
  its as-at date, in the coverage sheet, **with `[manual]` workstreams naming owners**
- `mock` status is reported wherever it applies, and **no timetable or budget rests on mock data**
- Every measurement traces to a tool result in this conversation or to labelled user-supplied evidence; the
  answer ends with a single **Data sources** line
- Every jargon term carries a plain-language gloss at first mention, with the professional term kept
  alongside
- No internal numeric ids and no raw `data_source_id` values appear in anything the user sees
- **No financial figures, measurements, cap table data, valuations or IPO-timetable specifics are
  persisted** into a skill bundle — **an IPO plan is material non-public information**; every persisted
  preference states the datasource and `display_name` it covers
- File naming consistent
- Nothing was written back to any system — no process changed, no control remediated, nothing filed
