## Output Quality Standards

*The original did not carry a Quality Standards section; these are Mosofin additions, plus the standards
implied by the workflow.*

- Every matter carries its **probability and estimate as supplied**, with **who supplied them and when**
- **No probability, range or outcome is inferred by this skill** from any ledger signal
- The two accrual tests — **probable** *and* **reasonably estimable** — are assessed separately, and a
  probable-but-not-estimable matter is disclosed rather than accrued
- Where a range was used, the output states whether the **best estimate** or the **minimum of the range**
  was accrued, and why
- **The accrual proposed is the movement**, computed against the balance already carried
- Insurance recoveries are assessed separately and **not netted** unless permitted and realisable; the
  gross exposure is shown
- **The completeness sweep was run and its result stated** — including when it found nothing, and
  including when it could not be run
- Unmatched signals are raised as **questions**, never as assertions that a matter exists
- Disclosure language contains nothing the assessment did not supply, and names no restricted matter,
  counterparty or counsel
- The framework is stated, and **IFRS matters apply IAS 37's own thresholds**, not US GAAP's
- The workspace was confirmed **by name** and the user said yes before any data was read
- Every in-scope company file is named by `display_name`; every excluded one is named **as excluded**
- The capability map was discovered **this run** via Gate 2 — never recalled from a previous conversation
  or from this file
- Every task carries its verdict (`[auto]` / `[gated]` / `[manual]`), the tool used, and that tool's
  policy, in the coverage sheet
- Each matter states **which legal entity bears it**; group exposure is never presented without the
  per-entity breakdown
- `mock` status is reported wherever it applies, and no accrual or disclosure rests on mock data
- Every figure traces to a tool result in this conversation or to labelled user-supplied evidence; the
  answer ends with a single **Data sources** line
- Every jargon term carries a plain-language gloss at first mention, with the professional term kept
  alongside
- No internal numeric ids and no raw `data_source_id` values appear in anything the user sees
- **No matter narratives, counterparty names, counsel advice, probabilities, estimates or ranges are
  persisted** into a skill bundle; every persisted preference states the datasource and `display_name`
  it covers
- Nothing was written back to any system — every accrual and disclosure is a proposal for management,
  counsel and the CFO to approve
