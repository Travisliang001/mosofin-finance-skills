# Contingencies and Litigation (ASC 450 / IAS 37) — Mosofin

Provides a structured approach to **evaluate, account for, and disclose contingencies** — litigation,
claims, assessments, warranties — based on **likelihood** and **estimability**.

**In plain words:** sometimes you might have to pay out for something that hasn't been settled yet — a
lawsuit, a warranty claim, a tax assessment. The accounting question is not "will we lose?" but "how
likely is it, and can we put a number on it?" If it's likely *and* measurable, you put it in the
accounts. If it's only possible, you describe it in the notes. If it's remote, usually neither.

It is **workspace-scoped**: existing accruals, settlements paid and legal spend come from tool calls
against a company file connected to your Mosofin workspace in this conversation, or from something you
supplied by hand and that is labelled as such.

## What this skill will and will not do from a connected workspace

**A hard limit first, because it governs everything else.**

> **Mosofin will not assess the probability of a legal outcome, and will not estimate a loss.** Those
> are legal and management judgments with real consequences — for the financial statements, for
> privilege, and occasionally for the litigation itself. **The probability and the estimate come from
> management and outside counsel, always `[manual]`.** This skill structures the framework, records the
> assessments it is given, computes the resulting accounting, and drafts the disclosure. It does not
> form a view on whether you will lose.

That said, a connected workspace makes one genuinely valuable contribution, and it happens to address
the hardest assertion in this area:

> **Completeness.** The risk with contingencies is not usually mis-measuring the matters on the list —
> it is matters that never reach the list. **Legal spend is visible in the ledger.** A law firm being
> paid steadily, a new firm appearing, a sharp rise in professional fees, a settlement payment, an
> insurance recovery received — each is a signal of a matter that may exist. **Cross-check the legal
> spend against the contingency log and report anything generating fees that nobody has listed.**

Also `[auto]`: what is already accrued, what has already been paid out, and how prior-period accruals
have moved.

**Mosofin is read-only.** Every accrual and disclosure below is a *proposal* for management, counsel
and the CFO to approve.
