## Edge Cases

All twelve preserved, in order.

**First year as a public company**: **significantly more complex than a private year-end. Plan early.** `[manual]`. See an IPO readiness review and the 10-Q and 10-K preparation — and note the ICFR obligation in Step 3 may apply for the first time.

**Acquisition during the year**: **the target's year-end activity is included from the acquisition date; opening balance sheet at fair value; PPA finalised within the measurement period, typically one year.** `[manual]` — the purchase price allocation needs valuations. the business combination accounting. **`[auto]` for one fact**: whether the acquired entity is separately connected, which determines whether its figures can be read at all.

**IPO or major financing during the year**: **pro-forma disclosures, additional statements.** `[manual]`.

**Change in fiscal year**: **short period filing, transition disclosures.** `[manual]`. **`[auto]` to detect** — a fiscal calendar that does not match the prior year's is worth confirming rather than assuming a data error.

**Going-concern doubt**: **significant disclosure work; may impact the audit opinion.** `[manual]`. **Flag early** — it changes the audit and the timetable, not just a note.

**Auditor changes mid-year**: **new auditor coordination, transition documents.** `[manual]`. **`[auto]` matters more here**: a new auditor asks for everything from scratch, and a pre-assembled PBC is worth considerably more in a first-year engagement.

**Material restatement**: **prior year statements re-issued; the current year's filing references restated comparatives.** `[manual]` to conclude; **`[auto]` to detect** — Step 2's restatement block. a restatement review.

**Litigation resolved during the year changing prior-period estimates**: **change in estimate versus error correction — a judgment call.** `[manual]`, **and the distinction turns on what was known at the time**, which is not in any ledger. The same structure as the recognised/non-recognised distinction in the subsequent events review.

**Subsequent events bracket**: **from year-end through report issuance, can be 60–90 days. Continuous monitoring.** **`[auto]`, and this is where re-running is cheap and valuable** — the window is long, the review is normally done once, and the sweep can run the day before issuance. the subsequent events review.

**Year-end falls on a non-business day**: **the cutoff is still the calendar date; weekend operations may shift.** `[auto]` to identify transactions dated within the year and entered afterwards — the cutoff population. See `revenue-cutoff-testing`.

**Cross-border tax filings with different fiscal years**: **each jurisdiction's deadlines tracked separately.** `[manual]`; Sheet 8.

**Compliance with new accounting standards effective at year-end**: **transition adoption disclosures.** `[manual]`. **`[auto]` for one supporting read**: whether a transition adjustment was posted to opening equity, and for how much — which the statement of changes in equity requires to be shown as its own line.
