## Coverage and Provenance sheet (mandatory)

**Section A — Environment**
- Workspace name (never the numeric id)
- Company file `display_name`(s) (never the `data_source_id`) — **and whether the figures are entity or consolidated**
- Fiscal year end and framework; audit type and firm
- **Which of the twelve months were verified closed**
- **Lock date, and the date the lock was last verified**
- Materiality applied
- **Fixture flag** — any `mock: true`? In bold: **a PBC assembled from fixture data would be handed to an audit team as the client's records.**

**Section B — Capability map as observed today**
Each tool used: name as returned by `get_datasource_tools`, `effective_policy`, resulting verdict. Note every divergence from this file's defaults — **that divergence is what next year's evolved version should absorb.**

**Section C — Steps by verdict**
Every step and **every one of Step 2's fifteen blocks**, with the verdict as it actually applied. Count them.

**Section D — What was not done, and why**
For a year-end this is a long list, and it should be:

- **Bank, AR, debt and legal confirmations** — **third-party evidence obtained by the auditor; not on this surface**
- **Physical asset and inventory counts** — **only counting says what is there**
- **The payroll register**, and therefore the GL-to-register reconciliation's other half
- **Actuarial reports** — pension, OPEB, self-insurance
- **Valuations** — goodwill impairment, investments, purchase price allocation
- **The cap table** — share counts, forfeiture rates, stock compensation
- **Deferred tax attributes** — NOLs, credits, carryforwards; **not ledger balances**
- **Debt maturity dates and facility headroom** — in the agreements
- **Litigation exposure** — attorney letters
- **RPO and backlog** — unbilled commitments
- **Commitments and contingencies** — not transactions
- **The going-concern conclusion** — management's
- **The management representation letter** — management's
- **The audit** — the auditor's
- **Acceptance of audit adjustments** — the entity's and its auditor's
- **Every filing.** The gateway is read-only

**Section E — Unknowns carried forward**
Months not verified closed. Post-lock entries unresolved. Accounts above materiality without a lead schedule. Estimates whose rate has not moved. Prior-year comparative differences. **These are Sheet 10, and Sheet 10 is next year's starting point.**

**Section F — Parameters used**
Materiality. Periods read. Accounts mapped to lead schedules. Lock date. Consolidation basis. **Next year's close must be comparable to this one**, and comparability is what makes Step 6's process improvement measurable.
