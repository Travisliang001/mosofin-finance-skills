## Output Quality Standards

**The original has no Output Quality Standards section. Everything below is a Mosofin addition**, drawn
from the original's own workflow requirements and from the workspace model.

**From the original workflow:**

- The **organizational boundary** is stated, with the **equity share vs. control** approach named
- The **operational boundary** is stated by **Scope 1 / 2 / 3 category**
- The **period and frequency** are stated
- Every metric has a **definition and explicit units**
- Every metric has a **named owner and source system**
- Every metric has **documented calculation steps**
- Every metric has **data quality controls** designed
- The pipeline covers **extraction, transformation, review and approval, change management and version
  control, and evidence retention**
- The report includes **tables and trends, a methodology and limitations statement**, and framework
  alignment where one applies
- The deliverables include the **metric catalog and data dictionary, collection templates, the control
  checklist, and the reporting calendar with owners**

**Mosofin additions:**

- The workspace was confirmed **by name** and the user said yes before any data was read
- Every in-scope company file is named by `display_name`; every excluded one is named **as excluded**; the
  ESG boundary is stated and **any difference from the financial boundary is explained**
- The capability map was discovered **this run** via Gate 2 — never recalled from a previous conversation
  or from this file
- **No spend figure is presented as a physical quantity.** Every spend-derived metric is labelled as
  estimated, with its data-quality tier
- **Every emission factor carries its source, version and year**, and none was supplied from memory
- **Every spend-based input records its currency**, and factors are keyed to entity and jurisdiction
- **Spend used in the ESG calculation reconciles to operating expenditure per the P&L**, with differences
  explained
- Year-on-year movements in spend-derived metrics **separate price from volume**, or say that they cannot
- Metrics the entity cannot yet produce are **catalogued in full with owners**, not omitted
- In a multi-entity run, the consolidation approach is applied consistently and **intercompany spend is
  handled deliberately** for the reporting unit chosen
- Every metric carries its verdict (`[auto]` / `[gated]` / `[manual]`), the tool or external source used,
  and its as-at date, in the coverage sheet
- `mock` status is reported wherever it applies, and **no disclosed figure rests on mock data**
- Every figure traces to a tool result in this conversation or to labelled user-supplied evidence; the
  answer ends with a single **Data sources** line
- Every jargon term carries a plain-language gloss at first mention, with the professional term kept
  alongside
- No internal numeric ids and no raw `data_source_id` values appear in anything the user sees
- **No published metric values, emission totals or supplier-level environmental data are persisted** into a
  skill bundle; every persisted preference states the datasource and `display_name` it covers
- File naming consistent
- Nothing was written back to any system — no metric published, no disclosure submitted
