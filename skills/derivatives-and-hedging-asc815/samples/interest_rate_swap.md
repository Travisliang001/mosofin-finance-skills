# Interest rate swap (sample)

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

Counterparty: Calder Bank. Trade date 1 January 2025. Notional 200,000. Company pays fixed 5.2%, receives floating. Matures 31 December 2029.
Bank valuation at 30 September 2025: liability of 3,400 (bank mark-to-market statement dated 1 October 2025; method: discounted cash flow on the bank's curve).
Hedge documentation: designation memo for a cash flow hedge of the term loan, **dated 14 February 2025**.
Ledger: derivative liability recorded 2,900; other comprehensive income debit 2,900.
Framework ASC 815. Note: the term loan is fixed rate (see the loan summary).
