# Personal Tax Return Preparation (Mosofin)

Organizes and computes an **individual income tax return** — gathering income sources, applying deductions,
reliefs and credits, computing taxable income and tax, and producing **return-ready figures with a document
checklist**. Helps individuals and their advisors prepare and review.

**In plain words:** everything a person earned in a year, from every source, brought together; then the
things the law lets them subtract; then the tax on what remains, less what has already been paid.

## Two rules govern this skill, and both come before the workflow

**First — the scope boundary.**

> **A Mosofin workspace holds business accounting records. It does not hold a person's tax affairs.**
>
> **Employment income from an unrelated employer, personal investments, capital gains, pensions, and
> personal deductions are all `[manual]`.** They come from statements, brokers and the taxpayer.
>
> **The workspace is relevant in exactly one situation: where the taxpayer owns or draws from a business
> whose books are connected.** Then it supplies several figures the return needs, precisely — and that is a
> genuinely useful contribution, because those are often the figures the taxpayer is least able to produce
> from memory.

**Second — the jurisdiction rule, which is identical to the payroll tax filings and equally absolute.**

> **This skill does NOT hardcode tax rates, brackets, allowances, or thresholds** — these vary by
> jurisdiction and year and are **inputs confirmed against current official sources.**
>
> **None of them are on this surface.** **Never supply a rate, a band, a personal allowance, a threshold or
> a deadline from memory** — not as a default, not as an illustration. **A remembered band applied to a real
> return produces a real underpayment.**

**And the professional-review point, which the original states and which bears repeating:** **personal tax
has significant individual circumstances and legal consequences; this skill organizes and computes, and
recommends review by a qualified tax professional before filing.** **Mosofin cannot file anything**, and
should not be the last set of eyes on a return.

## Privacy — stricter here than anywhere else in the pack

**This skill assembles one person's complete financial picture**: what they earn, what they own, what they
owe, their household, sometimes their health spending and their charitable giving.

- **Confirm at Gate 0 who is asking and whose return this is.** **A workspace may belong to a business with
  several owners**, and **one owner's personal tax position must never be visible to another.** If the
  relationship is not clear, ask.
- **Read only what the return needs.** Owner-specific business figures, not the whole ledger's personal
  detail.
- **Never persist anything from this skill.** See Step 11 — **the persistence rule here is "nothing", not
  "nothing sensitive".**
- **Do not compile.** Business data plus personal documents plus prior returns is exactly the aggregation
  that should stay in the taxpayer's own hands.
