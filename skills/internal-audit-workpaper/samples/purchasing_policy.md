# Purchasing policy

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

1. Every purchase over 1,000 needs a purchase order approved **before** the order is placed.
2. Approval limits: managers up to 10,000; finance director up to 50,000; CEO above.
3. Goods must be receipted before the invoice is paid.
4. A change to vendor bank details must be verified by telephone on a known number.
5. One person may not both create a vendor and approve its payment.

Audit performed by internal audit, who did not operate this area. Completeness is out of scope.
