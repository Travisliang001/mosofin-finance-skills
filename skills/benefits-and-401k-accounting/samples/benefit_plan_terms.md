# Benefit plan terms

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

**401(k):** employees contribute 4% of gross; employer matches 100% of the first 4%. Match vests over three years (33% a year).
**Remittance:** employee contributions are due to the plan within 7 business days of each pay date.
**Paid time off:** 15 days a year, accrued monthly, carried forward up to 5 days, paid out on leaving.
