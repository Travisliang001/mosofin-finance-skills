## Edge Cases

All ten preserved, in order.

**Single reportable segment**: **still must provide entity-wide disclosures — products and services, geography, major customers. The segment note is brief but the entity-wide disclosures remain.** `[auto]` for the major-customer test and the product split. **This is the commonest omission among smaller filers**, precisely because "we only have one segment" feels like it settles the matter. It does not.

**Matrix organisation** (managed by both product and geography): **the CODM's primary view governs. If genuinely dual, the entity picks the basis that best reflects the core principle.** `[manual]`. `[auto]` for one relevant fact: **whether the book carries both dimensions**, which determines whether either view is even producible from the data.

**Segment measure differs from GAAP**: **allowed and common. The reconciliation to consolidated GAAP income bridges the gap. Disclose the basis of the measure.** `[manual]` for the basis; `[auto]` for the bridge.

**Intersegment pricing**: **disclose the basis — market price, cost-plus. Intersegment revenue is eliminated in the reconciliation to consolidated.** `[manual]` for the policy; `[auto]` for the amounts where coded. See the transfer pricing documentation where the entities are in different tax jurisdictions — the same transactions carry a tax obligation as well as a disclosure one.

**Changes in segment structure**: **restate prior comparatives unless impracticable. Disclose the change.** `[auto]` to determine practicability from whether the prior detail survives.

**A segment reportable last year but not this year**: **continue reporting it if management believes it is of continuing significance.** `[manual]` — a judgment. `[auto]` to flag that the situation has arisen, which is easy to miss when attention is on this year's tests.

**A new segment crossing thresholds this year**: **report it and restate comparatives.** `[auto]` to detect the crossing; `[auto]` to restate where the prior detail supports it.

**Asymmetric allocations**: **if the CODM is allocated costs or assets to segments differently than GAAP, report as the CODM sees it and reconcile.** `[manual]`. **The reconciliation absorbs the difference**, and this is a case where the ledger's own allocation may be the GAAP one while the CODM's report uses another — in which case the ledger slice is *not* the segment figure. Ask.

**Discontinued operations**: **typically excluded from segment results once classified as discontinued; disclose separately.** `[manual]` for the classification. `[auto]` to exclude where the component is a dimension.

**Goodwill by segment / reporting unit**: for impairment testing, **goodwill is assigned to reporting units, which relate to but are not identical to segments.** `[manual]`, and the distinction matters: **a reporting unit is generally at or one level below an operating segment**, so the two structures are related and must not be conflated.
