# Scenario request

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

Decision: whether to take a second packing line in 2026. Model: the twelve-month forecast (uploaded). Output metric: cash at 30 September 2026 and minimum cash in the year.
Drivers to vary: revenue growth, gross margin, DSO, payroll. Ranges: propose from history.
Scenarios: 'Base'; 'Steel shock' (margin down 3 points for six months); 'Lost customer' (Harlow leaves in January).
Threshold: minimum cash 250,000. No probability weights.
