# Scenario request

*Sample document for testing. Fictitious company and figures.*

Decision: whether to take a second packing line in 2026. Model: the twelve-month forecast (uploaded). Output metric: cash at 30 September 2026 and minimum cash in the year.
Drivers to vary: revenue growth, gross margin, DSO, payroll. Ranges: propose from history.
Scenarios: 'Base'; 'Steel shock' (gross margin down 3 points, October 2025 to March 2026); 'Lost customer' (Harlow Fabrication leaves on 1 January 2026; its revenue is about 62,000 a month).
The forecast has no cash lines: start from cash at 30 September 2025 on the balance sheet and bridge using the forecast's profit plus depreciation, less loan repayments of 5,000 a month.
Threshold: minimum cash 250,000. No probability weights.
