## Coverage and Provenance sheet (mandatory)

**Section A — Environment**
- Workspace name (never the numeric id)
- Company file `display_name`(s) (never the `data_source_id`) — **all entities swept**
- **Balance sheet date; window end date; the definition used**
- **Date the sweep was first run, and the date of the final run**
- Framework: ASC 855 or IAS 10
- **Fixture flag** — any `mock: true`? In bold: **the headline output of this skill is often "nothing found", and fixture data guarantees it.**

**Section B — Capability map as observed today**
Each tool used: name as returned by `get_datasource_tools`, `effective_policy`, resulting verdict.

**Section C — Steps by verdict**
Every step and **every one of the five Step 2 procedures**, with its verdict and owner.

**Section D — What was not done, and why**
- **Board minutes and legal letter updates** — **not on this surface**
- **Management enquiry** — a conversation
- **The classification of every event** — **turns on when a condition existed, which is a fact about the world**
- **The materiality judgment** on non-recognised events
- **Disclosure drafting and approval**
- **Any Type 2 event** — **structurally outside a transactional sweep**
- **Posting.** The gateway is read-only

**Section E — Unknowns carried forward**
Candidates surfaced but not classified. Estimates not yet tested against post-period activity. Enquiry categories not yet covered. The gap between the last sweep and the issuance date, if any remains.

**Section F — Parameters used**
Window dates. Accounts and transaction types swept. Patterns tested. Entities included. **A subsequent events review is re-performed by auditors**, and it must be reproducible.
