## Output Quality Standards

All of the original standards, plus the Mosofin ones.

- **Over-time recognition criterion confirmed per contract**
- Performance obligations identified; transaction price allocated
- **Measure of progress chosen and applied appropriately** (inefficiencies excluded; uninstalled
  materials handled)
- **Cumulative revenue and gross profit computed; catch-up adjustments applied on estimate changes (not
  retrospective)**
- **Loss contracts identified and the entire loss recognized immediately**
- Variable consideration applied **within the constraint**
- **WIP schedule shows contract assets and liabilities per contract (not netted across contracts)**
- Modifications tracked with approval status
- Backlog disclosure prepared
- File naming consistent
- **No deferral of recognized losses or inflation of progress via inefficient costs**

**Mosofin additions:**

- The workspace was confirmed **by name** and the user said yes before any data was read
- Every in-scope company file is named by `display_name`; every excluded one is named **as excluded**
- The capability map was discovered **this run** via Gate 2 — never recalled from a previous
  conversation or from this file
- **The job-tagging dimension is stated**, and where costs are not tagged by job, the output says the
  books cannot support contract-level accounting
- **Costs incurred are marked per contract as read or supplied**; no company-wide cost is allocated
  across contracts
- **Every estimate at completion carries its date and its owner**, and is checked against incurred plus
  committed cost for staleness
- **Catch-up adjustments are shown separately** from the period's ordinary recognition
- **The loss-contract test was run on every contract this period**, and the result stated even where nil
- Billings are never presented as revenue; the aging is never presented as the WIP position
- Every contract carries its verdict (`[auto]` / `[gated]` / `[manual]`), the tool used, and that tool's
  policy, in the coverage sheet
- `mock` status is reported wherever it applies, and no recognised revenue or loss provision rests on
  mock data
- Every figure traces to a tool result in this conversation or to labelled user-supplied evidence; the
  answer ends with a single **Data sources** line
- Every jargon term carries a plain-language gloss at first mention, with the professional term kept
  alongside
- No internal numeric ids and no raw `data_source_id` values appear in anything the user sees
- **No contract values, margins or claim positions are persisted** into a skill bundle; every persisted
  preference states the datasource and `display_name` it covers
- Nothing was written back to any system — every entry and provision is a proposal for a human to post
